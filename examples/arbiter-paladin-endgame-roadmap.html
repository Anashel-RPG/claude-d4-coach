How this build works

Read first

The loop

Spin Blessed Hammer as your core, stand in Consecration, keep both auras up, and pop Arbiter of Justice on tough packs — that transform is your burst-and-survive window, and your amulet + rings all scale off being in it.

Damage in plain terms

+ additive bonuses (skill ranks, most paragon) pile into one sum. × multiplicative bonuses (Herald's, Weaken, Vulnerable, crit) each multiply the whole — an empty multiplier is your biggest single gain, which is why charms and the right aspects matter so much.

How to play it

Drop Consecration and stay in it. Spin Blessed Hammer. Use Falling Star to reposition and burst. Pop Arbiter of Justice on elites and bosses and keep its uptime through transitions. Rally for the crit/heal window.

Paragon & glyphs

Scaling

How boards work

Boards are panels you attach in sequence; each has one Legendary node (a build-defining bonus) you path toward, plus glyph sockets. A glyph leveled to 15 expands its radius to also activate the nearby Rare nodes — a big chunk of your scaling.

  • Board sequence — attach & path to each Legendary node
  • Attach & clear the Starting Board
  • Attach the Beacon board + path to its Legendary node & glyph socket
  • Attach the Endure board + path to its Legendary node
