# Barbarian

> Season: 13 · Patch: 2.4.1 · Last refreshed: 2026-05-23 · Source: https://maxroll.gg/d4/resources/barbarian-class-overview

## Core identity
Barbarian is a melee powerhouse that equips four weapons simultaneously and cycles through them via the Arsenal System. Gameplay centers on generating and spending Fury to deal tremendous damage up close while maintaining Berserking uptime for mobility and offense.

## Damage scaling buckets
- **Multiplicative sources:** Berserking (damage bonus while active); Weapon Expertise bonuses (e.g., Two-Handed Axe — Vulnerable damage); assigned weapon's raw DPS (per-skill, not averaged across all weapons)
- **Additive sources:** Skill rank bonuses (+10% of base value per rank, up to rank 5 plus item bonuses); rolled affixes from all four equipped weapons (always apply to damage regardless of which weapon is assigned to the skill); Intelligence (+40 All Resistances per 100 points, defensive contribution)
- **Critical / Vulnerable / Overpower hooks:** Two-Handed Axe Expertise (Vulnerable damage); Two-Handed Sword Expertise (Bleeds, enabling Vulnerable via Bleed synergies); Gushing Wounds (Bleed-based Overpower); Cut to the Bone (Bleed amplification)

## Key passives / class mechanic
| Key Passive | Buffs (state/skill) | Effect | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Walking Arsenal | All damage | Maintain bonus by using three different weapon types at least once every 15 seconds | Builds that naturally cycle 2H Sword, 2H Axe/Mace, and dual-wield |
| Gushing Wounds | Bleed / Overpower | Causes Bleeding enemies to Overpower under specific conditions | Bleed/Rupture archetypes with high Overpower investment |

> Only one Key Passive can be equipped at a time.

## Paragon priority
- Starting board: Path through core damage/life nodes; notable coordinates include X12-Y12 (used by multiple archetypes) and X13-Y12 / X13-Y14 clusters. Reach board connectors to unlock secondary boards.
- Glyph order to L15: Secondary boards used across archetypes include **Weapons Master**, **Blood Rage**, **Warbringer**, **Hemorrhage**, and **Carnage** — prioritize the board most aligned to your weapon type (Weapons Master for Arsenal builds, Blood Rage / Hemorrhage for Bleed builds, Carnage for Berserking crit builds).

> Exact glyph socket coordinates and level-up order beyond board names are not covered in source.

## Common mistakes
- Ignoring the Arsenal System assignment — a skill's raw DPS is determined **only** by the weapon assigned to it, not an average of all four equipped weapons.
- Picking a second Key Passive — only one is allowed; the second slot does nothing.
- Neglecting the Technique Slot — it is unlocked via a level 15 quest in the Dry Steppes and passively grants a Weapon Expertise bonus regardless of which weapon is in use. Common choices are Two-Handed Sword (Bleeds) or Two-Handed Axe (Vulnerable damage).
- Letting Fury drop to zero outside of combat — Fury depletes slowly when not fighting, which can slow opener rotations.
- Skipping Weapon Expertise ranks — expertise levels up **on hit**, not on kill, so actively using a weapon type is required; note that PvP hits do not grant Expertise XP.
- Playing Rend without committing to Bleed payoff — its DoT requires patience; pairing it with Rupture and Gushing Wounds is necessary to realize its damage ceiling.
- Choosing Whirlwind expecting strong single-target output — it excels at Fury efficiency and AoE but underperforms on bosses without supplemental single-target skills.
