# Barbarian

> Season: 13 · Patch: 2.4.1 · Last refreshed: 2026-05-19 · Source: https://maxroll.gg/d4/resources/barbarian-class-overview

## Core identity
Barbarian is a melee-only berserker who equips four weapons simultaneously and rotates between them to maximize damage through the Arsenal System. High tankiness and mobility while dealing damage makes it a dominant frontline class, at the cost of strict Fury management and close-range commitment.

## Damage scaling buckets
- **Multiplicative sources:** Berserking bonuses; Weapon Expertise passive bonuses (active weapon type); Technique Slot expertise passive (always active regardless of weapon in hand); Death Blow + Encroaching Wrath + Earthstriker combo (two-handed)
- **Additive sources:** Rolled affixes on all four equipped weapons always apply to damage; skill rank bonuses (+10% of base value per rank, up to rank 5 plus item ranks); passive skills (3 ranks each, linear scaling)
- **Critical / Vulnerable / Overpower hooks:** Two-Handed Axe Expertise (Technique Slot) → Vulnerable damage bonus; Two-Handed Sword Expertise (Technique Slot) → Bleed application; Gushing Wounds (passive) → Bleed/Overpower interaction; Steel Grasp → applies Vulnerable to enemies

## Common mistakes
- Ignoring Arsenal System weapon assignment — raw skill DPS comes only from the weapon assigned to that skill, not from all equipped weapons
- Skipping the Technique Slot quest (unlocked at level 15 in the Dry Steppes), losing a permanent Weapon Expertise bonus
- Choosing Two-Handed Axe Expertise for Technique Slot on Bleed builds — Two-Handed Sword Expertise is preferred for Bleed application, while Axe is better for Vulnerable-focused builds
- Leveling Weapon Expertise in PvP — expertise does not gain experience points in PvP
- Trying to maintain Walking Arsenal without reliably hitting with all three weapon types within 15 seconds
- Investing in more than one Key Passive or more than one Ultimate Skill — only one of each can be active
- Underestimating Fury drain outside combat — resource dries up quickly, starving the next encounter's opener
- Stacking into ranged or AoE fights — Barbarian requires close combat and has no meaningful group support tools
- Neglecting Intelligence itemization — it provides +40 All Resistances per 100 points, the Barbarian's primary resistance scaling stat
