# Barbarian

> Season: 13 · Patch: 2.4.1 · Last refreshed: 2026-05-22 · Source: https://maxroll.gg/d4/resources/barbarian-class-overview

## Core identity
Barbarian is a melee powerhouse that equips four weapons simultaneously, cycling between them via the Arsenal System to maximise damage through Berserking uptime and Fury management. Strengths are tremendous damage, mobility, and tankiness; weaknesses are Fury dependency, long cooldowns, mandatory close range, and a slow early game.

## Damage scaling buckets
- **Multiplicative sources:** Berserking (class state); Weapon Expertise bonuses (e.g. Two-Handed Axe Expertise — Vulnerable damage); Arsenal System weapon assignment (raw DPS of each skill determined solely by its assigned weapon)
- **Additive sources:** Rolled affixes on all four equipped weapons (always apply to damage regardless of assignment); skill rank bonuses (+10% of base value per rank, up to rank 5 + item bonuses); Intelligence (+40 All Resistances per 100 points — defensive, not damage)
- **Critical / Vulnerable / Overpower hooks:** Vulnerable (Two-Handed Axe Expertise via Technique Slot); Bleed / DoT (Two-Handed Sword Expertise via Technique Slot; Flay, Rend, Gushing Wounds, Cut to the Bone); Overpower (not covered in source)

**Arsenal System notes:**
- Raw DPS of a skill = weapon assigned to it only.
- Rolled affixes of all four equipped weapons always contribute to damage.
- Weapon Expertise levels up to rank 10, gained on hit (not on kill); no XP gained in PvP.
- Technique Slot (unlocked at level 15 via Dry Steppes quest): grants a Weapon Expertise bonus regardless of which weapon is actively swung. Top picks: Two-Handed Sword (Bleeds) or Two-Handed Axe (Vulnerable damage).
- Only one Key Passive and one Ultimate Skill can be active per build.

## Paragon priority
- Starting board: Path through core stat clusters — key nodes used in both Flay and Lunging Strike builds include X12-Y12 (shared), with routes branching toward strength/damage nodes on the starting board.
- Glyph order to L15: Boards used across documented builds — **Weapons Master**, **Blood Rage**, **Warbringer**, **Hemorrhage**, **Carnage** (Flay build uses Weapons Master + Blood Rage + Warbringer + Hemorrhage; Lunging Strike build uses Blood Rage + Carnage). Prioritise the glyph socket board most central to your core skill first.

## Common mistakes
- Assigning the wrong weapon to a skill — raw skill DPS is determined by its assigned weapon only, not total weapon power.
- Skipping the Technique Slot quest (available at level 15 in the Dry Steppes) — losing a free permanent Weapon Expertise bonus is a significant DPS loss.
- Slotting two Key Passives or two Ultimates — only one of each is permitted per build.
- Relying on PvP for Weapon Expertise levelling — expertise XP is disabled in PvP; farm on-hit in PvE instead.
- Dropping Fury to zero outside combat — Fury depletes slowly out of combat; re-engage before fully draining so cooldown-intensive openers are available.
- Building for AoE on Double Swing — it has best-in-class single-target and Fury efficiency but a very small AoE; swap to Whirlwind for clear-heavy content.
- Ignoring Bleed ramp time on Rend — its damage per hit is the highest of all core skills but is DoT-dependent; don't skip targets before bleed fully ticks.
