# Barbarian

> Season: 13 · Patch: 2.4.1 · Last refreshed: 2026-05-21 · Source: https://maxroll.gg/d4/resources/barbarian-class-overview

## Core identity
Barbarian is a melee powerhouse that equips four weapons simultaneously and cycles through them via the Arsenal System. It excels at close-range sustained damage, Berserking uptime, and high survivability — at the cost of Fury management and long cooldowns.

## Damage scaling buckets
- **Multiplicative sources:** Berserking damage bonus; Weapon Expertise bonuses (rank 1–10, gained on hit); Technique Slot expertise passive (applies regardless of active weapon)
- **Additive sources:** Skill rank bonuses (~+10% base value per rank, up to rank 5 + item ranks); rolled affixes on all four equipped weapons (always apply to damage); Intelligence (+40 All Resistances per 100 points, defensive scaling)
- **Critical / Vulnerable / Overpower hooks:** Two-Handed Axe Expertise (Technique Slot) → Vulnerable damage; Two-Handed Sword Expertise (Technique Slot) → Bleed application; Rend → Bleed DoT (highest damage-per-hit of core skills); Steel Grasp → applies Vulnerable; Death Blow + Encroaching Wrath + Earthstriker combo → highest boss damage in the game

## Paragon priority
- Starting board: Path through key nodes including X12-Y12 (used by multiple builds); prioritize nodes that feed into board connectors for Weapons Master, Blood Rage, Warbringer, and Hemorrhage boards
- Glyph order to L15: Weapons Master board and Blood Rage board are core for Flay/bleed builds; Carnage board is core for Lunging Strike builds; Warbringer board supports both Flay and Frenzy builds; Hemorrhage board is a Flay-build priority

## Common mistakes
- Neglecting Weapon Expertise leveling — expertise ranks are gained on hit (not on kill) and do not progress in PvP; falling behind on ranks is a significant DPS loss
- Assigning the wrong weapon to a skill in the Arsenal System — raw skill DPS is determined solely by the weapon assigned to that skill, not by your highest-damage weapon overall
- Skipping the Technique Slot quest — unlocked at level 15 in the Dry Steppes; missing it delays a free expertise bonus applicable to all weapon uses
- Choosing more than one Key Passive — only one can be active; spreading points toward a second is wasted
- Running Whirlwind into single-target content — it has poor single-target damage; swap to Double Swing or Death Blow for bosses
- Letting Fury drain between pulls — Fury depletes slowly outside combat; Basic Skills must be used to rebuild it quickly at the start of each fight
- Ignoring close-combat positioning — all strong Barbarian skills require melee range; kiting or playing reactively starves the build of Fury and Berserking uptime
