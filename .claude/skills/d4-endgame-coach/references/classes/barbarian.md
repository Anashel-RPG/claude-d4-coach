# Barbarian

> Season: 13 · Patch: 2.4.1 · Last refreshed: 2026-05-20 · Source: https://maxroll.gg/d4/resources/barbarian-class-overview

## Core identity
Barbarian is a melee juggernaut that wields four weapons simultaneously and cycles through them via the Arsenal System to amplify damage. The class excels at staying in enemies' faces, maintaining Berserking uptime, and converting raw Fury generation into massive burst or sustained DPS.

## Damage scaling buckets
- **Multiplicative sources:** Berserking (damage bonus while active), Weapon Expertise bonuses (e.g., Two-Handed Axe Expertise: Vulnerable damage), skill-specific multipliers from Key Passives (e.g., Walking Arsenal)
- **Additive sources:** Rolled affixes on all four equipped weapons (always apply to damage), skill rank bonuses (+10% of base value per rank, up to rank 5 plus item ranks), Intelligence (+40 All Resistances per 100 points — defensive, not offensive)
- **Critical / Vulnerable / Overpower hooks:** Two-Handed Axe Expertise (Technique Slot) grants Vulnerable damage; Two-Handed Sword Expertise (Technique Slot) applies Bleeds; Steel Grasp applies Vulnerable; Rend applies Bleed (DoT); Gushing Wounds (passive) interacts with Bleed/Overpower

## Key passives / class mechanic
| Key Passive | Buffs (state/skill) | Effect | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Walking Arsenal | All weapon-type skills | Requires using three different weapons at least once every 15 seconds to maintain its damage bonus | Builds that naturally cycle all four weapon slots |
| Gushing Wounds | Bleed skills (Flay, Rend, Rupture) | Interacts with Bleed and Overpower for bonus damage | Bleed-focused builds (e.g., Flay, Rend) |
| Endless Fury | Fury-spending skills | Fury generation / cost reduction synergy | High-spam Fury builds |

*Only one Key Passive can be chosen per build.*

## Common mistakes
- Ignoring Arsenal System weapon assignment — raw skill DPS is determined only by the weapon assigned to that skill, not all equipped weapons
- Letting Fury deplete between pulls; Fury drains slowly out of combat, so chain engagements to maintain resources
- Running a second Key Passive — only one can be active; wasted points elsewhere
- Running a second Ultimate Skill — same restriction applies
- Skipping the Technique Slot quest (level 15, Dry Steppes); missing a free Weapon Expertise passive significantly reduces damage or Bleed uptime
- Leveling Weapon Expertise in PvP — no experience is gained there; farm in PvE instead
- Choosing Two-Handed Sword or Axe Expertise for the Technique Slot without aligning it to the build's damage type (Sword = Bleed, Axe = Vulnerable — pick based on which the build stacks)
- Taking Walking Arsenal without reliably hitting all three weapon types every 15 seconds, causing the bonus to drop
- Playing Rend and not waiting for bleed ticks — the skill's damage-per-hit is highest among core skills but the payoff is in the DoT
