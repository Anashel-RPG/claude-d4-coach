# Barbarian

> Season: 13 · Patch: 2.4.1 · Last refreshed: 2026-05-25 · Source: https://maxroll.gg/d4/resources/barbarian-class-overview

## Core identity
Barbarian is a melee powerhouse that equips four weapons simultaneously and cycles through them via the Arsenal System to amplify damage. Fury-fueled and Berserking, it excels at tanky close-range combat with strong mobility but demands constant engagement to sustain resources.

## Damage scaling buckets
- **Multiplicative sources:** Berserking (active), Weapon Expertise bonuses (up to rank 10), assigned-weapon raw DPS via Arsenal System, Technique Slot expertise bonus (applied regardless of active weapon)
- **Additive sources:** Skill rank bonuses (~+10% base value per rank, up to rank 5 baseline), rolled affixes on all four equipped weapons (always apply to damage), Intelligence (+40 All Resistances per 100 pts — defensive scaling)
- **Critical / Vulnerable / Overpower hooks:** Two-Handed Axe Expertise (Vulnerable damage); Crit/Overpower interactions (not covered in source)

- Starting board: Path through core combat nodes; Flay build uses extensive pathing through rows Y2–Y15 spanning columns X9–X14, including the Magic/Rare cluster around X12–X13. Lunging Strike build keys X12-Y12.

## Common mistakes
- Assigning a weak weapon to a high-priority skill — raw DPS of a skill is determined **only** by the weapon assigned to it in the Arsenal System, not by all equipped weapons combined.
- Neglecting weapon affix rolls — rolled affixes on **all four** equipped weapons always contribute to damage, so low-roll off-hands still matter.
- Failing to maintain Walking Arsenal — the bonus drops if you don't use three different weapon types within every 15-second window.
- Choosing more than one Key Passive — only one Key Passive slot is available per build.
- Trying to level Weapon Expertise in PvP — expertise gains no experience in PvP zones; level it on hits vs. PvE enemies.
- Ignoring Technique Slot setup — the Technique Slot grants a Weapon Expertise bonus regardless of which weapon is active; Two-Handed Sword (Bleed) and Two-Handed Axe (Vulnerable damage) are the two most common picks.
- Letting Fury decay — Fury depletes outside of combat, so re-engaging quickly or using Fury-generation passives is essential.
- Expecting strong group support — Barbarian has limited utility for allies; build for self-sustain.
