# Barbarian

> Season: 13 · Patch: 2.4.1 · Last refreshed: 2026-05-24 · Source: https://maxroll.gg/d4/resources/barbarian-class-overview

## Core identity
Barbarian is a close-range powerhouse that equips four weapons simultaneously and cycles them through the Arsenal System to maximize damage. The class excels at high mobility, sustained Berserking uptime, and extreme tankiness — at the cost of Fury dependency and mandatory melee range.

## Damage scaling buckets
- **Multiplicative sources:** Berserking damage bonus; weapon-specific expertise bonuses (applied via Arsenal System or Technique Slot)
- **Additive sources:** Rolled affixes on all four equipped weapons (always apply to damage regardless of which weapon fires the skill); skill rank bonuses (+10% of base value per rank)
- **Critical / Vulnerable / Overpower hooks:** Two-Handed Axe Expertise (Vulnerable damage); Gushing Wounds (Overpower + bleed interaction); Crit hooks available via passives and paragon (specific numbers not covered in source)

> **Arsenal System rules:** Weapon Expertise levels to rank 10, gained on hit (not on kill). No Expertise XP in PvP. Raw skill DPS is determined solely by the weapon assigned to that skill. Rolled affixes from all four weapons always contribute to damage. The **Technique Slot** permanently grants the Expertise bonus of the assigned weapon type regardless of active weapon.

**Top Technique Slot picks:**
- Two-Handed Sword Expertise — applies Bleeds
- Two-Handed Axe Expertise — grants more Vulnerable damage

## Paragon priority
- Starting board: Path through core damage nodes; Flay builds heavily utilize the Starting Board cluster around X11–X14, Y2–Y15. Lunging Strike builds route through X12-Y12 as a key junction.
- Glyph order to L15: Flay builds use **Weapons Master**, **Blood Rage**, **Warbringer**, and **Hemorrhage** boards. Lunging Strike builds use **Blood Rage** and **Carnage** boards. Frenzy builds route into **Warbringer**. Specific glyph socket L15 priority order not covered in source.

## Common mistakes
- Assigning high-DPS skills to the wrong weapon — raw skill damage is set only by the weapon assigned in the Arsenal System, not your best weapon overall.
- Ignoring the Technique Slot — it gives you a free Expertise bonus; always assign it deliberately (Two-Handed Axe for Vulnerable, Two-Handed Sword for Bleeds are the top picks).
- Failing to maintain Walking Arsenal's 15-second weapon-rotation window, losing its multiplicative bonus entirely.
- Neglecting Fury generation — Fury depletes outside combat; running dry mid-fight cripples output.
- Skipping melee range — every Barbarian damage skill requires close contact; trying to play at range kills effectiveness.
- Leveling Expertise in PvP — no Expertise XP is earned there; don't expect to rank weapons through PvP.
- Taking two Key Passives — only one can be slotted; plan your build around a single Key Passive identity.
