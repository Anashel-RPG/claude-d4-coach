# Warlock

> Season: 13 · Patch: 2.4.1 · Last refreshed: 2026-05-17 · Source: https://maxroll.gg/d4/getting-started/warlock-class-overview

## Core identity
Warlock is a demon-summoning/transformation class introduced in the Lord of Hatred expansion (Season 13, Patch 2.4.1). It blends demonic minion command with personal shapeshifting, offering distinct playstyles through four Soul Shards: Legion, Vanguard, Mastermind, and Ritualist.

## Damage scaling buckets
- **Multiplicative sources:** Demonform stacks (+1% Demonology Skill damage per stack, up to 100%); Hellfire bonus while in Demonform (+30%); Mastermind Shard Abyss Skill bonus while in Shadowform (+30%)
- **Additive sources:** Intelligence (Resistances); Strength (Armor); Legion Shard Greater Demon cast — Lesser Demon cast rate +25%, cooldown rate +20% for 10 seconds
- **Critical / Vulnerable / Overpower hooks:** Profane Sentinel applies Vulnerable to enemies; Hellion Sting 5% chance to Eviscerate for 660% damage; Rampage 5% chance to Eviscerate for 2560% damage; Doom Hexes enemies (20% Lucky Hit Chance)

## Key passives / class mechanic
| Key Passive | Buffs (state/skill) | Effect | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Soul Shards (class mechanic) | All skills | Select one Shard + one Fragment per loadout; defines playstyle | All builds |
| Legion Shard | Lesser Demon skills | Casting a Greater Demon skill increases Lesser Demon cast rate by 25% and cooldown rate by 20% for 10s; grants Summon Ae'Grom skill | Minion swarm builds |
| Legion — Sacrificial Fragment | Warlock (self) | Warlock is Unstoppable while active Lesser Demons exist; when CC'd, one Lesser Demon is sacrificed instead | Legion survivability |
| Vanguard Shard | Metamorphosis / Archfiend skills | Grants Summon Abodian; while in Demonform casting Archfiend Skills also summons the Archfiend Demon | Transformation/melee builds |

| Mastermind Shard | Abyss Skills / Shadowform | Abyss Skills deal 30% increased damage in Shadowform; +5% movement speed per Shadowform stack; Recast Skills don't break Stealth (costs 2 Shadowform stacks per cast) | Stealth / Abyss builds |

## Common mistakes
- Selecting Dexterity-heavy gear — Dexterity is the least valuable core stat for Warlock, providing only minor Critical Strike Chance and Dodge Chance bonuses
- Ignoring Intelligence itemization — Intelligence provides Resistances, a key defensive scaling stat
- Equipping two Ultimates — Warlock has only one Ultimate slot; choose Metamorphosis or Fiend of Abbadon, not both
- Picking a Soul Shard without committing to its Fragment synergy — e.g., running Ritualist Shard without managing the 4% Maximum Life cost of Scorching Fragment will drain health rapidly
- Dropping Demonform stacks — the Demonology damage bonus scales up to 100% at max stacks; losing the state resets the multiplier
- Not maintaining active Lesser Demons under Legion Shard — the Unstoppable protection and cast-rate bonuses both require living Lesser Demons on the field
