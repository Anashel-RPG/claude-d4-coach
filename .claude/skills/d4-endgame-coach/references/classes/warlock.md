# Warlock

> Season: 13 · Patch: 2.4.1 · Last refreshed: 2026-05-18 · Source: https://maxroll.gg/d4/getting-started/warlock-class-overview

## Core identity
Warlock (introduced Season 13 / Patch 2.4.1, Lord of Hatred expansion) commands demons and dark forces through its unique Soul Shard mechanic, choosing between summoner, shapeshifter, stealth, and ritualist playstyles. Strengths include great mobility, stunning skill effects, and deep build variety.

## Damage scaling buckets
- **Multiplicative sources:** Demonform stacks (up to +100% Demonology Skill damage at 100 stacks); Demonform Hellfire bonus (+30% Demonology Skill damage while active); Mastermind Shard Shadowform (+30% Abyss Skill damage while in Shadowform)
- **Additive sources:** Intelligence (Resistances); Strength (Armor); Dexterity (minor Critical Strike Chance, minor Dodge Chance — lowest priority stat)
- **Critical / Vulnerable / Overpower hooks:** Profane Sentinel applies Vulnerable to enemies (enabling Vulnerable damage multiplier); Doom applies Hex; Rampage has 5% chance to Eviscerate for 2560% damage; Lucky Hit Chance varies by skill (Doom 20%, Tyrant's Grasp 15%, Profane Sentinel 22%, Rampage 30%, Fiend of Abbadon 22%, Terror Swarm 1%)

## Common mistakes
- Selecting Dexterity as a primary stat — it provides only minor Critical Strike Chance and Dodge Chance; prioritize Intelligence (Resistances) and Strength (Armor) first.
- Equipping only one Ultimate when the wrong choice is locked in — each Warlock can slot only one Ultimate at a time; choose based on build (Metamorphosis for Demonform, Fiend of Abbadon for summon burst, Terror Swarm for sustained AoE).
- Ignoring Soul Shard Fragment synergy — picking a Shard without matching its Fragment to the build (e.g., taking Warden Fragment without maintaining Close Greater Demons in Demonform) wastes the Shard's secondary benefit.
- Not maintaining Lesser Demons when running Legion Shard — losing all active Lesser Demons drops the Unstoppable buff, leaving the Warlock vulnerable to crowd control.
- Overlooking Profane Sentinel's Vulnerable uptime — it is a primary Vulnerable application; missing its recast window drops a key damage multiplier off enemies.
