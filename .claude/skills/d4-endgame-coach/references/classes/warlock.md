# Warlock

> Season: 13 · Patch: 2.4.1 · Last refreshed: 2026-05-16 · Source: https://maxroll.gg/d4/getting-started/warlock-class-overview

## Core identity
The Warlock (introduced in the *Lord of Hatred* expansion, Season 13, Patch 2.4.1) is a demon-wielding caster that picks one of four Soul Shards to define its playstyle: summoning hordes, transforming into a demon, hiding in shadow, or detonating elemental hellfire. All builds revolve around the Soul Shard mechanic — one Shard + one Fragment chosen per character.

## Damage scaling buckets
- **Multiplicative sources:** Demonform (+1% Demonology Skill damage per stack, up to 100%); Demonform Hellfire bonus (+30% to Hellfire Demonology Skills while active); Mastermind Shard Abyss Skills bonus (+30% while in Shadowform); Volatility empowered cast (+50% damage on next Hellfire Skill cast); Ritualist Shard Scorching Fragment (Hellfire Skills permanently empowered, costs 4% Max Life per use)
- **Additive sources:** Intelligence (grants Resistances); Strength (grants Armor); Demonform Maximum Life bonus (+25% while active); Legion Shard Greater Demon cast synergy (+25% Lesser Demon cast rate, +20% cooldown rate for 10 seconds)
- **Critical / Vulnerable / Overpower hooks:** Profane Sentinel applies Vulnerable to enemies; Hellion Sting has a 5% Eviscerate proc (660% damage); Rampage has a 5% Eviscerate proc (2560% damage); Apocalypse has a 50% Lucky Hit Chance

## Common mistakes
- Picking Dexterity-heavy gear over Intelligence and Strength — Dex provides only minor Critical Strike Chance and Dodge Chance for the Warlock, making it the least valuable core stat.
- Running Apocalypse and investing in Cooldown Reduction — Apocalypse explicitly does **not** benefit from Cooldown Reduction (66.6 s CD is fixed).
- Forgetting the one-Shard / one-Fragment limit — only one Soul Shard and one Fragment may be active; build your entire skill selection around the chosen Shard's synergies before looking elsewhere.
- Letting Lesser Demons expire during crowd-control when using the Legion Shard's Sacrificial Fragment — being CC'd sacrifices one demon to grant Unstoppable, which can deplete your swarm unexpectedly if you aren't building Unstoppable uptime elsewhere.
- Ignoring the 4% Max Life cost of Ritualist Shard's Scorching Fragment — empowering every Hellfire Skill cast requires a meaningful Life pool or the self-damage will threaten survivability at high Pit tiers.
