# Sorcerer

> Season: 13 · Patch: 2.4.1 · Last refreshed: 2026-05-22 · Source: https://maxroll.gg/d4/resources/sorcerer-class-overview

## Core identity
Sorcerer is a ranged elemental caster dealing Cold, Fire, and Lightning damage with screen-wide AoE, strong crowd control, and high mobility via Teleport. The class is Mana-dependent and fragile at close range, trading survivability for unmatched damage output on the move.

## Damage scaling buckets
- **Multiplicative sources:** Enchantment slot passive effects; Key Passive bonuses (e.g., Combustion, Esu's Ferocity, Vyr's Mastery); elemental damage multipliers from Aspects
- **Additive sources:** Intelligence (+12.5% Skill Damage per 100 points); Dexterity (+2% Critical Strike Chance per 100 points); Willpower (+25% Overpower Damage per 100 points); Strength (+200 Armor per 100 points, defensive only)
- **Critical / Vulnerable / Overpower hooks:** Dexterity provides +2% Critical Strike Chance per 100 points; Willpower provides +25% Overpower Damage per 100 points; Aspect of Burning affects Overpower (Sorcerer + Barbarian); Meteor-Overpower and Hydra-Overpower skill upgrade nodes exist

**Class mechanic — Enchantment Slots:**
- Slot 1 unlocks at level 15 (triggers Priority Quest "Legacy of the Magi")
- Slot 2 unlocks at level 30
- Any Sorcerer skill placed in a slot grants a passive bonus effect
- A skill can be on the action bar and in an Enchantment Slot simultaneously
- Slots can be swapped at any time at no cost

## Common mistakes
- Equipping a Staff instead of 1H + Focus — costs one Legendary Aspect slot and the Focus's Cooldown Reduction
- Neglecting Intelligence: each 100 points is +12.5% Skill Damage and +40 All Resistances
- Ignoring Enchantment Slots early — Slot 1 is available at level 15 and is a free passive bonus
- Slotting only one element when the build benefits from keeping a skill both on the action bar and in an Enchantment Slot simultaneously
- Running into melee range — Sorcerer's close-combat survivability is a listed class weakness
- Over-investing in Attack Speed without respecting Cap 1 (gear + paragon + elixir) on Enlightenment builds
- Choosing a Key Passive that doesn't match the primary damage element (only one Key Passive can be active at a time)
