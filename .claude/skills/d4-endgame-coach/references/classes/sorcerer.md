# Sorcerer

> Season: 13 · Patch: 2.4.1 · Last refreshed: 2026-05-24 · Source: https://maxroll.gg/d4/resources/sorcerer-class-overview

## Core identity
Sorcerer is a ranged elemental caster dealing Cold, Fire, and Lightning damage with screen-wide AoE, strong crowd control, and high mobility via Teleport. The class is Mana-dependent and fragile in melee, trading survivability for burst and sustained damage on the move.

## Damage scaling buckets
- **Multiplicative sources:** Skill-specific damage multipliers; Enchantment passive procs; Key Passive bonuses (e.g., Avalanche, Esu's Ferocity, Combustion)
- **Additive sources:** Intelligence — 12.5% Skill Damage per 100 pts; Dexterity — 2% Critical Strike Chance per 100 pts; Willpower — 25% Overpower Damage per 100 pts
- **Critical / Vulnerable / Overpower hooks:** Dexterity 2% Crit Chance per 100 pts; Willpower 25% Overpower Damage per 100 pts; Meteor (Overpower enhancement); Hydra (Overpower enhancement); Aspect of Burning (Overpower, shared with Barbarian)

## Common mistakes
- Equipping a two-handed Staff without accounting for the lost Legendary Aspect slot and Focus Cooldown Reduction.
- Skipping Intelligence investment — each 100 points provides 12.5% Skill Damage and +40 All Resistances.
- Filling both Enchantment Slots before level 30 is impossible; plan first Enchantment at level 15, second at level 30.
- Choosing more than one Key Passive — only one can be active at a time.
- Choosing more than one Ultimate Skill — only one can be active at a time.
- Going into melee range without defensive skills active (Ice Armor, Teleport); Sorcerer has low close-combat survivability.
- Ignoring Mana management — all core damage skills consume Mana, and the class has limited party utility to compensate for downtime.
- Over-investing in Attack Speed when running Enlightenment without checking Cap 1 (gear + paragon + elixir cap applies).
