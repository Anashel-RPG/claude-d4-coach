# Sorcerer

> Season: 13 · Patch: 2.4.1 · Last refreshed: 2026-05-23 · Source: https://maxroll.gg/d4/resources/sorcerer-class-overview

## Core identity
Ranged elemental caster dealing Cold, Fire, and Lightning damage. Excels at screen-wide AoE, crowd control, and DPS on the move; struggles in melee and requires careful Mana management.

## Damage scaling buckets
- **Multiplicative sources:** Intelligence (12.5% Skill Damage per 100 pts); elemental damage affixes (Cold/Fire/Lightning %); Enchantment passive procs
- **Additive sources:** Willpower (25% Overpower Damage per 100 pts); Dexterity (2% Critical Strike Chance per 100 pts); Strength (+200 Armor per 100 pts); flat skill damage rolls on gear
- **Critical / Vulnerable / Overpower hooks:** Dexterity feeds Crit Chance; Willpower feeds Overpower Damage; Blizzard applies 9% Chill per tick (×2/sec) enabling Freeze → Shatter chain; Meteor-Overpower and Hydra-Overpower skill upgrades tie into Overpower bucket

## Key passives / class mechanic
**Class Mechanic — Enchantment Slots:** Slot 1 unlocks at level 15 (triggers Priority Quest "Legacy of the Magi"); Slot 2 unlocks at level 30. Skills placed in slots provide passive effects and can be swapped freely at any time. A skill can appear on the action bar and in an Enchantment Slot simultaneously. Only one Ultimate Skill and one Key Passive may be chosen.

## Common mistakes
- Equipping a two-handed Staff and losing a full Legendary Aspect slot plus Focus Cooldown Reduction — run 1H+Focus unless the Staff implicit is specifically required.
- Picking two Key Passives or two Ultimate Skills — only one of each is allowed.
- Ignoring Enchantment Slots at level 15/30; these are free passive power and should be filled immediately.
- Neglecting Intelligence stacking — it provides both 12.5% Skill Damage and +40 All Resistances per 100 points, making it the primary stat on nearly every stat stick.
- Letting the Enlightenment Attack Speed bonus exceed Cap 1 without checking gear/paragon/elixir contributions — points beyond cap are wasted.
- Diving into melee without a defensive skill active; Sorcerer is fragile at close range and Mana-dependent, making sustained melee engagement a primary cause of death.
