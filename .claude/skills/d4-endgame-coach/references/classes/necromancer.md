# Necromancer

> Season: 13 · Patch: 2.4.1 · Last refreshed: 2026-05-23 · Source: https://maxroll.gg/d4/resources/necromancer-class-overview

## Core identity
Necromancer bends Shadow, Blood, Bone, Corpses, and Curses to deal versatile damage across multiple distinct playstyles. The class leverages its unique Book of the Dead mechanic to either field an undead army or sacrifice minions for potent personal bonuses.

## Damage scaling buckets
- **Multiplicative sources:** Key Passive bonus (one active at a time); Book of the Dead sacrifice bonuses (Skeletal Mage sacrifice → damage/resource; Golem sacrifice → DPS/tankiness); skill upgrade specialization choices
- **Additive sources:** Intelligence (+12.5% Skill Damage per 100 pts); each additional skill rank (+10% of base value per rank); Corpse and Macabre passive cluster ranks (12 ranks per skill type: Bone, Blood, Darkness)

## Common mistakes
- Running into endgame without corpses available — Corpse Explosion and Blood Mist both require corpse generation; ensure a consistent source is in every build.
- Picking multiple key passives — only **one** Key Passive slot exists; choose the one matching your primary damage type.
- Neglecting Book of the Dead decisions — leaving minions alive when a sacrifice bonus would better serve a solo-damage build is a significant DPS loss.
- Ignoring Intelligence scaling — each 100 INT = +12.5% Skill Damage and +40 All Elemental Resistances; stack it on all armor slots.
- Skipping the class quest — Golem is locked behind "Necromancer: Call of the Underworld" (unlocks at level 25, requires 12 Unbroken Bones from undead near the Shrine of Rathma); missing it means losing a full Book of the Dead entry.
- Bloating the skill bar with too many active skills — the class suffers from Ability Bloat by design; trim to the minimum rotation and lean on passives for filler effects.
- Expecting immediate tankiness — the Necromancer is initially squishy; Blood Mist is the primary panic button and should be taken as soon as available.
