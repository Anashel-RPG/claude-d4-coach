# Paladin

> Season: 13 · Patch: 2.4.1 · Last refreshed: 2026-05-19 · Source: https://maxroll.gg/d4/resources/paladin-class-overview

## Core identity
A melee holy warrior with four Oath specializations (Zealot, Juggernaut, Judicator, Disciple). Excels at speed, tankiness, and support while managing cooldown-gated resource loops — Fervor, Resolve, Judgement, and Arbiter form.

## Damage scaling buckets
- **Multiplicative sources:** Resolve stacks (Juggernaut damage buff on spend), Arbiter form bonuses (Disciple), Judgement explosion chain reactions (Judicator), Retribution Thorns explosions
- **Additive sources:** Strength (+12.5% Skill Damage per 100 pts), active skill ranks (+10% base value per rank)

## Common mistakes
- Running more than one Key Passive or more than one Ultimate — only one of each is allowed
- Ignoring Resolve generation on Juggernaut builds — spending Resolve triggers the damage buff and extra defense
- Not investing in Strength: each 100 pts gives +12.5% Skill Damage and +20 Armor
- Leaving active skills at rank 1 — each additional rank adds +10% base value or –5% cooldown per rank
- Skipping the enhancement + upgrade step after unlocking a skill (one enhancement point + one of three upgrade choices available per skill)
- Playing Disciple without enabling Arbiter form — Falling Star and the Disciple Oath's improved Evade only activate in Arbiter form
- Undervaluing Willpower: it provides +3.5% Healing Received per 100 pts, relevant for sustain
- Treating the Paladin as a flex/ranged class — it is primarily melee and lacks flexibility; build around cooldown uptime accordingly
