# Paladin

> Season: 13 · Patch: 2.4.1 · Last refreshed: 2026-05-22 · Source: https://maxroll.gg/d4/resources/paladin-class-overview

## Core identity
A melee holy warrior with four Oath specializations (Zealot, Juggernaut, Judicator, Disciple), each tied to a distinct keyword and playstyle. The Paladin excels at high-speed clearing, extreme tankiness, and optional team support — at the cost of cooldown dependency and limited ranged flexibility.

## Damage scaling buckets
- **Multiplicative sources:** Resolve stacks (Juggernaut damage buff when spent); Arbiter form enhanced abilities (Disciple); Judgement chain explosions (Judicator); Strength — 12.5% Skill Damage per 100 points
- **Additive sources:** Skill rank bonuses — +10% of base value per rank (up to 5 ranks per active skill); passive ranks (3 ranks each, linear scaling); Armor/Block Chance via Coat of Arms key passive; Thorns/Retribution explosions

## Paragon priority
- Starting board: Core path through Starting Board nodes used by all top builds (Arbiter-Paladin, Arbiter's Zeal, Charge Auradin, Blessed Shield all invest heavily here). Cluster around X9–X13 / Y2–Y14 range.
- Glyph order to L15: Top builds (Arbiter-Paladin) extend into the Castle board and Relentless board after completing Starting Board pathing. Arbiter-Paladin specifically paths through Castle (X6–X20 range) and Relentless boards. Precise glyph names not covered in source.

## Common mistakes
- Picking more than one Key Passive or Ultimate Skill — only one of each is allowed.
- Neglecting Strength — it provides both 12.5% Skill Damage and 20 Armor per 100 points, making it the primary offensive and defensive stat.
- Ignoring cooldown reduction from skill ranks — each additional skill rank reduces cooldown by 5%; maxing active skills to rank 5 meaningfully reduces downtime.
- Not selecting an enhancement and upgrade after unlocking a skill — spending the extra point for enhancement + one of three upgrade options is mandatory, not optional.
- Playing a Disciple build without enabling Arbiter form — Arbiter is the payoff for the Disciple Oath (enhanced Evade, Exaltation buffs, Falling Star synergy).
- Stacking only one Oath's keyword — e.g., ignoring Resolve on a Juggernaut build loses the primary damage and DR buff.
- Treating flails as two-handed — flails are one-handed only; two-handed weapon options are limited to Two-Handed Axe and Two-Handed Sword.
