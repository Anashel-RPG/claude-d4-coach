# Paladin

> Season: 13 · Patch: 2.4.1 · Last refreshed: 2026-05-25 · Source: https://maxroll.gg/d4/resources/paladin-class-overview

## Core identity
Melee-focused holy warrior built around four Oath specializations (Zealot, Juggernaut, Judicator, Disciple). Excels at speed, tankiness, and support while spending class-specific resources (Fervor, Resolve) and transforming states (Arbiter) to amplify damage and defense.

## Damage scaling buckets
- **Multiplicative sources:** Resolve stacks (Juggernaut damage buff when spent), Arbiter form (Disciple enhanced abilities), Strength — 12.5% Skill Damage per 100 points
- **Additive sources:** Skill ranks — +10% of base value per rank (up to rank 5); passive ranks — 3 ranks, linear scaling; Intelligence — 2% All Resistances per 100 points (mitigation layer); Willpower — 3.5% Healing Received per 100 points
- **Critical / Vulnerable / Overpower hooks:** Judgement (mark enemies → detonation); Retribution (Thorns explosions triggered by skills/passives); Coat of Arms key passive (scales damage from Armor and Block Chance)

| Key Passive | Buffs (state/skill) | Effect | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Judgement Day | Judgement (state) | Creates Judgement chain reactions between marked targets | Judicator / AoE nuke builds |

| Coat of Arms | All damage | Scales damage based on Armor and Block Chance | Juggernaut / tanky builds |

*Only one Key Passive can be active at a time. Only one Ultimate Skill can be chosen.*

## Paragon priority
- Starting board: Path toward the cluster at X11–X13 / Y12–Y14 (shared by all top builds), then extend toward X12/Y5–Y7 range for damage nodes; pick up X9–X10/Y12–Y13 defensive nodes early.
- Glyph order to L15: Arbiter-Paladin and Arbiters-Zeal both invest heavily in the Starting Board before branching into the Castle board (X12–X20 / Y6–Y15 range) then Relentless board. Prioritize glyphs that land on your highest-density node clusters. Mrronits Charge Auradin stays mostly on Starting Board + Castle X16–X20.

## Common mistakes
- Ignoring Resolve generation — failing to stack and spend Resolve wastes the Juggernaut damage buff and defensive reduction.
- Running a second Key Passive or second Ultimate — only one of each is permitted; extra points are wasted.
- Neglecting Armor and Block Chance when using Coat of Arms — the key passive is directly tied to those stats for damage scaling.
- Over-investing in ranged or flexible options — the Paladin is primarily melee and lacks flexibility by design; trying to play it at range underperforms.
- Skipping skill enhancements and upgrades — after unlocking a skill, spending one point for the enhancement and choosing an upgrade path is a significant power increase that new players miss.
- Ignoring skill rank caps — active skills rank up to 5 (+10% base value per rank or –5% cooldown per rank); passive skills cap at 3 ranks; points spent beyond those caps are wasted.
- Not triggering Arbiter via Falling Star for Disciple builds — missing the transformation window severely limits Disciple damage and the Disciple Oath's improved Evade.
