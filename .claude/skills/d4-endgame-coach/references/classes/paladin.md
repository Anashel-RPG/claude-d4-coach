# Paladin

> Season: 13 · Patch: 2.4.1 · Last refreshed: 2026-05-21 · Source: https://maxroll.gg/d4/resources/paladin-class-overview

## Core identity
A heavily-armored melee class that specializes in one of four Oath paths—Zealot, Juggernaut, Judicator, or Disciple—generating and spending oath-specific resources (Fervor, Resolve, Judgement marks, Arbiter form) to output massive burst damage while remaining nearly unkillable. Strengths: very fast, super tanky, support-capable. Weaknesses: primarily melee, cooldown-reliant, low flexibility.

## Damage scaling buckets
- **Multiplicative sources:** Resolve stacks consumed for damage buff (Juggernaut mechanic); Arbiter form empowerment (Disciple mechanic); Judgement chain explosions (Judicator mechanic)
- **Additive sources:** Strength (+12.5% Skill Damage per 100 pts); active skill ranks (+10% of base value per rank); passive ranks (3 ranks, linear scaling)

## Common mistakes
- Equipping more than one Key Passive or more than one Ultimate Skill (only one of each is allowed per build).
- Neglecting skill ranks — each rank is a free +10% base damage or −5% cooldown; leaving skills at rank 1 is a large loss.
- Ignoring Strength itemization — it provides both 12.5% Skill Damage and 20 Armor per 100 points, making it the dominant primary stat.
- Not choosing the enhancement point after unlocking a skill (costs 1 additional point and is mandatory before accessing the three upgrade options).
- Over-investing in Willpower — it only provides 3.5% Healing Received per 100 points; far weaker than Strength for damage or tanking.
- Playing a ranged playstyle — the Paladin is exclusively melee and kits must account for that range constraint.
- Mismanaging cooldowns — the class relies heavily on ability uptime; spending Resolve or entering Arbiter/Fortress on cooldown without cooldown reduction itemization will stall damage windows.
