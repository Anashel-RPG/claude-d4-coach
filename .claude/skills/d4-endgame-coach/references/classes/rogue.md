# Rogue

> Season: 13 · Patch: 2.4.1 · Last refreshed: 2026-05-24 · Source: https://maxroll.gg/d4/resources/rogue-class-overview

## Core identity
High-mobility assassin that weaves melee and ranged attacks automatically based on skill used. Excels at burst damage through Imbuements, Combo Points, and conditional debuffs (Vulnerable, Poison, Shadow), but demands precise positioning and resource management.

## Damage scaling buckets
- **Multiplicative sources:** Imbuement damage multipliers; Specialization bonuses (Combo Points, Inner Sight, Preparation); Key Passive activations (Victimize AoE proc, Precision, Momentum, etc.)
- **Additive sources:** Dexterity (11.1% Skill Damage per 100 points); Intelligence (2% Critical Strike Chance per 100 points; +40 All Resistances per 100 points); skill rank bonuses
- **Critical / Vulnerable / Overpower hooks:** Vulnerable — required trigger for Victimize Key Passive (AoE damage on Vulnerable targets, analogous to D3 Area Damage); Invigorating Strike applies Vulnerable for 3 seconds when hitting enemies below 50% Energy; Critical Strike Chance scaled by Intelligence; Blade-Shift-Overpower skill variant available

## Key passives / class mechanic
| Key Passive | Buffs (state/skill) | Effect | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Momentum | Movement / melee skills | Rewards consecutive melee hits | Fast-moving melee builds |
| Close Quarters Combat | Melee range skills | Bonus damage when fighting at close range | Melee / Flurry builds |
| Precision | Critical Strike | Enhanced crit bonuses | Crit-focused builds |
| Victimize | Vulnerable targets | Deals AoE damage when hitting Vulnerable enemies (functions like D3 Area Damage) | Pack-clear; Vulnerable-uptime builds |
| Exposure | Trap / Imbuement skills | Enhances trap and Imbuement interactions | Trap / mixed-Imbuement builds |
| Alchemical Admixture | Imbuement skills | Boosts Imbuement effects | Heavy Imbuement builds |

*Only one Key Passive can be active at a time.*

**Class mechanics:**
- **Specializations** (one active at a time; swappable mid-combat): Combo Points, Inner Sight (unlocked earlier), Preparation (unlocks at level 30)
  - Inner Sight: fills gauge by hitting marked enemies; larger hits fill faster → grants unlimited Energy burst window
  - Combo Points: Basic Skills build points spent by Core Skills for bonus effects
  - Preparation: resets Cooldowns on Ultimate use
- **Weapon system:** equips two one-handed melee weapons (Swords/Daggers) + one ranged weapon (Bow/Crossbow); swaps automatically by skill used — no manual selection required
- **Imbueable skills:** all Core Skills, Dash, Shadow Step, Rain of Arrows
- **Multi-hit Imbuement rule:** Flurry, Barrage, and Rapid Fire apply Imbuement on every hit without consuming extra charges

- Glyph order to L15: Boards referenced across top builds — **Eldritch Bounty**, **Cheap Shot**, **Tricks of the Trade** (Heartseeker); **Cunning Stratagem** (Dance of Knives); **No Witnesses**, **Danse Macabre** (Death Trap); **Eldritch Bounty**, **Cheap Shot** (Rain of Arrows). Prioritize the board whose glyph directly multiplies your primary damage type first, then route to rare nodes before socketing.

## Common mistakes
- Not maintaining Vulnerable uptime before spending Core Skills — Victimize deals no bonus damage without it
- Ignoring positioning; Rogue is squishy and many damage bonuses are conditional on melee range or flanking
- Forgetting that the Specialization can be swapped mid-combat — use this to adapt to encounter phases
- Using a single-hit skill to deliver an Imbuement when a multi-hit skill (Flurry, Barrage, Rapid Fire) would apply it on every hit for free
- Skipping the Enhance + Upgrade steps on key skills — each unlock grants meaningful passive effects
- Over-investing in a second Key Passive candidate; only one is ever active
