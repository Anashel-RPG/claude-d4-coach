# Rogue

> Season: 13 · Patch: 2.4.1 · Last refreshed: 2026-05-23 · Source: https://maxroll.gg/d4/resources/rogue-class-overview

## Core identity
Fast, mobile assassin that weaves melee and ranged attacks automatically based on skill used. Excels at burst damage through Imbuements and conditional multipliers (Vulnerable, Crit, Overpower), but demands careful positioning and resource management.

## Damage scaling buckets
- **Multiplicative sources:** Imbuement damage bonuses; Vulnerable debuff multiplier (required for Victimize); Key Passive multipliers; Specialization-specific multipliers (e.g., Preparation cooldown resets enabling burst loops)
- **Additive sources:** Dexterity → +11.1% Skill Damage per 100 points; Intelligence → +2% Critical Strike Chance per 100 points (+40 All Resistances per 100 points); flat weapon damage rolls; skill rank bonuses
- **Critical / Vulnerable / Overpower hooks:** Critical Strike Chance sourced from Intelligence; Vulnerable applied by Invigorating Strike (hits enemy below 50% Energy) and other skills; Victimize key passive requires Vulnerable on target to proc AoE burst; Overpower available via Blade-Shift-Overpower upgrade

> Only **one** Key Passive can be equipped at a time.

**Class Mechanics:**
- **Specializations** (swap freely at any time, even mid-combat): Combo Points, Inner Sight, Preparation (unlocks at level 30)
  - *Inner Sight*: fills gauge by hitting marked enemies; larger hits fill gauge faster
- **Weapon system**: equips two one-handed melee weapons (Swords/Daggers) + one ranged weapon (Bow/Crossbow); swaps automatically by skill used — no manual selection required
- **Imbueable skills**: all Core Skills, Dash, Shadow Step, Rain of Arrows; multi-hit skills (Flurry, Barrage, Rapid Fire) apply Imbuement on every hit without consuming extra charges
- **Skill upgrade path**: unlock → 1 point to Enhance → choose one of two Specialization upgrades
- **Primary damage types**: Physical (default), Cold, Poison, Shadow

## Common mistakes
- Not maintaining Vulnerable uptime before expecting Victimize to deal meaningful damage — Victimize does nothing without the debuff on target
- Ignoring positioning requirements; standing still against density negates the class's primary defensive mechanic (Dodge: +0.6% per 100 Dexterity)
- Wasting Imbuement charges on single targets when multi-hit skills (Flurry, Barrage, Rapid Fire) would apply the Imbuement on every hit for free
- Manually trying to weapon-swap (no system for it — the Rogue auto-swaps based on skill used, unlike Barbarian)
- Picking a second Key Passive — only one can be active; spending skill points toward a second is wasted
- Not swapping Specialization to match the build; Specialization can be changed freely even mid-combat, so there is no reason to run a mismatched one
- Skipping the Enhance + upgrade steps on key skills and leaving power on the table (e.g., Invigorating Strike's Vulnerable proc is gated behind the enhanced/upgraded version)
