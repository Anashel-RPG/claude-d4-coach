# Rogue

> Season: 13 · Patch: 2.4.1 · Last refreshed: 2026-05-16 · Source: https://maxroll.gg/d4/resources/rogue-class-overview

## Core identity
Fast, mobile damage dealer that weaves melee and ranged attacks seamlessly. Excels at burst through Vulnerable, Critical Strike, and Imbuement stacking, but demands precise positioning and resource discipline.

## Damage scaling buckets
- **Multiplicative sources:** Dexterity (11.1% Skill Damage per 100 pts — main multiplier); Imbuement damage bonuses; Specialization-triggered free-cast windows (Inner Sight Unlimited Energy)
- **Additive sources:** Intelligence (2% Critical Strike Chance per 100 pts); Willpower (25% Overpower Damage per 100 pts); Strength (3% Resource Generation per 100 pts); skill rank bonuses
- **Critical / Vulnerable / Overpower hooks:** Vulnerable applied by Invigorating Strike (enemies below 50% Energy), Poison Imbue, Shadow Imbue; Victimize Key Passive chains AoE off Vulnerable; Precision Key Passive converts charged skill hits to guaranteed Crits with massively increased damage; Overpower scaled by Willpower (low priority for most builds)

## Key passives / class mechanic
| Key Passive | Buffs (state/skill) | Effect | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Victimize | Vulnerable state | Hitting Vulnerable enemies triggers bonus AoE damage, similar to D3 Area Damage | AoE clear, Vulnerable-focused builds |
| Precision | Charged-skill mechanic | Accumulates charges; consuming charges makes the hit a guaranteed Critical Strike with massively increased damage | Single-target / bossing, Barrage/Flurry crit builds |
| Close Quarters Combat | Cutthroat + Marksman skills | Grants attack speed steroid and a damage buff scaling with Damage vs. Close Enemies; requires ≥1 Cutthroat skill and ≥1 Marksman skill equipped | Hybrid melee/ranged builds |
| Exposure | Poison Trap, Caltrops, Death Trap | Massively reduces active cooldowns of all three trap skills on use | Trap-heavy / Poison builds |
| *(one Key Passive per build)* | — | Only one may be active at a time | — |

## Common mistakes
- Ignoring Dexterity on gear — it is the primary damage multiplier (11.1% Skill Damage per 100 pts) and should be the top stat priority.
- Stacking Willpower for Overpower — Overpower is a low-value scaling vector for most Rogue builds; treat Willpower as near-dump.
- Picking Shadow Clone as your Ultimate — it scales poorly with endgame damage modifiers; use Rain of Arrows or another Ultimate instead.
- Failing to maintain Vulnerable uptime before activating Victimize — the entire AoE chain collapses without the debuff.
- Using Close Quarters Combat without both a Cutthroat and a Marksman skill in the build — the passive's requirements are hard-gated.
- Neglecting Intelligence — even small investments grant 2% Crit Chance per 100 pts and +40 All Resistances per 100 pts, patching the class's squishiness.
- Misusing Shadow Step — it can traverse impassable terrain; players who treat it as a simple dash miss significant positional advantages.
- Forgetting that all three weapon slots (two melee + one ranged) contribute their stats regardless of which weapon type the active skill uses — never leave a weapon slot with weak affixes.
