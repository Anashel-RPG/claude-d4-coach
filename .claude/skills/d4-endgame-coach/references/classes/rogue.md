# Rogue

> Season: 13 · Patch: 2.4.1 · Last refreshed: 2026-05-19 · Source: https://maxroll.gg/d4/resources/rogue-class-overview

## Core identity
Mobile melee/ranged hybrid that chains precise attacks with Imbuements and debuffs to burst down single targets and packs. Rewards tight resource management and positioning, punishes standing still.

## Damage scaling buckets
- **Multiplicative sources:** Imbuement damage bonuses (Cold/Poison/Shadow per hit on multi-hit skills), Vulnerable multiplier (triggered via Victimize key passive AoE), Specialization bonuses (Combo Points stacked damage, Inner Sight unlimited Energy window, Preparation cooldown reset enabling burst loops)
- **Additive sources:** Dexterity (+11.1% Skill Damage per 100 pts), Intelligence (+2% Critical Strike Chance per 100 pts), Strength (+3% Resource Generation per 100 pts / +200 Armor per 100 pts), flat damage affixes on weapons
- **Critical / Vulnerable / Overpower hooks:** Critical Strike Chance (Intelligence scaling); Vulnerable applied by Invigorating Strike (hits enemy below 50% Energy → 3 sec Vulnerable); Vulnerable required for Victimize AoE proc; Overpower via Blade-Shift-Overpower upgrade

## Common mistakes
- Not maintaining Vulnerable uptime before expecting Victimize AoE procs — the debuff is the entire damage condition
- Picking Shadow Clone as the Ultimate — it misses most endgame damage modifier interactions
- Ignoring Energy recovery; Energy regenerates slowly by default and must be actively managed through Basic skill use and Strength (Resource Generation) investment
- Not choosing a Specialization early — Inner Sight unlocks at level 20, Preparation at level 30; delaying locks out significant damage windows
- Playing only one weapon type consciously — the Rogue auto-swaps between melee and ranged based on skill used (no manual Arsenal system needed), so skill selection drives weapon usage organically
- Stacking only additive damage (flat skill damage, Dexterity) while ignoring multiplicative Imbuement and Vulnerable interactions
- Poor positioning — class strengths (high damage, mobility) are negated by taking avoidable hits due to the class's inherent squishiness
