# Rogue

> Season: 10 · Patch: 2.4.x · Last refreshed: 2026-05-16 · Source: https://maxroll.gg/d4/resources/rogue-class-overview https://www.icy-veins.com/d4/rogue-class-overview

## Core identity
Fast melee/ranged hybrid spending Energy on high-damage Core Skills, enhanced by Imbuements (Cold/Poison/Shadow) and a swappable Specialization mechanic. Positioning and decision-making are central; damage is conditional on debuffs like Vulnerable.

## Damage scaling buckets
- **Multiplicative sources:** Key Passives (Momentum, Precision, Victimize, Close Quarters Combat), Combo Point-empowered Core Skills, Imbuement potency (Alchemical Admixture)
- **Additive sources:** Dexterity (11.1% Skill Damage per 100), Intelligence (2% Crit Chance per 100), attribute bonuses from Paragon boards
- **Critical / Vulnerable / Overpower hooks:** Vulnerable (required for Victimize; applied by Puncture, Forceful Arrow, Invigorating Strike); Critical Strike (guaranteed crit on Precision key passive 3-stack spend; Concealment + Combo Points ambush); Overpower (Willpower scales it but not prioritized for most builds)

## Key passives / class mechanic
**Key Passives (choose one):**
- **Momentum** — Stacks from Cutthroat skills; large additive multiplier; strong early, less scalable late
- **Close Quarters Combat** — Requires Cutthroat + Marksman skill; attack speed steroid + damage buff (active only while both stacks held); scales with damage vs close targets stat
- **Precision** — Marksman only; 3 stacks → next Core/Ultimate Marksman skill = guaranteed Crit + massively increased damage
- **Victimize** — Area Damage proc on Vulnerable targets; best AoE damage scaler
- **Exposure** — Enables Trapper archetype; rapidly reduces cooldowns of Poison Trap, Caltrops, Death Trap
- **Alchemical Admixture** — Imbuement archetype; two active elemental damage types increase all Imbuement potency

**Specialization (unique class mechanic, unlock at level 15 via "True Potential" quest):**
- **Combo Points** — Basic Skills generate up to 3 points; empower next Core Skill (damage + bonus effect per skill: extra arrows, attack speed, movement speed, etc.)
- **Inner Sight** *(unlocks level 20)* — Hitting marked enemy fills gauge; full gauge = zero Energy cost + full Energy for ~?s; weaker vs tanky endgame enemies
- **Preparation** *(unlocks level 30)* — Spending Energy resets Ultimate cooldown; using Ultimate resets all other skills; strong with Death Trap + Exposure or high Attack Speed builds
- Specialization can be swapped at any time including mid-combat

## Common mistakes
- Running Inner Sight in high-end content where enemies are too tanky to fill the gauge efficiently
- Not repositioning after Twisting Blades cast — returning blades must pass through enemies to deal damage
- Using Shadow Clone in endgame — clone does not interact with most endgame damage modifiers
- Neglecting the ranged weapon slot on a melee build (or vice versa) — all equipped weapon stats always apply regardless of attack type
- Picking Willpower over Dexterity/Intelligence for offensive scaling — Willpower provides minimal value for most Rogue builds
- Not swapping Specialization to match current build archetype — it is free to swap at any time
