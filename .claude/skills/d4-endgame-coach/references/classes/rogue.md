# Rogue

> Season: 13 · Patch: 2.4.1 · Last refreshed: 2026-05-17 · Source: https://maxroll.gg/d4/resources/rogue-class-overview

## Core identity
Rogue is a high-damage, mobile striker that seamlessly swaps between melee and ranged weapons based on the skill used. Damage output is conditional — dependent on positioning, debuff uptime (Vulnerable, Poison, Shadow, Cold), and Imbuement management.

## Damage scaling buckets
- **Multiplicative sources:** Imbuement effects (Shadow/Poison/Cold applied per hit on Flurry, Barrage, Rapid Fire); Specialization bonuses (Combo Points, Inner Sight, Preparation); Key Passive procs (Victimize, Momentum, Close Quarters Combat, Precision)
- **Additive sources:** Dexterity (+11.1% Skill Damage per 100 pts); Intelligence (+2% Critical Strike Chance per 100 pts, +40 All Resistances per 100 pts); Strength (+3% Resource Generation per 100 pts, +200 Armor per 100 pts)
- **Critical / Vulnerable / Overpower hooks:** Vulnerable applied via Invigorating Strike (hits enemy below 50% Energy), Cold Imbuement, and Shadow Imbuement; Victimize key passive triggers AoE damage explosions off Vulnerable; Overpower scaling via Willpower (+25% Overpower Damage per 100 pts); Intelligence scales Crit Chance

## Common mistakes
- Ignoring Vulnerable uptime — Victimize and Invigorating Strike both hinge on consistent Vulnerable application; letting it drop kills DPS
- Equipping Shadow Clone as the Ultimate — it interacts poorly with endgame damage modifiers; prefer Death Trap or Rain of Arrows
- Investing in Willpower — low value for most Rogue builds; prioritize Dexterity and Intelligence
- Using multi-hit skills (Flurry, Barrage, Rapid Fire) without an Imbuement active — these skills apply Imbuement on every hit for free; not having one loaded wastes the mechanic
- Forgetting weapon-swap is automatic — unlike Barbarian, Rogue has no manual Arsenal management; build around both melee and ranged skill slots freely
- Neglecting positioning — Rogue is squishy with conditional damage; standing still or pulling excess packs without Dark Shroud/Concealment active spikes incoming damage sharply
- Taking Momentum into endgame — strong while leveling, but does not scale into high-tier content
