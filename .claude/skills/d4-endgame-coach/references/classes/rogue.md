# Rogue

> Season: 13 · Patch: 2.4.1 · Last refreshed: 2026-05-18 · Source: https://maxroll.gg/d4/resources/rogue-class-overview

## Core identity
Fast-moving melee/ranged hybrid that weaves Imbuements onto Core Skills to burst enemies with Cold, Poison, or Shadow damage. Effective play demands precise positioning and debuff management — particularly keeping Vulnerable active — in exchange for top-tier single-target and AoE output.

## Damage scaling buckets
- **Multiplicative sources:** Imbuement skill damage (applied per-hit on Flurry, Barrage, Rapid Fire without consuming extra charges); Specialization bonuses (Combo Points scaling at 1/2/3 stacks); Vulnerable debuff (required for Victimize key passive AoE proc)
- **Additive sources:** Dexterity (+11.1% Skill Damage per 100 pts); Intelligence (+2% Critical Strike Chance per 100 pts, +40 All Resistances per 100 pts); Strength (+3% Resource Generation per 100 pts)
- **Critical / Vulnerable / Overpower hooks:** Vulnerable — applied by Invigorating Strike (hits below 50% Energy, lasts 3 sec) and other sources; Critical Strike scaled by Intelligence; Overpower accessible via Blade-Shift-Overpower upgrade

## Common mistakes
- Neglecting Vulnerable uptime — Victimize does nothing without it; always have a reliable Vulnerable applicator in the build
- Picking Shadow Clone as the Ultimate — it lacks interaction with most endgame damage modifiers
- Ignoring Energy management — Energy recovers slowly on its own; Basic Skills and Strength (resource generation) are the primary accelerators
- Undervaluing Dexterity — it provides both Skill Damage (+11.1% per 100) and Dodge Chance (+0.6% per 100), making it the primary offensive stat
- Stacking Willpower — considered low value for most Rogue builds
- Skipping the Enhance + Upgrade steps on key skills — each skill has a mandatory Enhancement point before specialization becomes available
- Poor positioning — the class is squishy and conditional damage requires being in the right place relative to marked/Vulnerable targets
