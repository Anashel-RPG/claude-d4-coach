# Rogue

> Season: 13 · Patch: 2.4.1 · Last refreshed: 2026-05-25 · Source: https://maxroll.gg/d4/resources/rogue-class-overview

## Core identity
Fast-paced melee/ranged hybrid that weaves Imbuements into Core Skills to spike damage through Critical Strikes, Vulnerable, and Poison. Requires careful positioning and Energy management to unlock its full burst ceiling.

## Damage scaling buckets
- **Multiplicative sources:** Imbuement damage bonuses (Cold/Poison/Shadow), Vulnerable multiplier (requires debuff on target), Specialization bonuses (Combo Points rank-up multiplier, Inner Sight unlimited Energy window, Preparation cooldown reset)
- **Additive sources:** Dexterity — 11.1% Skill Damage per 100 points; Strength — 3% Resource Generation per 100 points; flat % Skill Damage affixes on gear
- **Critical / Vulnerable / Overpower hooks:** Intelligence — 2% Critical Strike Chance per 100 points; Dexterity — 0.6% Dodge Chance per 100 points; Invigorating Strike applies Vulnerable (3 s) on hits below 50% Energy; Victimize key passive triggers AoE burst when hitting Vulnerable targets

**Class mechanics:**
- **Specializations** (choose one; Preparation unlocks at level 30):
  - *Combo Points* — Basic Skills build points that amplify Core Skill damage
  - *Inner Sight* — Gauge fills by hitting a marked enemy; larger hits fill faster; full gauge grants unlimited Energy window
  - *Preparation* — (exact buff not covered in source; unlocks level 30)
- Weapons: equip two one-handed melee (Sword/Dagger) + one ranged (Bow/Crossbow); class auto-swaps based on ability used — no manual Arsenal selection
- Primary resource: **Energy** (slow natural regeneration)
- Default damage type: **Physical**; Imbuements add **Cold, Poison, or Shadow**
- Skill upgrades: unlock → 1 Enhance point → choose 1 of 2 Specialization upgrades

## Paragon priority
- Starting board: Key nodes at X14-Y12 and X8-Y12 (used by Heartseeker and Rain of Arrows builds); path X8-Y6 through X8-Y9 for Rain of Arrows
- Glyph order to L15: Boards referenced across top builds — **Eldritch Bounty**, **Cheap Shot**, **Tricks of the Trade**, **Cunning Stratagem**, **No Witnesses**, **Danse Macabre**; prioritize the board matching your build's ultimate (Danse Macabre for Death Trap, Cunning Stratagem for Dance of Knives, Eldritch Bounty + Cheap Shot for Heartseeker/Rain of Arrows)

## Common mistakes
- Not maintaining Vulnerable uptime before spending Energy — Victimize and Invigorating Strike synergy breaks down without it
- Ignoring positioning requirements; Rogue is notably squishy and punished heavily for standing still in dense packs
- Running the wrong Specialization for the build — Combo Points, Inner Sight, and Preparation are not interchangeable
- Slotting two Key Passives — only one can be active at a time
- Neglecting Energy management; Energy recovers slowly naturally, so relying on passive regen instead of Basic Skills starves Core Skill uptime
- Using Rain of Arrows as the ultimate without Imbuing it — it is one of the few ultimates that accepts Imbuements
