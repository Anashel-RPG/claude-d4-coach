# Druid

> Season: 10 · Patch: 2.4.x · Last refreshed: 2026-05-16 · Source: https://maxroll.gg/d4/resources/druid-class-overview https://www.icy-veins.com/d4/druid-class-overview

## Core identity
Druid shapeshifts between Werewolf, Werebear, and human form, spending Spirit (generated by Basic Skills) on high-cost Core Skills. Defensively strong in shapeshift forms; relatively fragile and immobile in human/caster form. Primarily Physical, Lightning, and Poison damage.

## Damage scaling buckets
- **Multiplicative sources:** Willpower (12.5% Skill Damage per 100), Key Passives (Ursine Strength, Bestial Rampage, Lupine Ferocity, Perfect Storm, Nature's Fury, Earthen Might, One With Nature), Legendary Aspect multipliers on 2H weapons
- **Additive sources:** Spirit Boon bonuses (Critical Strike Chance, Critical Strike Damage, Attack Speed), Companion damage (Wolves, Ravens, Poison Creeper), Dexterity (2% Crit Chance per 100)
- **Critical / Vulnerable / Overpower hooks:** Storm Strike (high Vulnerable chance); Snake Spirit Boon (guaranteed Overpower on Earth Skills); Petrify (bonus Crit Damage vs bosses); Earthen Might (Crit proc); Rampaging Werebeast (stacking Crit Damage during Grizzly Rage); Dexterity (Crit Chance); Willpower (25% Overpower Damage per 100)

## Key passives / class mechanic
**Spirit Boons (class mechanic):** Unlock at level 15 via quest "Spirits of the Lost Grove" after clearing Túr Dúlra stronghold. Four Spirits (Deer, Eagle, Wolf, Snake), each with 4 Boons; costs 15 Druidic Spirit Offerings per Boon (240 total to unlock all). Snake Spirit requires all 4 unlocked at once (60 Offerings). Bond with one Spirit to select 2 Boons from it; one Boon each from the other three. Boons swappable at any time.

- **Deer:** Thorns, max Spirit, Damage Reduction vs Elites, Movement Speed / Impairment Reduction
- **Eagle:** Critical Strike Chance, Lucky Hit Chance, Attack Speed, Critical Strike Damage, bonus Life
- **Wolf:** Lucky Hit → reset Companion Cooldowns, Lucky Hit → grant Spirit, Fortify boost, extend Ultimate duration
- **Snake:** Guaranteed Overpower on Earth Skills, Lightning discharge proc, Fortify on Shapeshifting Crit, chance to reduce Ultimate Cooldown on Nature Magic use

**Key Passives (choose one):**
- Ursine Strength — any-Skill damage increase
- Bestial Rampage — bonus from alternating forms; enhanced by Wildheart Hunger
- Lupine Ferocity — synergy with Tempest Roar Storm Skills
- Perfect Storm — strong while leveling Storm builds
- Nature's Fury — Earth/Storm Skills trigger each other; interaction rules: Earth Spike only triggers Wind Shear; Landslide triggers Tornado or Lightning Storm (not both)
- Earthen Might — Crit-triggers; activated by Pulverize (Ursine Horror) or Storm Skills (Mighty Storm's)
- One With Nature — Companions passively attack regardless of skill bar presence

## Common mistakes
- Playing in human caster form without accounting for reduced survivability vs shapeshift forms
- Choosing a Two-Handed Weapon vs 1H + Totem without comparing Legendary Aspect count and power — one strong Aspect on a 2H often outweighs two weaker Aspects
- Casting Hurricane twice unnecessarily, which resets the Tempest damage ramp
- Casting Enhanced Lightning Storm more than once every 6 seconds — the Storm size buff just needs one cast to maintain, not continuous channeling
- Not having Snake Spirit Offerings saved (60 required) before attempting to unlock Snake Boons
- Bonding with a Spirit without reviewing which two Boons provide the best synergy for the active build
- Placing Trample on cooldown for damage instead of reserving it as a CC escape tool
- Expecting Companions to function passively without at least one on the skill bar (One With Nature key passive required for passive attacks without skill bar placement)
