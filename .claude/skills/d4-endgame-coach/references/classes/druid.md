# Druid

> Season: 13 · Patch: 2.4.1 · Last refreshed: 2026-05-20 · Source: https://maxroll.gg/d4/resources/druid-class-overview

## Core identity
Druid blends shapeshifting (Werewolf / Werebear), nature spells, and wildlife companions into a versatile class with high burst damage and strong defensive utility. Shapeshifted forms are tankier; human-form caster playstyle is squishier and demands careful positioning.

## Common mistakes
- Skipping Spirit Boon unlocks early — Druidic Spirit Offerings drop from level 15 onward; all 16 Boons cost 240 total Offerings (15 each), but all four Snake Boons must be unlocked together (60 Offerings at once), so save up before attempting Snake.
- Trying to bond with a Spirit before all 16 Boons are unlocked — the double-Boon bond perk requires full unlock first.
- Leaving Spirit Boons unswapped when changing build focus — Boons can be swapped freely, even mid-combat.
- Running Werebear skills outside Grizzly Rage prep — while in Grizzly Rage, only Werebear Skills on the skill bar are usable; plan the bar accordingly.
- Using a Two-Handed Weapon expecting fast clear — 2H grants stronger individual hits and more powerful Legendary Aspects but has slower attack speed; swap to 1H + Totem when attack speed matters.
- Not slotting a Basic Skill — Spirit is generated exclusively by Basic Skills; omitting one starves all Core and Ultimate spending.
- Over-investing in human-form nature spells without defensive layers — human form has no shapeshifting damage reduction and limited mobility options.
