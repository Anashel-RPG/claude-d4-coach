# Druid

> Season: 13 · Patch: 2.4.1 · Last refreshed: 2026-05-21 · Source: https://maxroll.gg/d4/resources/druid-class-overview

## Core identity
Druid harnesses nature magic, shapeshifting, and animal companions to deliver high burst damage with strong defensive utility. It excels in Werebear and Werewolf forms but is comparatively fragile when casting spells in human form.

## Paragon priority
- Starting board: Path through key magic/rare clusters toward the board socket; Mega-Bear-Punch routing uses a wide sweep of nodes across the starting board before transitioning to secondary boards.
- Glyph order to L15: Mega-Bear-Punch build routes through **Earthen Devastation**, **Survival Instincts**, **Heightened Malice**, and **Sinister Tendrils** boards — prioritize the glyph socket on whichever board provides the largest damage multiplier for Pulverize first.

## Common mistakes
- Picking Spirit Boons before unlocking all 16 (costs 240 Druidic Spirit Offerings total, 15 per Boon) — you cannot bond with a Spirit for a double Boon until all are unlocked.
- Forgetting that Druidic Spirit Offerings don't drop until level 15; plan your leveling accordingly.
- Slotting Companions on the bar just for passive attacks when One With Nature is the chosen Key Passive — passive attacks trigger regardless; bar slots are only needed for active Companion abilities.
- Ignoring positioning; Druid has few mobility tools, so poor placement in human-form casting windows is a primary cause of death.
- Entering Grizzly Rage without Werebear skills on the bar — only those skills are usable during the Ultimate.
- Using a Two-Handed Weapon when attack speed matters more than raw hit power; know the trade-off (stronger Aspects and hits, slower attack speed) before committing.
- Trying to maintain human-form casting in high-content without defensive investment — nature spells offer little innate mitigation compared to shapeshifted forms.
