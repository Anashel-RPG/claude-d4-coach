# Druid

> Season: 13 · Patch: 2.4.1 · Last refreshed: 2026-05-16 · Source: https://maxroll.gg/d4/resources/druid-class-overview

## Core identity
Druid fights through shapeshifting (Werewolf / Werebear), nature spells, and wildlife companions, dealing Physical, Lightning, and Poison damage. Shapeshift forms are defensively robust; human-form casting is squishier with limited mobility, demanding disciplined positioning.

## Common mistakes
- Skipping "Spirits of the Lost Grove" — the Priority Quest auto-starts at level 15 and clearing Túr Dúlra Stronghold is required to unlock Spirit Boons; delaying it locks you out of a major power system.
- Not collecting enough Druidic Spirit Offerings: all 16 Boons cost 240 total (Snake's 4 Boons must all be unlocked at once for 60 Offerings); missing the bond unlock (requires all Boons) means only one Boon per Spirit instead of two from your bonded Spirit.
- Forgetting that Spirit Boons can be swapped freely at any time — swap situationally rather than treating them as permanent choices.
- Running Grizzly Rage without a Werebear-focused Skill bar — the Ultimate locks you into Werebear Skills only; non-Werebear skills on the bar are wasted slots.
- Using Landslide as an AoE clear tool — it is single-target/stationary specialist; pair with Nature's Fury for storm proc value rather than expecting pack clear.
- Neglecting positioning — Druid has minimal mobility (Trample is the primary option, valued mainly for Unstoppable, not gap-closing); poor positioning in human form leaves you exposed.
- Treating Intelligence as a damage stat — it provides resistances and resource generation, not direct damage; Willpower is the primary damage-scaling primary stat.
- Channeling Stone Burst without managing Spirit reserves — it ramps in size/damage but will drain Spirit rapidly, interrupting the channel at the worst moment.
