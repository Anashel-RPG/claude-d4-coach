# Druid

> Season: 13 · Patch: 2.4.1 · Last refreshed: 2026-05-25 · Source: https://maxroll.gg/d4/resources/druid-class-overview

## Core identity
Druid is a shapeshifting class that blends nature magic, Werebear/Werewolf melee, and wildlife companions. It excels at burst damage and defensive utility but demands careful positioning due to limited mobility.

**Spirit Boons (class mechanic)**
- Unique to Druid; choose one passive Boon from each of four Spirits: **Deer, Eagle, Snake, Wolf**
- 16 total Boons to unlock; each costs 15 Druidic Spirit Offerings (240 total)
- Spirit Offerings begin dropping from monsters at level 15
- Snake Spirit: all four Boons must be unlocked simultaneously (60 Offerings in one purchase)
- After unlocking all 16 Boons, bond with one Spirit to select **two** Boons from it instead of one
- Boons can be swapped at any time, including mid-combat

## Common mistakes
- Playing human-form nature spells without defensive investment — Druid is noticeably squishy outside shapeshifting forms
- Not unlocking Spirit Boons early; Offerings drop from level 15 onward, so prioritize farming 240 total to access the double-Boon bond
- Forgetting that Grizzly Rage locks you to only Werebear Skills on the bar — building a mixed bar will waste button slots during the ultimate
- Putting Companion skills off the skill bar while not running One With Nature — companions become inactive
- Not swapping Spirit Boons situationally; they can be changed mid-combat at no cost
- Ignoring positioning — Druid has very few mobility tools, so poor placement leads to avoidable deaths
