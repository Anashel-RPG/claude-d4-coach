# Spiritborn

> Season: 13 · Patch: 2.4.1 · Last refreshed: 2026-05-24 · Source: https://maxroll.gg/d4/resources/spiritborn-class-overview

## Core identity
Spiritborn is a brand-new class introduced with the Vessel of Hatred expansion (Season 13, Patch 2.4.1). It channels four Spirit Guardians to deal elemental damage, weaving Basic Skills (Vigor generation) with Core and Ultimate Skills across Jaguar (Fire), Eagle (Lightning), Gorilla (Physical), and Centipede (Poison) themes.

## Damage scaling buckets
- **Multiplicative sources:** Dexterity (multiplies all damage done); Spirit Hall Primary selection (all skills count as chosen Guardian's skills, enabling Guardian-specific multipliers); Adaptive Stances key passive (empowers based on invoked Spirit)
- **Additive sources:** Skill ranks (passives scale linearly across 3 ranks); Ferocity stacks (generated by Rake/Thrash, enhances attack speed and damage); Thorns (via Rock Splitter)
- **Critical / Vulnerable / Overpower hooks:** Vital Strikes key passive (synergizes with Vulnerable — enhances damage, provides healing, restores Vigor); The Seeker Ultimate (applies Vulnerable to nearby enemies); Counterattack passive (retaliates on each successful dodge, potential Crit synergy)

| Key Passive | Buffs (state/skill) | Effect | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|

## Common mistakes
- Skipping the Spirit Hall unlock at level 15 — choosing your Primary Spirit is mandatory for all skill-type synergies to function.
- Running a second Ultimate when only one can be slotted; invest those points elsewhere.
- Ignoring Dexterity on gear — it is the primary damage multiplier for the class, not a secondary stat.
- Not stacking Vigor generation from Basic Skills before spending on Core Skills; Vigor starvation kills damage output.
- Picking Ferocity-generating skills (Thrash, Rake, Ravager) without a payoff skill that consumes or benefits from Ferocity stacks.
- Playing Spiritborn without owning the Vessel of Hatred expansion — the class is expansion-locked.
