# Spiritborn

> Season: 13 · Patch: 2.4.1 · Last refreshed: 2026-05-21 · Source: https://maxroll.gg/d4/resources/spiritborn-class-overview

## Core identity
Brand-new class introduced in the Vessel of Hatred expansion (Season 13, Patch 2.4.1). The Spiritborn channels four Spirit Guardians—Jaguar, Gorilla, Eagle, and Centipede—spending Vigor to unleash elemental attacks, with playstyle defined by which Guardian spirits are slotted in the Spirit Hall.

## Damage scaling buckets
- **Multiplicative sources:** Dexterity (multiplies all damage done); Spirit Guardian elemental bonuses (Fire/Lightning/Physical/Poison per guardian)
- **Additive sources:** Skill rank bonuses; passive skill ranks (3 ranks each, scale linearly)
- **Critical / Vulnerable / Overpower hooks:** Vital Strikes key passive (synergizes with Vulnerable — enhances damage, provides healing, restores Vigor); The Seeker ultimate (applies Vulnerable to nearby foes)

## Paragon priority
- Starting board: Path through nodes at X10Y8–Y12, X13Y7–Y10, X9Y6, X9Y12 — confirmed used across multiple builds
- Glyph order to L15: Sapping board and Convergence board are core second/third board picks (both appear in multiple build paths); Drive and Viscous Shield boards are also heavily used; In-Fighter board appears in Rake/Fireborn builds — prioritize glyphs on Sapping → Convergence → Drive → Viscous Shield

## Common mistakes
- Skipping the Spirit Hall at level 15 — not selecting a Primary Spirit is a major damage loss since all skills stop counting as Guardian skills without it
- Selecting a Secondary Spirit at level 30 that conflicts with your Primary Spirit's elemental focus (e.g., mixing Fire/Jaguar with Poison/Centipede without a dedicated hybrid build)
- Using Ultimate Skills without a plan for their long cooldown — only one Ultimate can be chosen; pick based on whether you need Vulnerable application (The Seeker) or burst reset potential (The Hunter)
- Ignoring Dexterity on gear — it is the primary damage multiplier for the class
- Treating Basic Skills as filler — they are the sole source of Vigor generation; dropping a Basic Skill leaves you resource-starved
- Positioning poorly with Crushing Hand — the two shockwaves must overlap at center to maximize damage on elites and bosses
