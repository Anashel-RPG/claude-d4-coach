# Spiritborn

> Season: 13 · Patch: 2.4.1 · Last refreshed: 2026-05-25 · Source: https://maxroll.gg/d4/resources/spiritborn-class-overview

## Core identity
Brand-new class introduced with the Vessel of Hatred expansion (Season 13, Patch 2.4.1). The Spiritborn channels four Spirit Guardians—Jaguar, Gorilla, Eagle, and Centipede—through the Spirit Hall mechanic, weaving their elemental powers to deal damage and survive while spending Vigor generated by Basic Skills.

## Damage scaling buckets
- **Multiplicative sources:** Dexterity (multiplies all damage done); Spirit Hall Primary Spirit bonus (all skills count as chosen Spirit's skills, enabling concentrated synergies)
- **Additive sources:** Passive skills (3 ranks each, linear scaling); Ultimate Skill upgrades (2-step linear path)
- **Critical / Vulnerable / Overpower hooks:** Vital Strikes key passive (synergizes with Vulnerable to enhance damage, provide healing, and restore Vigor); The Seeker ultimate (applies Vulnerable to nearby enemies); Counterattack passive (dodge-based retaliation)

## Common mistakes
- Not selecting a Primary Spirit at level 15 via the Spirit Hall — this is the largest single power spike and is missable if you skip the unlock.
- Choosing a Secondary Spirit at level 30 that conflicts with your Primary Spirit's element, losing concentrated damage bonuses.
- Using Crushing Hand without positioning enemies between the two shockwave overlap points — damage is significantly lower on single-target if enemies are outside the overlap zone.
- Neglecting Dexterity on gear; it is the primary damage multiplier for the class and outweighs most other stat options.
- Equipping more than one Ultimate Skill upgrade path — only one Ultimate can be chosen; investing upgrade points into unused Ultimates is wasted.
- Ignoring Vigor generation from Basic Skills early on, then running dry during Core Skill rotations.
- Treating all passive skill ranks equally — passives have only 3 ranks and scale linearly; over-investing past rank 3 on any passive is impossible but players sometimes deprioritize reaching rank 3 on key passives.
