# Spiritborn

> Season: 10 · Patch: 2.4.x · Last refreshed: 2026-05-16 · Source: https://maxroll.gg/d4/resources/spiritborn-class-overview https://www.icy-veins.com/d4/spiritborn-class-overview

## Core identity
Spiritborn draws power from four Spirit Guardians (Jaguar, Eagle, Gorilla, Centipede), mixing and matching their skills to build around a chosen primary and secondary spirit. Vigor is the class resource, generated by Basic Skills.

## Damage scaling buckets
- **Multiplicative sources:** Dexterity (12.5% Skill Damage per 100), Beat Strike damage stacking (Jaguar primary), Spirit Hall bonuses
- **Additive sources:** Strength (Armor, 2% Crit Chance per 100), Intelligence (All Resistances, Resource Generation)
- **Critical / Vulnerable / Overpower hooks:** Eagle secondary (every other cast = guaranteed Crit + 20%[x] Vulnerable Damage bonus); Vital Strikes key passive (Vulnerable synergy); Noxious Resonance key passive (Crit consumes poison for burst); Willpower (25% Overpower Damage per 100)

## Key passives / class mechanic

**Key Passives (pick one):**
- **Vital Strikes** — Vulnerable synergy: bonus damage, healing, Vigor restore
- **Adaptive Stances** — Empowers based on invoked Spirit
- **Prodigy's Tempo** — Bonus for using same skill 3× consecutively
- **Noxious Resonance** — Critical Strike consumes poison for burst damage

**Spirit Hall (class mechanic):**
- Unlocked at level 15 (Primary) and level 30 (Secondary)
- Primary spirit makes all skills count as that spirit type in addition to their normal type

| Spirit | Primary Bonus | Secondary Bonus |
|---|---|---|
| Jaguar | Every 15th direct hit triggers Beat Strike (100%[x] dmg); stacks up to 5×, each doubling; +3%[x] all damage per stack; resets after 1s no attack | +1 max Ferocity; gain Ferocity on kill or boss hit |
| Eagle | Eagle Skill or Evade fires 6 Storm Feathers (140%[x] Lightning, Vulnerable 5s); 0.5s ICD; Storm Feather speed bonus doubled and always active | Every other cast = guaranteed Crit + 20%[x] Vulnerable Damage for 5s |
| Gorilla | Gorilla Skill attacks deal 150% Thorns and grant Barrier (2.5% max Life, 3s) | +2 max Resolve; at 5+ Resolve stacks → Unstoppable |
| Centipede | Centipede Skill hits reduce enemy damage 4%, Slow 10%, apply 160%[x] Poison over 8s (stacks 8×) | Heal 1% max Life/sec per nearby Poisoned enemy (cap 5%) |

**Keywords:**
- **Ferocity** — Increases Attack Speed; default cap 4 stacks; Jaguar-themed
- **Resolve** — Damage Reduction while ≥1 stack active; direct hits consume stacks; cap 8; Gorilla-themed
- **Storm Feathers** — Grant movement speed; Eagle skills consume them to deal damage
- **Pestilent Swarms** — Poison damage swarms created by Centipede skills

## Common mistakes
- Willpower is a low-priority attribute for most Spiritborn builds; do not stack it at the expense of Dexterity
- Only one Ultimate and one Key Passive can be equipped at a time
- Crushing Hand shockwaves must overlap on the target to maximize damage; poor positioning wastes the skill
- Ravager buff drops on boss transitions if kills are not secured on trash along the way
- Beat Strike stacks (Jaguar primary) reset after 1 second without attacking; do not pause during DPS windows
