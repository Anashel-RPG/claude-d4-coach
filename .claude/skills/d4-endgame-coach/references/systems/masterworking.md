# Masterworking

> Season: 13 · Patch: 2.4.1 · Last refreshed: 2026-05-25 · Source: https://maxroll.gg/d4/resources/masterworking-guide

## What it is

Masterworking is an endgame crafting system accessed at the **Blacksmith** NPC (found in any major Town) that improves the quality of affixes — including Tempering Affixes — on **Legendary, Unique, and Mythic Unique** equipment. Max quality rank is **25**, after which a Capstone Bonus upgrade becomes available.

*(Season 13, Patch 2.4.1)*

---

## Mechanics

1. **+1% per rank** to base Damage, Armor, Resistance, and all affix values (including Tempering Affixes). Total at rank 25: **+25%**.
2. Each upgrade attempt grants **1–5 Quality ranks** at random — progression speed is variable.
3. **Obducite cost per upgrade** follows the formula: `floor(3.75 × CurrentQuality + 10)`.
4. **Capstone Bonus** unlocks at rank 25: grants **+50%** to a randomly selected affix.
5. Capstone Bonus can be **rerolled** by paying Obducite + Neathiron + Gold (see Costs).
6. **Greater Affix interaction:** a Greater Affix already carries a +50% bonus; the Capstone +50% stacks with it separately — and both stack with the +25% from MW ranks.
   - Example: 18 Max Resource (Greater Affix, no Capstone) at rank 25 → **32** (18 × (1 + 25% + 50%)).

## Costs / requirements

**Where to access:** Blacksmith NPC in any major Town.

**Materials consumed:**

| Material | Source |
|---|---|
| Obducite | Nightmare Dungeons, Undercity (Tribute of Refinement), Infernal Hordes, Mercenary Bartering |
| Veiled Crystals | Standard crafting drops (used for 750 IP items) |
| Abstruse Sigils | Nightmare Dungeon activities |
| Forgotten Souls | Standard crafting drops (used for 800+ IP items) |
| Gold | Always required |
| Neathiron | Endgame Bosses throughout Sanctuary (Capstone reroll only) |

**Capstone Bonus reroll cost:** 200 Obducite + 1 Neathiron + 1,000,000 Gold per reroll.

## Strategy

1. **Farm Obducite efficiently** — prioritize sources in this order: Nightmare Dungeon with Treasure Breach sigil affix (Goblin NMD) → Strongroom Chest (NMD sigil affix) → Undercity with Tribute of Refinement → Infernal Hordes → other NMD sigils → Mercenary Bartering for Masterworking Cache (lowest priority).
2. **Temper before Masterworking** — Masterworking amplifies Tempering Affix values, so lock in your desired Tempering Affixes first.
3. **Socket gems before Masterworking** to avoid wasting upgrade resources on a piece you may replace.
4. **Prioritize Greater Affix items** as Masterworking targets — the Capstone Bonus +50% stacks with the Greater Affix +50%, maximising affix value.
5. **Hold Capstone rerolls** until you have a healthy Neathiron reserve; each reroll costs 1 Neathiron + 200 Obducite + 1,000,000 Gold and is random.
6. **Push the Pit of Artificers** only after gear is fully Tempered and Masterworked for maximum effectiveness.

## Pitfalls

- **RNG per upgrade (1–5 Quality ranks):** worst-case runs require ~2.5–3.5× more Obducite than best case — budget accordingly.
- **Capstone targets a random affix:** rerolling is expensive; don't expect the Capstone to land on your best affix without multiple attempts.
- **Neathiron is gated behind endgame Bosses:** don't plan Capstone rerolls if you haven't been running boss content consistently.
- **Higher item power = more expensive secondary mats:** 900 IP items replace Abstruse Sigils with Forgotten Souls, which can be scarcer.
- **Masterworking a piece you'll replace is wasted Obducite** — confirm the item is your long-term BiS before investing heavily.
- **Mercenary Bartering for Masterworking Cache is the least efficient Obducite source** — use it only as a last resort.
