# Masterworking

> Season: 13 · Patch: 2.4.1 · Last refreshed: 2026-05-24 · Source: https://maxroll.gg/d4/resources/masterworking-guide

## What it is

Masterworking is an endgame crafting system accessed at the **Blacksmith** (found in any major Town). It improves the quality of affixes — including Tempering Affixes — on **Legendary**, **Unique**, and **Mythic Unique** equipment, scaling all stats by +1% per rank up to a maximum of rank 25. *(Season 13, Patch 2.4.1)*

---

## Mechanics

1. Each upgrade attempt grants **1–5 Quality ranks** at random, making total upgrade count variable.
2. Every Quality rank adds **+1% to base Damage, Armor, Resistance, and all affix values** (including Tempering Affixes).
3. At **rank 25** the total bonus is **+25%** to all of the above.
4. Reaching rank 25 unlocks the **Capstone Bonus** upgrade — a one-time +50% bonus applied to a random affix (can target Tempering Affixes).
5. The Capstone Bonus **stacks** with the Greater Affix +50% bonus on the same affix.
   - Example: base 18 Max Resource (Greater Affix) at rank 25 + Capstone → 18 × (1 + 25% + 50%) = **32**.
6. The Capstone Bonus can be **rerolled** by paying Obducite, Neathiron, and Gold (see Costs below).
7. Per-upgrade Obducite cost formula: **floor(3.75 × CurrentQuality + 10)**.

## Recipes / breakpoints / interactions

| Rank | Effect |
|---|---|
| 1–25 | +1% per rank to Damage, Armor, Resistance, all affixes (including Tempering Affixes) |
| 25 | Total: **+25%** to all stats; Capstone Bonus upgrade becomes available |
| Capstone | **+50%** to one random affix; can target Tempering Affixes; stackable with Greater Affix bonus |

**Greater Affix + Capstone interaction:**
`Final value = base × (1 + 0.25 [MW] + 0.50 [Greater Affix] + 0.50 [Capstone])`
- Example (18 Max Resource, Greater Affix, rank 25 + Capstone): **18 × 1.75 = 32**

## Strategy

1. **Prioritize high-item-power pieces** before investing heavy Obducite — cost scales with item power tier and secondary material requirements.
2. **Target Greater Affix items** for Masterworking; the Capstone +50% stacks with the existing Greater Affix +50%, maximizing the return on a single affix.
3. **Aim the Capstone at your most impactful affix** (primary damage multiplier or core Tempering Affix); reroll with Neathiron if it lands poorly.
4. **Farm Goblin NMD sigils first** for Obducite efficiency before falling back to Undercity or Infernal Hordes.
5. **Follow the recommended crafting order:** Socket gems → Masterwork to rank 25 → Apply/reroll Tempering → reroll Capstone if needed.
6. **Fully Masterwork gear before pushing the Pit of Artificers** — the +25% across all stats is a significant power gate.

## Pitfalls

- **Obducite is the hard bottleneck** — costs balloon sharply at bad RNG (worst-case 1,366 vs. best-case 492 for 850+ IP items).
- **Capstone target is random** — budget Neathiron for rerolls if the Capstone lands on a low-value affix.
- **Dismantling a Masterworked item returns only a small fraction of Obducite spent** — do not Masterwork items you plan to replace soon.
- **Secondary material requirements shift by IP tier** — upgrading a 900 IP item requires Forgotten Souls (up to 275 worst-case), which can be a separate farming bottleneck.
- **Each upgrade grants 1–5 ranks randomly** — worst-case scenarios cost more than 3× the best case; plan material reserves accordingly.
- **Rerolling the Capstone requires Neathiron** (endgame-boss exclusive drop) — if Neathiron is scarce, you may be stuck with a suboptimal Capstone target.
