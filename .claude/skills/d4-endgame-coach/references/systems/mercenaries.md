# Mercenaries

> Season: 13 · Patch: 2.4 · Last refreshed: 2026-05-29 · Coaching synthesis over d4-mcp graph data

## What it is
Companion system introduced in Vessel of Hatred. Four hireable NPCs (Pale Hand Mercenaries) each with unique skill trees. One acts as Hired (active), one as Reinforcement (summoned on command). Available in solo and multiplayer.

## Mechanics
1. Unlock Raheir automatically after the Vessel of Hatred campaign; unlock Aldkin, Varyana, and Subo via key quests from The Den.
2. One Hired Mercenary fights alongside you continuously using their full skill tree and passives.
3. One Reinforcement Mercenary is summoned tag-team style when triggered; uses only base skill versions, unaffected by passives.
4. Mercenaries can "die" (take a knee); player can revive them like a co-op player.
5. Rapport is earned by fighting with Mercenaries. Hired Mercenary earns full Rapport; Reinforcement earns 50% of normal rate.
6. Rapport unlocks skill points (tiers 1–4), Bartering upgrades (tiers 5, 8), caches (tiers 6, 7, 9, 10), and Pale Marks (tiers 5+).
7. At max Rapport (tier 10), replaying tier 10 yields Pale Marks indefinitely.
8. Mercenary-related events appear in Sanctuary and award bonus Rapport.

## Strategy
1. Prioritize Hired Mercenary choice based on build needs — passives directly affect your build's performance.
2. Use Raheir as Hired if you need armor/resistance to reach Torment IV survivability thresholds.
3. Use Subo as Hired for DoT, Overpower, or Crit builds; use Seeker passive for overworld mapping efficiency.
4. Use Aldkin cautiously — Blasphemous Fate applies Vulnerable to you; confirm your build benefits before selecting.
5. Use Varyana as Hired in high-density content; her grouping frees up build skill slots.
6. Farm Rapport via active combat; prioritize Mercenary events in Sanctuary for bonus Rapport.
7. Spend Pale Marks at Fayira for targeted Aspects or caches; restock at 100 Pale Marks if needed.
8. Reinforce with a Mercenary whose triggered skill complements your active skill usage.

## Pitfalls
- Reinforcement Mercenary passives do NOT apply — only base skill executes.
- Aldkin's Blasphemous Fate can make the player Vulnerable, which is harmful if your build does not exploit Vulnerable.
- Varyana's attack speed buffs (Bloodthirst, Hysteria) require her to actively hit targets — uptime is unreliable against mobile or sparse enemies.
- Subo and Aldkin both have knockback, which can disrupt grouping and AoE positioning.
- Rapport on Reinforcement earns at only 50% rate — factor this in when planning Pale Mark farming.
- Scattering monsters (Raheir con) can break AoE-dependent builds.
