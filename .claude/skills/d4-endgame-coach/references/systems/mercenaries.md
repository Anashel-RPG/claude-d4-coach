# Mercenaries

> Season: 13 · Patch: 2.4.1 · Last refreshed: 2026-05-25 · Source: https://maxroll.gg/d4/resources/mercenaries-overview

## What it is

Mercenaries are a companion system introduced in the **Vessel of Hatred** expansion (Season 13, Patch 2.4.1). Players equip up to two Mercenaries at once and interact with them through **The Den**, the Mercenary hideout.

| Mercenary | Role | Notable Trait |
|-----------|------|---------------|
| **Raheir** | Defensive (tank) | Shield user; absorbs damage, draws aggro |
| **Subo** | Utility (ranged) | Disgraced archer; marks enemies, provides CDR |
| **Aldkin** | Caster | Half-child, half-demon; can shapeshift into demon form |
| **Varyana** | Melee DPS | Former cannibal; Massacre Meter tracks kill streak |

---

## Mechanics

1. **Two Mercenary slots:**
   - **Hired Mercenary** — fights alongside the player constantly; has a fully fleshed-out skill tree with skills and passives.
   - **Reinforcement Mercenary** — summoned on command in tag-team style; has an abbreviated skill tree.

2. **Downed state:** Mercenaries can "die" by taking a knee and resting. While downed they cannot use skills. Players can rush to a downed Mercenary and revive them (similar to reviving a co-op player).

3. **Rapport:** Primary progression currency for each Mercenary. Used to unlock Skill Points, abilities, caches, and bartering options.

4. **Rapport tiers — key unlocks:**

   | Tier | Reward |
   |------|--------|
   | 1–4 | Skill Points |
   | 5 | Bartering Upgrade + Pale Mark |
   | 6 | Gear Cache + Pale Mark |
   | 8 | Bartering Upgrade (second expansion) |
   | 9 | Boss Materials Cache + Pale Mark |
   | 10+ | Repeatable — each completion earns additional Pale Marks |

5. **Pale Marks:** Currency earned at Rapport milestones. Spent at vendor **Fayira** in The Den via a bartering system.

6. **Mercenary-related events** appear throughout Sanctuary and award bonus Rapport with the current companion.

7. **Per-Mercenary key passives and skills:**

   | Mercenary | Key Passives / Skills | Build Synergy |
   |-----------|----------------------|---------------|
   | Raheir | Raheir's Aegis, Raheir's Guard, Raheir's Inspiration, Valiance, Bastion, Ground Slam | Inspiration has high uptime via Ground Slam + Bastion triggers |
   | Subo | Scorched Earth, Bargaining Chips, Opening Fire, Seeker | DoT / Overpower / Crit Strike Damage builds |
   | Aldkin | Blasphemous Fate, Field of Languish (Shadow path), Raging Havoc (Fire path) | Vulnerable application; AoE Damage Reduction + Damage buff |
   | Varyana | Massacre, Bloodthirst, Hysteria, No Escape, Ancient Harpoons | Kill-streak movement speed; enemy grouping |

8. **Blasphemous Fate (Aldkin):** Makes the player and nearby enemies Vulnerable **or** drains 8% Maximum Resource per second, depending on Curse type selected.

9. **Bartering unlocks by Mercenary:**

   | Mercenary | Aspect Type | Cache Type |
   |-----------|-------------|------------|
   | Raheir | Defensive Aspects | Crafting Material Cache |
   | Subo | Utility Aspects | Boss Summoning Material Cache |
   | Aldkin | Resource Aspects | Augmentations Cache (Runes, Scattered Prisms, Gem Fragments) |
   | Varyana | Mobility Aspects | Masterworking Cache |

## Costs / requirements

- **Vessel of Hatred expansion** required — all four Mercenaries are gated behind it.
- **Raheir:** Unlocked automatically after completing the Vessel of Hatred campaign.
- **Subo, Aldkin, Varyana:** Unlocked via key quests given through The Den.
- **Pale Marks:** Earned at Rapport Tiers 5, 6, 9, 10+ — spent at Fayira for bartering.
- **Fayira restocking:** Costs Pale Marks (100 Pale Marks per restock per the structural data).

|-------------|---------|

## Strategy

1. **Survivability priority:** Use **Raheir** as Hired Mercenary when pushing into Torment IV or needing Armor/Resistance buffs — his CC, shield blocking, Damage Boost, and Valiance/Bastion tools shore up defensive gaps.
2. **Damage build matching:** Slot **Subo** based on your damage type — Scorched Earth for DoT, Bargaining Chips for Overpower, Opening Fire for Crit builds. His Seeker passive provides a free maphack in all overworld content.
3. **Farming efficiency:** **Varyana** excels in high-density areas. Pair No Escape or Ancient Harpoons to group enemies and sustain her Massacre kill-streak movement speed buff.
4. **Caster support:** **Aldkin** adds Crowd Control, high DPS, and Resource Generation. Use the Shadow path (Field of Languish) for tanky content needing Damage Reduction; use the Fire path (Raging Havoc) when raw damage output is the priority.
5. **Rapport grind:** Complete Mercenary-related events throughout Sanctuary for bonus Rapport. Prioritize reaching Tier 5 and Tier 8 quickly to unlock bartering expansions and Pale Mark income.
6. **Reinforcement slot:** Place whichever Mercenary you want on-demand burst from into the Reinforcement slot — their abbreviated tree still provides meaningful impact without constant uptime.
7. **Pale Mark farming:** After reaching Rapport Tier 10, repeatedly re-complete Tier 10 milestones to accumulate Pale Marks for sustained bartering at Fayira.

## Pitfalls

- **Subo:** Effectiveness drops sharply in low mob-density content (Mob Density Reliance). His Knock Back can scatter packs and his kit has No Defensive Option.
- **Aldkin:** Knock Back disrupts positioning. AoE Placement Dependency means Field of Languish can be wasted if enemies move. Blasphemous Fate makes the **player** Vulnerable, not just enemies — this can be dangerous in high Torment content.
- **Aldkin kit coherence:** His kit is noted as poorly mixed; not all skills synergize cleanly with each other.
- **Varyana:** Attack Speed buffs (Bloodthirst, Hysteria) are inconsistent — dependent on Varyana actively hitting targets. Her grouping is limited to 3 targets, capping value in spread encounters.
- **Mercenary downed:** A downed Mercenary loses all skill contribution until revived. In solo play, prioritize keeping them alive rather than ignoring the revive prompt.
- **Bartering gating:** Fayira's vendor options do not expand until Rapport Tier 5 and Tier 8 — do not expect early access to high-value Aspects or Caches.
- **Expansion requirement:** The entire system is locked behind Vessel of Hatred — no Mercenaries, no Den, no Rapport progression on base-game-only accounts.
