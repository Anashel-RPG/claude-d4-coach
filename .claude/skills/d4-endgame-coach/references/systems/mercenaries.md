# Mercenaries

> Season: 13 · Patch: 2.4.1 · Last refreshed: 2026-05-20 · Source: https://maxroll.gg/d4/resources/mercenaries-overview

## What it is

Mercenaries are a companion system introduced in the **Vessel of Hatred** expansion (Season 13, Patch 2.4.1). Players equip two mercenaries simultaneously:

| Slot | Name | Behavior |
|---|---|---|
| **Hired Mercenary** | Any of the four | Fights alongside the player constantly |
| **Reinforcement Mercenary** | Any of the four | Summoned on command in tag-team style; has an abbreviated skill tree |

The four available mercenaries are **Raheir**, **Subo**, **Aldkin**, and **Varyana**. All are managed from **The Den**, the mercenary hideout.

---

## Mechanics

1. **Two slots** — one Hired (always active) and one Reinforcement (summoned on demand).
2. **Downed state** — when a mercenary takes too much damage they "take a knee" and cannot use skills. Rush to them and revive manually, similar to reviving a co-op player.
3. **Rapport** — primary progression currency per mercenary. Gains come from normal play and from mercenary-related events scattered across Sanctuary (bonus Rapport with current companion).
4. **Rapport tiers unlock** — Skill Points (Tiers 1–4), a Gear Cache + Pale Mark (Tier 6), and expanded bartering options at Fayira (Tiers 5 and 8).
5. **Pale Marks** — earned by accumulating Rapport; spent at vendor **Fayira** in The Den via bartering.
6. **Bartering** — each mercenary unlocks a unique Aspect category and material cache type (see Recipes section). Fayira's inventory expands at Rapport Tier 5 and Tier 8 per mercenary.
7. **Mercenary skill trees** — invested via Skill Points unlocked through Rapport tiers.

## Strategy

1. **Raheir** — use when progressing toward Torment IV or whenever you need extra Armor/Resistance. His CC, survivability, and Armor/Resistance buff cover defensive gaps. Ground Slam + Bastion keep Raheir's Inspiration at high uptime.
2. **Subo** — best for overworld farming; Seeker passively reveals all enemies and materials. Match his key passive to your build: Scorched Earth (DoT), Bargaining Chips (Overpower), or Opening Fire (Crit Damage). Note his Cooldown Reduction on kill.
3. **Aldkin** — high DPS, CC, and Resource Generation. Run Shadow path (Field of Languish) for Damage Reduction + Damage buff in tight AoE; run Fire path (Raging Havoc) for single-target Damage buff. Be aware Blasphemous Fate makes both you and nearby enemies Vulnerable.
4. **Varyana** — prioritize high-density areas to maximize her kit. Use No Escape and Ancient Harpoons to group enemies before her melee burst. Massacre's stacking Movement Speed makes her particularly strong for farming runs.
5. **Reinforcement slot** — place whichever mercenary's abbreviated tree best complements your Hired choice (e.g., pair a defensive Hired Raheir with Subo's Reinforcement for Cooldown Reduction on kill).
6. **Rapport efficiency** — complete mercenary-related events in Sanctuary for bonus Rapport; prioritize reaching Tier 5 and Tier 8 on your main mercenary to unlock Fayira's full barter inventory.

## Pitfalls

- **Raheir:** Long Cooldowns and a tendency to scatter monsters limit his density value.
- **Subo:** Relies on mob density to perform; applies Knock Back (disrupts grouping); no defensive option for the player.
- **Aldkin:** Knock Back on skills disrupts positioning; AoE Placement Dependency means poor results on moving targets; Blasphemous Fate's Vulnerable also applies to the *player*; kit elements are poorly mixed between Fire and Shadow paths.
- **Varyana:** Attack Speed buffs (Bloodthirst and Hysteria) drop off immediately when she stops hitting — inconsistent against sparse or ranged packs. Enemy grouping caps at only 3 targets.
- **General:** A downed mercenary cannot use any skills until revived — in solo play this requires manually reaching them under pressure. Neglecting Rapport progression locks you out of Skill Points and expanded barter options.
