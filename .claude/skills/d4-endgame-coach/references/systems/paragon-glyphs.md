# Paragon Glyphs

> Season: 13 · Patch: 2.4.1 · Last refreshed: 2026-05-22 · Source: https://maxroll.gg/d4/resources/paragon-boards

## What it is

Paragon Glyphs are rune-like items socketed into **Glyph Socket Nodes** on Paragon Boards (one socket per board). They amplify nearby Paragon Nodes within a radius, grant a primary bonus, and—at Rare/Legendary quality—unlock a threshold-gated additional bonus when enough Attribute points are allocated within their radius.

Paragon Boards unlock at **character level 70**.

---

## Mechanics

1. **Socketing** — Each Paragon Board has exactly one Glyph Socket Node. Drop a Glyph in to activate its effects.
2. **Radius** — Glyphs start with a radius of **3** nodes. All Paragon Nodes (Normal, Magic, Rare) within that radius contribute Attributes to the Glyph's scaling.
3. **Attribute threshold bonus** — Rare and Legendary Glyphs grant an additional bonus when allocated Paragon Nodes within the radius meet a specified Attribute total. Example: Fulminate requires **25 Dexterity** on nodes within its radius to unlock its extra bonus.
4. **Quality tiers** — Glyphs start at **Rare** quality. At level 50 they convert to **Legendary** quality, activating a Legendary Bonus multiplier.
5. **Scaling** — Glyph bonuses scale with level. Example: Fulminate grants **+2.0% increased Lightning damage per 5 Dexterity** allocated in its radius at base level.
6. **Radius expansion** — At **level 50**, the Glyph's radius expands from **3 → 5**, affecting more surrounding nodes.
7. **Leveling** — Glyphs are leveled by completing **the Pit**. Clearing the Pit grants upgrade attempts; success chance scales with the difference between Pit tier and Glyph level (see Breakpoints).
8. **Drop source** — After level 70, any activity can drop a Glyph. The **Pit** and **Nightmare Dungeons** are the best sources.

## Strategy

1. **Farm the Pit** — It is the primary source for both Glyph drops and upgrade attempts. Prioritize it over other activities once level 70 is reached.
2. **Target +10 Pit level over Glyph level** — Run a Pit tier at least 10 levels above your current Glyph level for guaranteed 100% upgrade success. Efficient when catching up early Glyph levels.
3. **Aim for the +20 overshoot bonus** — When pushing Pit tiers you can handle, keeping 20+ levels above key Glyphs grants a free +3 bonus upgrade per successful attempt.
4. **Prioritize reaching level 50** — The Rare → Legendary conversion at level 50 is the single largest power spike: radius expands from 3 to 5 and the Legendary Bonus activates.
5. **Plan Attribute layout around Glyph thresholds** — Pack the appropriate Attribute nodes (Normal Nodes grant +5 each) within the Glyph's radius to meet thresholds. Example: cluster Dexterity nodes near Fulminate for its 25 Dex threshold.
6. **Go deathless in Pit runs** — No-death completion adds a free upgrade attempt per run (up to +1), and combined with a Seasonal Blessing can yield 5 attempts per clear.
7. **Use Nightmare Dungeons as a secondary source** — Good for acquiring new Glyph drops when you need a specific Glyph before committing Pit attempts.

---

## Pitfalls

- **Wasting attempts on low-delta runs** — Running a Pit tier equal to or below your Glyph level gives ≤55% success, burning attempts inefficiently. Target +10 for 100% or accept the trade-off consciously.
- **Skipping the level-50 Gem Fragment cost** — Forgetting to stockpile 15,000 Gem Fragments delays the Rare → Legendary conversion and the radius expansion that comes with it.
- **Ignoring Attribute thresholds** — Socketing a Glyph without placing enough of the correct Attribute nodes within its radius leaves its threshold bonus permanently inactive.
- **Under-utilizing radius expansion** — Building your board node path assuming radius 3 may leave powerful Rare or Legendary Nodes just outside range; re-evaluate board layout at level 50 when radius becomes 5.
- **Over-investing in sub-optimal Pit tiers** — Pushing far beyond +20 over Glyph level wastes Pit clears that could be used on other Glyphs; distribute attempts across all Glyphs you want leveled.
- **Dying in Pit runs** — Losing the no-death bonus costs one upgrade attempt per run—meaningful over many clears.
