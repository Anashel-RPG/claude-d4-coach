# Paragon Glyphs

> Season: 13 · Patch: 2.4.1 · Last refreshed: 2026-05-24 · Source: https://maxroll.gg/d4/resources/paragon-boards

## What it is

Paragon Glyphs are rune-like items slotted into the single **Glyph Socket Node** on each Paragon Board. They amplify nearby Paragon Nodes within a radius, scale in power as they level up, and convert from Rare to Legendary quality at level 50.

- Unlock condition: character level 70 (applies to all classes)
- One Glyph per board; each character can run up to 5 boards
- Start at Rare quality; become Legendary at level 50

## Mechanics
1. **Radius** — Glyphs start with a radius of 3, affecting all Paragon Nodes within 3 nodes of the socket. Radius expands to 5 at level 50.
2. **Attribute threshold bonus** — Rare and Legendary Glyphs grant an additional bonus when allocated Paragon Nodes inside the radius meet a specified Attribute threshold.
3. **Scaling** — Glyph bonuses increase as the Glyph levels up (max level 150).
4. **Legendary conversion** — At level 50 the Glyph upgrades from Rare to Legendary quality, activating a Legendary Bonus multiplier.
5. **Class-specific Glyphs** — Many Glyphs are exclusive to a single class (see class lists below); some (e.g. Exploit, Revenge) appear on multiple class lists.
6. **Example — Fulminate (Spiritborn):**
   - Base: +1.0% increased Lightning damage per 5 Dexterity allocated within radius
   - Additional bonus requires **25 Dexterity** on allocated Paragon Nodes within radius
   - Synergizes with Intelligence nodes (scales further at higher Glyph levels)

### Fulminate interaction

- Requires 25 Dexterity from allocated Paragon Nodes within radius to activate additional bonus.
- Base rate: +1.0% Lightning damage per 5 Dexterity in radius.
- Further scales with Intelligence at higher Glyph levels.

## Strategy
1. **Target Pit levels near +10 above your Glyph level** for guaranteed 100% upgrade chance — the most efficient path when pushing a fresh Glyph.
2. **Never die in the Pit** — the no-death +1 attempt is free and adds up significantly across many runs.
3. **Activate Seasonal Blessings** that grant extra upgrade attempts before committing to heavy Glyph-leveling sessions.
4. **Prioritize Legendary conversion at level 50** — the Legendary Bonus multiplier is a major power spike; budget 15,000 Gem Fragments per Glyph.
5. **Stack the right Attribute on nearby nodes** before socketing — Glyphs with Attribute threshold bonuses (e.g. Fulminate needing 25 Dexterity) require deliberate node routing within the radius.
6. **Use Nightmare Dungeons early** to farm Glyph drops before pushing the Pit; both activities are the best sources post-level 70.
7. **Plan radius expansion** — at level 50 the radius grows from 3 to 5, pulling in more nodes; route your board path accordingly so the extra nodes are high-value (Rare Nodes, Legendary Node).

## Pitfalls
- **Wasting attempts on too-low Pit tiers** — running a Pit 40+ levels below your Glyph level gives only 1–2% chance per attempt; find the closest tier where you can still kill the boss in time.
- **Dying negates the no-death bonus** — a single death costs you an upgrade attempt every run.
- **Skipping the Gem Fragment budget** — neglecting to stockpile 15,000 Gem Fragments per Glyph will stall Legendary conversions at level 50.
- **Ignoring Attribute thresholds in radius** — socketing a Glyph without enough of the required Attribute on nearby nodes means the threshold bonus never activates.
- **Not expanding path toward the Legendary Node** — after radius increases to 5 at level 50, nodes that were previously out of range become relevant; a poorly routed board wastes that expansion.
- **Assuming Glyphs are universal** — many top Glyphs are class-specific; check the class list before planning a build around one.
