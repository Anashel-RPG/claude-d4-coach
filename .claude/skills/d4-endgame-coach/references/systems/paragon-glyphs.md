# Paragon Glyphs

> Season: 13 · Patch: 2.4.1 · Last refreshed: 2026-05-23 · Source: https://maxroll.gg/d4/resources/paragon-boards

## What it is

Paragon Glyphs are rune-like items socketed into **Glyph Socket Nodes** on Paragon Boards (one socket per board). They amplify nearby Paragon Nodes within a set radius, and Rare/Legendary Glyphs unlock additional bonuses when an Attribute threshold is met by allocated nodes in that radius. At level 50 a Glyph converts from Rare to Legendary quality, gaining an extra bonus multiplier.

Paragon Boards unlock at **character level 70** (all classes).

---

## Mechanics
1. **Placement** — Each Paragon Board has exactly one Glyph Socket Node. Slot one Glyph per board.
2. **Radius** — Glyphs start with a radius of **3** (affects all Paragon Nodes within 3 nodes of the socket). Radius expands to **5** at Glyph level 50.
3. **Attribute threshold bonus** — Rare and Legendary Glyphs grant an additional bonus when allocated Paragon Nodes within the radius meet a specified Attribute total.
4. **Quality tiers** — Glyphs start as Rare. At level 50 they become **Legendary**, activating a Legendary Bonus multiplier.
5. **Leveling** — Upgraded exclusively via the Pit. Clearing the Pit grants upgrade attempts; success chance scales with the difference between Pit level and Glyph level (see breakpoints below).
6. **Maximum level** — Glyphs cap at **level 150**.
7. **Bonus levels** — When Pit level is more than 20 levels above Glyph level, the Glyph receives an additional **+3** upgrade (shown in green).

## Strategy
1. **Farm Glyphs in the Pit or Nightmare Dungeons** — these are the highest-density sources post-level 70.
2. **Don't die in the Pit** — preserving the no-death bonus gives a free +1 upgrade attempt per run.
3. **Run Pit levels ~10 above your target Glyph level** for guaranteed 100% upgrade success and no wasted attempts.
4. **Push 20+ levels above Glyph level** when possible to claim the +3 bonus upgrade per clear and accelerate leveling.
5. **Prioritize Glyph level 50 first** — radius expansion (3→5) and Legendary quality conversion dramatically increase board power; budget 15,000 Gem Fragments for the conversion cost.
6. **Stack the correct Attribute within Glyph radius** — place boards so Normal Nodes of the required Attribute type fall inside radius 3 (or 5 post-50) to consistently meet threshold bonuses.
7. **Activate Seasonal Blessings** that grant +1 Glyph upgrade attempt to maximize attempts per Pit run.

---

## Pitfalls
- **Running Pit too far below Glyph level** — at −40 or worse you have ≤2% chance; you'll burn attempts for almost no progress.
- **Forgetting the Gem Fragment cost** — converting a Glyph to Legendary at level 50 costs 15,000 Gem Fragments; running low stalls progression on multiple boards simultaneously.
- **Ignoring radius when building boards** — nodes outside the radius (3 or 5) contribute nothing to Glyph bonuses; poor board layout wastes Attribute allocation.
- **Missing the Attribute threshold** — Rare/Legendary Glyph bonus does not activate unless the exact Attribute requirement is met by nodes inside the radius; always verify threshold is reached.
- **Dying in the Pit** — costs 1 upgrade attempt per run; avoidable with appropriate Pit tier selection.
- **Over-pushing Pit tier** — dying to clear a higher tier loses the no-death attempt and may still fail the upgrade if the level gap isn't at least +10; sometimes clearing a lower tier at 100% is faster net XP for Glyphs.
