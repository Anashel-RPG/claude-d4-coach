# Paragon Glyphs

> Season: 13 · Patch: 2.4.1 · Last refreshed: 2026-05-25 · Source: https://maxroll.gg/d4/resources/paragon-boards

## What it is

Paragon Glyphs are rune-like items socketed into Glyph Socket Nodes on Paragon Boards. Each board has exactly one Glyph Socket. Glyphs amplify nearby Paragon Nodes within their radius and unlock additional bonuses when Attribute thresholds are met by allocated nodes inside that radius. Available from level 70 onward.

---

## Mechanics
1. **Socketing** — Place a Glyph into a Glyph Socket Node on any unlocked Paragon Board.
2. **Radius** — Glyphs start with a radius of 3 (affects all Paragon Nodes within 3 nodes of the socket). Radius expands to 5 at Glyph level 50.
3. **Attribute threshold bonus** — Rare and Legendary Glyphs grant an additional bonus when allocated Paragon Nodes within the radius meet the specified Attribute requirement (e.g., Fulminate requires 25 Dexterity on nearby nodes).
4. **Quality tiers** — Glyphs start at Rare quality. At level 50 they convert to Legendary quality, gaining a Legendary Bonus multiplier and costing 15,000 Gem Fragments to complete the upgrade.
5. **Level cap** — Maximum Glyph level is 150.
6. **Scaling** — Glyph bonuses scale with level (example: Fulminate grants +1.0% increased Lightning damage per 5 Dexterity in radius at base level).
7. **Bonus upgrade** — When the Pit level exceeds the Glyph level by more than 20, the Glyph receives an additional +3 levels (shown in green).

## Strategy
1. **Push Pit levels to maximize upgrade efficiency** — aim for Pit level at least 10 above your target Glyph level for guaranteed upgrades; +20 for the free +3 bonus.
2. **Prioritize the Glyphs you socket most** — each board has one socket, so the Glyph in each slot scales that entire board. Upgrade your highest-impact Glyphs first.
3. **Stack the correct Attribute nodes in radius** — pack Normal Nodes granting the required Attribute (e.g., Dexterity for Fulminate) within the Glyph's radius to unlock and maximize the threshold bonus.
4. **Plan for the level-50 conversion** — stockpile 15,000 Gem Fragments before a Glyph reaches level 50 to immediately convert it to Legendary quality.
5. **Take no-death runs seriously** — surviving a Pit run without dying nets a free extra upgrade attempt, compounding efficiency over many runs.
6. **Use Seasonal Blessings** — in Season 13, the relevant Blessing adds a fifth upgrade attempt per run; equip it while actively leveling Glyphs.
7. **Farm Glyphs in Pit and Nightmare Dungeons** — these are the best drop sources post-level 70 for acquiring new Glyphs.

---

## Pitfalls
- **Upgrading at too large a level deficit** — running a Pit tier 40+ levels below your Glyph level yields only 1–2% success chance; a waste of attempts.
- **Ignoring the Attribute threshold** — socketing a Glyph without enough of the required Attribute on nearby nodes loses the conditional bonus entirely.
- **Forgetting Gem Fragments** — the Rare → Legendary conversion at level 50 costs 15,000 Gem Fragments; running dry stalls progression.
- **Missing the boss timer** — killing the Pit boss after the timer expires does not grant upgrade attempts; the run is wasted for Glyph leveling purposes.
- **Neglecting the no-death bonus** — dying once per run forfeits the +1 attempt, meaningfully reducing efficiency over a session.
- **Mismatching class-specific Glyphs** — each class has its own pool of Glyphs; equipping a Glyph outside your class's pool is not possible, so plan board layouts around your class list (see below).
