# Horadric Cube

> Season: 13 · Patch: 2.4.1 · Last refreshed: 2026-05-20 · Source: https://maxroll.gg/d4/resources/horadric-cube

## What it is

The **Horadric Cube** is a crafting station that returns in Diablo IV with the **Lord of Hatred** expansion (Season 13, Patch 2.4.1). It is unlocked by completing the Lord of Hatred campaign and is thereafter located in **Temis**. It accepts combinations of materials and items to transmute, upgrade, or modify equipment, gems, runes, and sigils.

---

## Mechanics

1. Each recipe requires specific **Primordial Dust** variants (the tier of dust determines the recipe type) plus a quantity of **Raw Primordial Dust** as a secondary cost.
2. **Tuning Prisms** are optional (or mandatory for Focused Reroll) modifiers that narrow random outcomes to a specific affix category — available variants: Aggressive, Pragmatic, Protector's, Resourceful, Adept's, Chromatic (affix-category prisms) and Kullean, Entropic (Transfiguration-outcome prisms).
3. The **Amalgamation** recipe works at a fixed **5-to-1** ratio and is the only way to upgrade GrandGems → Horadric Gems → Flawless Horadric Gems.
4. **Transfiguration** has a 15/16 chance to make the output item **unmodifiable**; 2H Weapons receive double affix ranges and quality 2–30 vs. 1–15 for other item types.
5. **Legendary Rune crafting** consumes 1× specific Legendary Rune input + 5× any Rare Runes + 5× any Legendary Runes to produce 1× new Legendary Rune output.
6. **Recycle Uniques** requires 3× identical Unique/Mythic Unique items or Unique Charms and produces a new item of that type.
7. **3-to-1 Transmutation** converts 3× same Rune into 1× random new Rune of that type; using Ancestral inputs guarantees an Ancestral output.

## Recipes / breakpoints / interactions

**Affix manipulation**

| Recipe | Effect |
|---|---|
| Add Affix | Adds a random affix; Tuning Prism narrows to one affix category |
| Remove Affix | Removes a random affix |
| Chaotic Reroll | Changes a random affix to one from a *different* category; Tuning Prism can steer category |
| Focused Reroll | Changes an affix to another within the *same* category; Tuning Prism is **mandatory** to define the category |

**Item transformation**

| Recipe | Effect |
|---|---|
| Upgrade to Legendary | Transmutes 1× Rare → Legendary with a random Legendary power |
| Upgrade to Unique | Transmutes 1× item → random Unique of the same type; Ancestral input → Ancestral Unique output |
| Transfiguration | Transmutes 1× Legendary/Unique/Mythic Unique; 15/16 chance output becomes **unmodifiable**; Kullean Prism imprints random Utility Aspect on Amulets; Entropic Prism steers outcome; 2H Weapons get quality 2–30 and doubled affix ranges |
| Unique Power Reroll | Randomizes the value of the Unique Power on an Ancestral Unique |
| Recycle Uniques | 3× identical Unique/Mythic Unique or Unique Charm → 1× new item of that type |

**Amalgamation (5-to-1)**

| Input | Output |
|---|---|
| 5× same GrandGem | 1× Horadric Gem (same type) |
| 5× same Horadric Gem | 1× Flawless Horadric Gem (same type) |

## Strategy

1. **Plan before crafting** — use an external path-planner (e.g., Prism Pathfinder) to map the optimal sequence of Add Affix / Remove Affix / Focused Reroll operations before spending materials; wrong order wastes significant Raw Primordial Dust.
2. **Use Focused Reroll with the correct Tuning Prism** — always match the Tuning Prism type (Aggressive, Pragmatic, etc.) to the affix category you want; Focused Reroll without a Prism is not possible.
3. **Save Chaotic Reroll for category swaps only** — it is strictly worse than Focused Reroll when you want to stay in-category; use it only when you specifically need to escape an unwanted affix category.
4. **Transfigure on 2H Weapons for maximum value** — the doubled affix ranges and extended quality roll (2–30 vs. 1–15) make this slot the highest-upside Transfiguration target.
5. **Use Amalgamation to convert excess rune stock** — 5× any Rare Runes → 1× random Legendary Rune; batch-convert surplus before targeting specific rune crafts.
6. **Ancestral inputs for Ancestral outputs** — feeding Ancestral items into Upgrade to Unique or 3-to-1 Transmutation guarantees an Ancestral result.
7. **Kullean Prism on Amulets** — if your amulet rolled poor stats and is non-unmodifiable, Transfiguration with a Kullean Tuning Prism can imprint a random Utility Aspect as a recovery option.

---

## Pitfalls

- **Transfiguration has a 15/16 chance to lock the item** — do not Transfigure an item you want to further modify afterward; sequence this last.
- **Focused Reroll requires a Tuning Prism** — attempting it without the correct prism is not possible; budget prisms alongside dust.
- **Remove Affix is random** — it does not let you select which affix is removed; save before using if your platform allows, or plan around losing any single affix.
- **Unique Power Reroll costs 100× Raw Primordial Dust** — the highest Raw Dust cost of any recipe; only use on a near-perfect Ancestral Unique where only the power value needs improvement.
- **Legendary Rune crafting burns Legendary Runes as currency** — each craft consumes 5× any Legendary Runes in addition to the specific input rune; maintain a surplus before targeting a specific output.
- **Amalgamation is the sole gem upgrade path** — there is no other way to obtain Flawless Horadric Gems; do not salvage or vendor surplus lower-tier gems.
- **Crafting Unique Charms destroys an Ancestral Unique** — confirm the Ancestral Unique being consumed is intentional before committing.
