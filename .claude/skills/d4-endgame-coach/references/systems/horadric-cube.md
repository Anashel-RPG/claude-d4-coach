# Horadric Cube

> Season: 13 · Patch: 2.4.1 · Last refreshed: 2026-05-25 · Source: https://maxroll.gg/d4/resources/horadric-cube

## What it is

The **Horadric Cube** is a crafting station that returns in Diablo IV with the **Lord of Hatred** expansion (Season 13, Patch 2.4.1). It provides a suite of transmutation recipes for modifying, upgrading, and converting items, gems, runes, and sigils. After unlocking it via the Lord of Hatred campaign, the Cube is permanently accessible in **Temis**.

---

## Mechanics

1. All recipes consume **Primordial Dust** (various tiers) and optionally **Tuning Prisms** to influence outcomes.
2. **Tuning Prisms** are optional in most recipes but **mandatory** for Focused Reroll (determines affix category).
3. Transfiguration makes an item **Unmodifiable** ~15/16 times after the recipe fires; only gem/rune swaps remain possible.
4. Two-handed weapons receive **doubled affix ranges and 2–30 quality** from Transfiguration (vs. 1–15 for other slots).
5. The Amalgamation recipe is the **only way** to upgrade GrandGems → Horadric Gems → Flawless Horadric Gems.
6. Legendary Rune crafting recipes each require a **specific Legendary Rune input** plus 5× any Rare Runes and 5× any Legendary Runes.
7. The Prism Pathfinder tool can map deterministic crafting paths through the Cube to minimize material waste.

## Strategy

1. **Use Focused Reroll with the correct Tuning Prism** to lock affix changes within a desired category — this is significantly more material-efficient than Chaotic Reroll.
2. **Transfigure last.** Items become Unmodifiable ~15/16 of the time; finish all other modifications before running Transfiguration.
3. **Use Kullean Tuning Prism on Amulets before Transfiguring** — it imprints a random Utility Aspect on the item while it can still be modified.
4. **Use Entropic Tuning Prism with Transfiguration** to guarantee a useful result (~60% Bonus Affix / ~40% Bonus Quality) and exclude undesirable Indestructible/Replace/Greater Affix outcomes.
5. **Two-handed weapons are Transfiguration priority targets** — they receive doubled affix ranges and 2–30 quality vs. 1–15 for all other slots.
6. **Use Amalgamation to build up gem tiers** — it is the only path from GrandGem → Horadric Gem → Flawless Horadric Gem.
7. **Use Prism Pathfinder** to pre-plan reroll sequences and avoid wasting Refined Primordial Dust on sub-optimal crafting orders.
8. **For Legendary Rune targets**, stockpile 5× any Rare and 5× any Legendary Runes before crafting; the specific input Legendary Rune is also consumed.

---

## Pitfalls

- **Transfiguring too early** — if the item becomes Unmodifiable before you've finished affix work, all further modification is locked out.
- **Focused Reroll without a Tuning Prism** is not possible — the Prism is mandatory, not optional.
- **Remove Affix is random** — it does not target the affix you select; use a Tuning Prism to narrow the pool.
- **Recycle Uniques requires 3× identical items** — mixing different Uniques or mixing equipment with charms will not satisfy the recipe.
- **Legendary Rune crafting burns 5× any Legendary Runes as fuel** — this is expensive; do not craft blindly if you need those runes for runewords.
- **Unique Power Reroll costs 100× Raw Primordial Dust** — the highest Raw Dust cost of any recipe; only use on near-perfect Ancestral Uniques.
- **Amalgamation is the only gem upgrade path** — attempting to upgrade gems through any other system will fail.
- **Reroll Set Charm requires Infused Horadric Resin (50×)** in addition to Raw Dust — a secondary material many players overlook.
