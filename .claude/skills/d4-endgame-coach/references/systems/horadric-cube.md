# Horadric Cube

> Season: 13 · Patch: 2.4.1 · Last refreshed: 2026-05-19 · Source: https://maxroll.gg/d4/resources/horadric-cube

## What it is

The Horadric Cube is a crafting station that returns in Diablo IV with the **Lord of Hatred** expansion (Season 13, Patch 2.4.1). It accepts items and materials as inputs and transmutes them into new or modified outputs. After unlocking it via the Lord of Hatred campaign, it is located in **Temis**.

---

## Mechanics

1. Insert items and materials into the Cube and transmute to apply the selected recipe.
2. Most recipes produce random outcomes; adding a **Tuning Prism** narrows or steers the result.
3. **Focused Reroll** is the only recipe that *requires* a Tuning Prism — all others accept it optionally.
4. **Transfiguration** carries a chance to make the output item **unmodifiable**; 2H Weapons receive double affix ranges and 2–30 quality (vs. 1–15 for other slots).
5. **Amalgamation** is the only way to upgrade GrandGems → Horadric Gems → Flawless Horadric Gems (5:1 ratio each step).
6. Legendary Rune crafting requires a specific named Legendary Rune input plus bulk Rare and Legendary Runes as fuel.
7. Using Ancestral-tier inputs on eligible recipes guarantees Ancestral-tier outputs.

---

## Costs / requirements

- Unlocked by completing the **Lord of Hatred campaign**; found in **Temis** afterwards.
- Core materials: variants of **Primordial Dust** and **Tuning Prisms**.

**Primordial Dust variants used across recipes:**

| Variant | Recipes that consume it |
|---|---|
| Raw Primordial Dust | Almost all recipes (see table below) |
| Coarse Primordial Dust | Add Affix |
| Refined Primordial Dust | Chaotic Reroll, Focused Reroll, Remove Affix |
| Volatile Primordial Dust | Transfiguration |
| Attuned Primordial Dust | Unique Power Reroll |
| Pure Primordial Dust | Upgrade to Legendary |
| Enhanced Primordial Dust | Upgrade to Unique, Craft Unique Charm |

**Tuning Prism variants:**

| Prism | Category steered |
|---|---|
| Aggressive | Offensive affixes |
| Pragmatic | Utility affixes |
| Protector's | Defensive affixes |
| Resourceful | Resource affixes |
| Adept's | Mainstat / skill ranks |
| Chromatic | Resistances only |
| Entropic | Transfiguration outcomes |
| Kullean | Transfiguration — imprints random Utility Aspect on Amulets only |

## Strategy

1. **Plan your crafting path before starting.** Some operations (especially Transfiguration) can render an item permanently unmodifiable; map safe sequences first. The community tool **Prism Pathfinder** is purpose-built to find optimal Horadric Cube crafting paths and can save significant materials.
2. **Always use a Tuning Prism on Focused Reroll** — it is mandatory and determines which affix category is rerolled. Select the correct Prism variant for the stat category you want.
3. **Add a Tuning Prism to Add Affix and Chaotic Reroll** to narrow the random pool and avoid wasting Raw Primordial Dust on unwanted affixes.
4. **Use Kullean Tuning Prism on Amulets during Transfiguration** to imprint a random Utility Aspect — this is the only slot/prism combination that enables that outcome.
5. **Stack Amalgamation runs early** to convert bulk GrandGems → Horadric Gems → Flawless Horadric Gems; this is the only upgrade path for gems.
6. **Save Ancestral inputs for Upgrade to Unique** — Ancestral inputs guarantee Ancestral Unique outputs, giving you the highest-tier base to work with.
7. **Hoard Raw Primordial Dust** — it is consumed by nearly every recipe, with Unique Power Reroll costing 100× per use.

---

## Pitfalls

- **Transfiguration makes items unmodifiable** in 15 out of 16 outcomes — do not run this on a partially-crafted item unless you are happy with its current affixes.
- **Focused Reroll requires a Tuning Prism** — attempting it without one is not possible; budget Prisms accordingly.
- **Chaotic Reroll can change any affix to any other category** — without a Tuning Prism you may lose a well-rolled affix to an unwanted category entirely.
- **Remove Affix is random** — it does not let you choose which affix is deleted; use only when any affix on the item is acceptable to lose.
- **Legendary Rune crafting consumes 5× Rare + 5× Legendary Runes as bulk fuel** on top of the specific named input rune — this is expensive if you are low on Legendary Runes.
- **Unique Power Reroll costs 100× Raw Primordial Dust per cast** — confirm the Unique Power roll range is meaningfully impactful before spending.
- **Craft Unique Charm destroys the Ancestral Unique input** — do not use a BIS (best-in-slot) item you intend to equip.
- **Amalgamation is the only gem upgrade path** — there is no alternative; do not salvage or vendor GrandGems or Horadric Gems.
