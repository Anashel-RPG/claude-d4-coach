# Tempering

> Season: 13 · Patch: 2.4.1 · Last refreshed: 2026-05-18 · Source: https://maxroll.gg/d4/resources/tempering-guide

## What it is

Tempering adds **one extra deterministic affix** to Rare, Legendary, Unique, and Mythic Unique equipment. You choose the affix category and the specific affix in advance; only the rolled value is random. Performed at the **Blacksmith**. (Season 13, Patch 2.4.1 — affix-selection behavior introduced in Season 11.)

---

## Mechanics

1. Visit the Blacksmith, select a piece of gear, choose a learned Tempering Recipe, and supply materials.
2. Each item accepts **one** Tempering Affix slot.
3. After applying, the affix can be **rerolled** up to **3 times** by default (charges per item, not per session).
4. Each **Greater Affix already on the item** grants **+1 additional reroll charge** (no stated cap beyond base math).
5. When rerolling, the previous value is **discarded** — a good roll can be lost.
6. Every application or reroll has a small chance to produce a **Greater Affix** (+50% bonus to the value).
7. Once all charges are spent, a **Scroll of Restoration** resets them.
8. Tempering Affixes scale with **Masterworking** (+1% per rank alongside base affixes).

## Costs / requirements

**Material by item type (Ancestral Legendary cost shown):**

| Item Type | Primary Material | Qty (Ancestral Legendary) | Secondary Material | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Weapons / Off-hand | Abstruse Sigils | 25 | Forgotten Souls | 25 |
| Armor (Helm, Chest, Gloves, Pants, Boots, Shield) | Baleful Fragments | 25 | Forgotten Souls | 25 |
| Jewelry (Amulet, Ring) | Coiling Wards | 25 | Forgotten Souls | 25 |

- Cost scales with **Item Power** — higher Item Power = more materials.
- Cost is otherwise identical across item types of the same Item Power.

**Tempering Manuals (recipes):**

- Manuals are found randomly in the world; using one **permanently** teaches its recipe.
- Manual quality by difficulty:
  - **Magic** quality — Standard difficulties
  - **Rare** quality — Torment difficulties
  - **Legendary** quality — Torment difficulties
- Drop sources: Helltide, World Bosses, Nightmare Dungeons, Pit of Artificers.

## Recipes / breakpoints / interactions

**Six recipe categories and eligible slots:**

| Category | Eligible Slots |
|---|---|
| Weapons | Weapon, Off-hand |
| Offensive | Weapon, Off-hand, Amulet, Ring, Gloves |
| Defensive | Amulet, Helm, Chest Armor, Pants, Shield |
| Utility | Amulet, Helm, Chest Armor, Gloves, Pants, Boots, Shield |
| Mobility | Amulet, Boots |
| Resource | Amulet, Ring |

**Key interactions:**

- **Greater Affix on tempered result** → +50% to that affix's value.
- **Greater Affixes already on item** → each grants +1 reroll charge beyond the base 3.
- **Scroll of Restoration** → resets spent charges; obtainable from Dark Citadel, Infernal Hordes completion, World Bosses.
- **Masterworking** → improves Tempering Affix values alongside base affixes.

## Strategy

1. Confirm your build's required Tempering Affixes and categories **before** spending materials — each item only gets one slot.
2. Target Torment difficulties for **Rare/Legendary quality Manuals**, which contain more powerful affixes.
3. On high Item Power gear, temper last in the crafting order (after socketing); Masterwork after tempering to benefit from the increased value.
4. If low on Forgotten Souls, skip aggressive rerolling for Greater Affix tempers — the material cost per attempt is steep at high Item Power.
5. Use **Scrolls of Restoration** only after all base charges (including Greater Affix bonus charges) are exhausted.
6. Farm Helltide and Pit of Artificers as primary Manual sources; World Bosses supplement both Manuals and Scrolls of Restoration.
7. Boots offer both Mobility and Utility categories — pick whichever affix pool better serves your build, not just the category name.

---

## Pitfalls

- **Rerolling discards the previous result** — there is no "keep best" option; rolling past a strong value is permanent loss.
- **Charges are per-item and finite** — exhausting all 3 base charges without a Scroll of Restoration leaves the item locked to its last result.
- **Material confusion** — Baleful Fragments are for Armor, Coiling Wards for Jewelry, Abstruse Sigils for Weapons/Off-hand; stocking the wrong type wastes time.
- **Only one Tempering Affix per item** — choosing the wrong recipe category locks out all alternatives on that piece.
- **Forgotten Souls drain fast** — Ancestral Legendary pieces cost 25 Forgotten Souls per temper attempt regardless of item type; budget accordingly.
- **Greater Affix fishing on 900 Item Power gear** is expensive; rerolling repeatedly to chase a Greater Affix temper will rapidly deplete Forgotten Souls and gold.
