# Gems Runes

> Season: 13 · Patch: 2.4.1 · Last refreshed: 2026-05-19 · Source: https://maxroll.gg/d4/resources/runewords-overview

## What it is

Runewords are a socketing system exclusive to the **Vessel of Hatred** expansion (introduced Season 13, Patch 2.4.1). Each Runeword pairs one **Rune of Ritual** (condition/generator) with one **Rune of Invocation** (effect/trigger) socketed into the same item. Runes are distinct from Gems — you cannot socket both in the same item.

- 15 Runes of Ritual available
- 26 Runes of Invocation available
- New Invocation Runes **Kel** and **Zid** added in the Season 11 update (December 21, 2025)
- Runes are tradeable and stored in the **Socketables** tab

---

## Mechanics

1. **Offering** is the internal currency generated by the Rune of Ritual when its condition fires.
2. Once accumulated Offering meets the Rune of Invocation's threshold, the Invocation triggers its effect.
3. **Overflow** occurs when the Ritual rune generates more Offering than the Invocation requires. The surplus scales an additional bonus proportional to the Overflow amount.
4. More demanding Ritual conditions (harder to proc) generate higher Offering per trigger.
5. Runes of Invocation grant access to skills from **other classes** (e.g., a Barbarian using Frost Nova via Thul).
6. Each Runeword occupies **two sockets** in a single item — one socket for the Ritual rune, one for the Invocation rune.

## Costs / requirements

- **Expansion required:** Vessel of Hatred
- **Valid item slots:** Helm, Chest, Legs, Two-Handed Weapon (item must have 2 sockets)
- **Gems excluded:** Cannot equip Gems and Runes in the same item
- **Unique rune restriction:** Cannot use two copies of the same Rune across all Runewords on a character
- **Rune rarities:** Magic, Rare, Legendary — higher rarity = stronger effect
- **Mythic Unique crafting cost:** 2× Resplendent Spark + 6× Legendary Rune + 6× Rare Rune + 6× Magic Rune (specific names required per recipe)

### General Rune Crafting (Jeweler)
- Combine **3 runes of the same name** → receive **1 random rune**

### Overflow Breakpoints
- Overflow is proportional: the more surplus Offering, the larger the bonus
- Invocations with damage Overflow (Teb, Tzic): +1% damage per Offering unit over threshold
- Invocations with duration Overflow (Ner, Prid, Que, Qua, Yom, Wat): duration scales up
- Thul Overflow: increases Frost Nova **size**
- Vex Overflow: up to **+3 Skill ranks** (from base +1)
- Eom Overflow: further cooldown reduction beyond 0.1 s base

## Strategy

1. **Match Ritual output to Invocation cost.** Low-cost Invocations (Eom 100, Gar 25, Qua 50) pair with low-yield Rituals (Poc, Noc, Igni) for near-constant uptime; high-cost Invocations (Ohm 600, Ner 600) need high-burst Rituals (Zan 200, Neo 200, Cir 300) or fast-cycling ones.
2. **Exploit Overflow deliberately.** Pair a Ritual that generates well above the Invocation threshold to consistently land large Overflow bonuses — especially with Teb/Tzic for damage scaling or Vex for Skill rank stacking.
3. **BacJah for mobility builds.** Movement-heavy builds (Evade spam, repositioning) get free Teleport access without requiring a Sorcerer; replaces an Evade charge with Teleport on demand.
4. **Summon builds + Nagu.** Necromancer/Druid with 5 active summons proc Nagu for up to 500 Offering per 5 s interval — enough to chain high-cost Invocations like Yom or Kel.
5. **Cir for skill-rotation builds.** 300 Offering per 5-skill window with a 3 s downtime; pairs well with low-cost Invocations to chain multiple triggers per fight.
6. **Plan rune uniqueness.** If running multiple Runeword slots, every individual rune name must be unique across all equipped Runewords — plan your full loadout before crafting.

## Pitfalls

- **Gems vs. Runes conflict:** Slotting Runes into an item removes Gem sockets — plan which items carry Runewords and which keep Gems before itemizing.
- **Duplicate rune lockout:** Using the same rune name twice anywhere on your character is illegal — double-check all four possible Runeword slots.
- **Offering mismatch wastes Runewords:** Pairing a slow Ritual (Zan fires once per Ultimate use) with a very low-cost Invocation (Gar 25) wastes most Offering to Overflow inefficiently; match tiers deliberately.
- **Cir exhaustion window:** After proccing 300 Offering, Cir is locked for 3 s — builds relying on it for burst timing must account for the dead window.
- **Noc generates only 5 Offering per CC** (10 for non-Slow/Chill); pairing it with high-cost Invocations requires dozens of CC applications — only viable in extremely CC-heavy builds.
- **Rune crafting is random:** 3× same-name runes → 1 random rune; do not burn named runes needed for Mythic crafting on gambling upgrades.
- **Vessel of Hatred required:** Runes and Runewords are entirely unavailable without the expansion — seasonal characters on base game cannot access this system.
