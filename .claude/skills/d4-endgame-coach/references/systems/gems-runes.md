# Gems Runes

> Season: 13 · Patch: 2.4.1 · Last refreshed: 2026-05-21 · Source: https://maxroll.gg/d4/resources/runewords-overview

## What it is

Runes and Runewords are a socketing system introduced in the **Vessel of Hatred** expansion (Season 13, Patch 2.4.1). Two rune types exist:

- **Rune of Ritual** — generates a resource called **Offering** when a condition is met
- **Rune of Invocation** — fires an effect when enough Offering has been accumulated

Socketing one of each into the same item creates a **Runeword**. Runes drop at Magic, Rare, and Legendary rarity; higher rarity = stronger effect.

---

## Mechanics

1. **Offering loop** — The Ritual rune's trigger condition generates Offering. Once the paired Invocation rune's threshold is met, its effect fires automatically.
2. **Overflow** — Any Offering generated beyond the Invocation rune's requirement becomes Overflow; the bonus applied scales proportionally to the Overflow amount.
3. **Cross-class skills** — Runes of Invocation grant skills from other classes (e.g., Thul fires Frost Nova, Ner fires Concealment, Jah replaces Evade with Teleport).
4. **Rarity scaling** — More demanding Ritual conditions yield higher Offering per trigger; higher-rarity runes produce stronger base effects.
5. **Two new Invocation Runes** — Kel and Zid were added in the Season 11 update (December 21, 2025).

## Costs / requirements

- **Expansion required:** Vessel of Hatred (Runes exclusive to this expansion)
- **Pool size:** 15 Runes of Ritual, 26 Runes of Invocation (as of Patch 2.4.1)
- **Valid item slots:** Helm, Chest, Legs, Two-Handed Weapon (item must have 2 sockets)
- **Gems vs. Runes:** Cannot equip both Gems and Runes in the same item
- **Duplicate restriction:** Cannot use two of the same Rune across a character's Runewords
- **Storage:** Stored in the **Socketables** tab; Runes are **tradeable**
- **Crafting (upgrade):** Combine **3 runes of the same name** at the Jeweler → receive 1 random rune
- **Mythic Unique crafting:** Requires 2 Resplendent Spark + 6 Legendary Runes + 6 Rare Runes + 6 Magic Runes of specific names

### Key interactions
- **Nagu + summon builds:** Generates 100 Offering per maintained summon (up to 5 summons = up to 500 Offering per 5 s window)
- **Noc + hard-CC builds:** Doubles Offering for non-Slow/non-Chill CC, rewarding hard-CC-heavy builds
- **Cir:** Bursty 300 Offering every 5 skills, but triggers a 3-second exhaustion window — pairs best with fast-casting builds that can accept the pause
- **Overflow scaling:** High-Offering Ritual runes (Bac 400, Zan 200, Neo 200) paired with low-threshold Invocation runes (Gar 25, Qua 50, Eom 100) maximize Overflow bonus frequency

## Strategy

1. **Match Offering rate to Invocation threshold** — A Ritual rune that fires frequently for small amounts suits low-threshold Invocations (Gar, Qua, Eom, Wat). High-burst Ritual runes (Zan, Bac) suit high-threshold Invocations (Yom, Ohm, Ner).
2. **Prioritize cross-class utility** — Runes of Invocation bypass class restrictions; low-mobility classes can gain Teleport (BacJah), tankier classes can proc Frost Nova (Thul) or Decrepify (Wat) for free.
3. **Maximize Overflow** — When Offering generated far exceeds the Invocation cost, Overflow amplifies the effect proportionally; stack Nagu on summon builds or Cir on fast-casting builds against a low-threshold Invocation.
4. **Duplicate rule planning** — Because no two Runewords on the same character can share a rune, plan all slots (up to 4 items) as a set to avoid conflicts before trading or crafting.
5. **Upgrade path** — Farm duplicate low-value runes and combine 3× at the Jeweler to fish for higher-value runes; rarity directly upgrades effect strength.
6. **Mythic crafting use case** — If targeting Andariel's Visage, farming Tam and Zan has dual purpose: Offering generation in live builds and Mythic crafting material.

## Pitfalls

- **Gems and Runes are mutually exclusive per item** — committing sockets to a Runeword removes Gem slots on that piece permanently.
- **Duplicate rune restriction is character-wide** — equipping the same rune in two different items is not allowed; plan your full runeword loadout before socketing.
- **Vessel of Hatred required** — Runes do not exist in base Diablo IV; players without the expansion cannot access this system.
- **Cir exhaustion window** — 3-second exhaustion after 300 Offering fires; using it with a high-cost Invocation means dead time with no Offering generation.
- **Poc and Noc generate very low Offering (5 per trigger)** — pairing either with high-threshold Invocations (Yom 500, Ner 600) results in extremely slow trigger rates; only viable with many rapid procs.
- **Rarity matters at drop** — a Magic rune and a Legendary rune of the same name perform differently; socketing a low-rarity rune into a BiS item slot is a hidden opportunity cost.
- **Crafting produces a *random* rune** — combining 3× at the Jeweler does not guarantee the desired output; factor in trade value before consuming runes.
