# Helltide Farming

> Season: 13 · Patch: 2.4.1 · Last refreshed: 2026-05-23 · Source: https://maxroll.gg/d4/resources/helltide-guide

## Goal

Farm Aberrant Cinders to open Tortured Gifts, collect Forgotten Souls for Occultist enchanting, gather crafting materials (Living Steel, Angelbreath, Exquisite Blood), and acquire Boss Lair Keys — all within a 55-minute timed window.

---

## Unlock / Access

- Available from **Normal difficulty** onwards; unlocked immediately on seasonal character creation
- Helltide occupies a **fixed set of four connected zones** within a single region per event
- Schedule: starts **top of each hour**, lasts **55 minutes**, 5-minute break before next
- Threat Meter status **persists and carries over** between Helltide events
- Aberrant Cinders **do not carry over** — spend before the event ends
- **Dying loses half** of your currently held Aberrant Cinders

## DPS / EHP Checkpoints

| Difficulty | Characteristics | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Standard (WT1/WT2) | No roaming bosses, no meteor showers, reduced monster density | Lower Cinder income; Accursed Ritual requires only 2 Baneful Hearts |
| Torment | Roaming bosses (Kixxarth), meteor showers, full monster density, Armor/Weapon/Jewelry/Mystery Gifts all available | Required for Tortured Gift of Mysteries (250-Cinder version) and max Living Steel yields |

- No explicit DPS/EHP number thresholds are documented in source material
- Torment meteor showers and environmental fire deal **Fire damage** — plan resistances accordingly
- Hellborne mirrors abilities of one of the five player classes — EHP demands scale with encounter

## Strategy by Tier

1. **Prioritize Local Events** — Event Chests drop 3–6 Aberrant Cinders, the highest single-source yield; focus on high enemy density areas near event markers
2. **Run Hellspires and Ravenous Soulspires** — both generate Threat rapidly, advancing the Threat Meter toward the Hell Marked state and Hellborne spawn; stay inside the boundary ring
3. **Farm Damned/Tortured/Wretched Souls** (red-hued) — each drops 3–5 Cinders; prioritize over generic enemies
4. **Collect Fiend Rose and Screaming Hell Vein nodes** — 3–4 Cinders each; efficient passive pickups while pathing
5. **Complete Portal Invasion Events** — kill all combatants to spawn Helltide Commander for Aberrant Cinders + Obols bonus
6. **Deposit Baneful Hearts at Accursed Ritual pedestals** — contributing a Baneful Heart grants bonus loot from Blood Maiden; contributing early ensures credit
7. **Hunt Kixxarth (Torment)** — roaming across active zones; high Legendary drop rate on kill
8. **Spend Cinders before timer expires** — Aberrant Cinders do not carry over; prioritize Tortured Gift of Mysteries (Kehjistan for 3 spawns) on Torment for max Living Steel
9. **75-Cinder Gifts are most efficient for Forgotten Soul farming** — open as many as Cinder supply allows
10. **Avoid dying** — death forfeits 50% of held Aberrant Cinders

## Boss Mechanics (if applicable)

| Phase | Mechanic | Counter |
|---|---|---|
| **Blood Maiden** — ongoing | Flame Ring: moderate Fire damage + applies Vulnerable on contact | Use a movement skill to pass through the ring; stepping in grants the Vulnerable window but deals damage |
| **Blood Maiden** — health breakpoints | Summons 4 Hellborne Disciples | Burst down Disciples quickly; they mirror player class abilities |
| **Blood Maiden** — ongoing | Places fire circles every ~0.5s until 4 placed, then explode in sequence | Track placement order; dodge earliest-placed circles first |
| **Hellborne** — general | Abilities mirror one of the five player classes (Barbarian, Druid, Necromancer, Rogue, Sorcerer) | Anticipate class-specific burst/CC patterns based on visible archetype |
| **Helltide Commander** — Portal Invasion | Spawns only after all combatants in the Portal Invasion are defeated | Clear all portal waves before engaging; do not leave the event area |
| **Kixxarth, Helltide Assassin** — general | Roaming Torment-only boss; no documented phase mechanics in source | Intercept in active zones; standard burst-kill priority |
