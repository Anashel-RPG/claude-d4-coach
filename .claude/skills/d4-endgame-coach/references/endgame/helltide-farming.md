# Helltide Farming

> Season: 10 · Patch: 2.4.x · Last refreshed: 2026-05-16 · Source: https://maxroll.gg/d4/resources/helltide-guide

## Goal
Farm Aberrant Cinders to open Tortured Gifts for Legendaries/Uniques and collect Forgotten Souls for enchanting.

## Unlock / access
- Available immediately on character creation (Seasonal)
- Playable from Normal difficulty onwards
- Torment difficulties unlock roaming bosses, meteor showers, and individual gear-slot Gifts

## Strategy by tier

1. Track event timing — Helltide runs 55 min on, 5 min off, starting top of every hour.
2. Enter zone and immediately locate nearby Local Events — highest Cinder yield per minute.
3. Kill red-hued interactable objects (Tortured Souls, etc.) for guaranteed Cinder drops.
4. Fill Threat Meter intentionally; use Hellspires and Ravenous Soulspires for fast Threat accumulation.
5. Cap Threat → become Hell Marked → defeat Hellborne for Boss Lair Keys and loot.
6. Collect 3 Baneful Hearts (2 in WT1/WT2) → deposit at ritual site → defeat Blood Maiden for bonus loot.
7. Mark nearby Tortured Gifts on map; spend all Cinders before event ends — they do not carry over.
8. If farming Forgotten Souls specifically, open only 75-Cinder Gifts to maximize quantity.
9. Group up to stagger Threat Meter caps, spawning multiple Hellborne simultaneously.
10. Do not die — death costs 50% of current Aberrant Cinders.

## Boss mechanics (if applicable)

### Blood Maiden

| Phase | Mechanic | Counter |
|---|---|---|
| Any | Flame Ring — outward-expanding fire ring | Use movement skill to step inside the ring |
| Any | Flame Circle — 4 fire circles placed at player position every 0.5s, then explode in sequence | Move away immediately on cast; spread from other players |
| 75% / 50% / 25% HP | Summon Disciples — flies up, places 4 fire circles that spawn Hellborne Disciples | Kill Disciples quickly; dodge circles |
