# Helltide Farming

> Season: 13 · Patch: 2.4.1 · Last refreshed: 2026-05-24 · Source: https://maxroll.gg/d4/resources/helltide-guide

## Goal

Farm Aberrant Cinders to open Tortured Gifts, collect Forgotten Souls for Occultist enchanting, and gather crafting materials (Living Steel, Exquisite Blood, Angelbreath, Boss Lair Keys) during a 55-minute timed world event.

---

## Unlock / Access

- Available from **Normal difficulty** onwards on seasonal characters.
- No prerequisite quest — unlocked immediately on seasonal character creation.
- Helltide occupies **4 connected zones** within a single region per event.
- Schedule: starts at the top of each hour, lasts **55 minutes**, followed by a **5-minute break**.
- Threat Meter status **carries over** between Helltide events.
- Aberrant Cinders **do not carry over** — spend before the event ends.
- **Dying causes loss of half your collected Aberrant Cinders.**

## Strategy by Tier

1. **Standard Difficulty** — Prioritize Local Events for 3–6 Cinders per Event Chest. Gather Fiend Rose / Screaming Hell Vein nodes while routing between events. No roaming bosses to manage.
2. **Torment — Cinder Farming** — Route through all 4 active zones hitting Local Events first. Kill Damned/Tortured/Wretched Souls (red hue) for 3–5 Cinders each. Collect Treasure Goblins opportunistically.
3. **Torment — Threat Meter Management** — Kill enemies and open Gifts to fill the Threat Meter through its 3 tiers. Use Hellspires and Ravenous Soulspires to stack Threat rapidly. When Hell Marked triggers, survive the ambush waves until Hellborne spawns — kill it for Boss Lair Keys and crafting materials.
4. **Torment — Accursed Ritual** — Collect 3 Baneful Hearts (from Gifts or Hellborne kills). Deposit into pedestals at the ritual site to trigger enemy waves culminating in Blood Maiden. Fight Blood Maiden for Exquisite Blood and Forgotten Souls. Use a movement skill to enter/exit Flame Rings safely.
5. **Torment — Portal Invasion Event** — Kill Occultists to trigger Invasion Portals. Clear all combatants to spawn the Helltide Commander Boss. Rewards: Aberrant Cinders, items, and Murmuring Obols.
6. **Tortured Gift of Mysteries Priority** — Save 250 Cinders per Mysteries chest. In Kehjistan (3 spawns vs. 2 elsewhere), prioritize this region when chasing Living Steel or Angelbreath. Spend remaining Cinders on 75-Cinder Gifts for Forgotten Soul efficiency before the event timer expires.

## Boss Mechanics (if applicable)

| Phase | Mechanic | Counter |
|---|---|---|
| Blood Maiden – Any HP | Flame Ring — places fire circles every ~0.5s (up to 4), then explodes in placement order; deals Fire damage + applies Vulnerable on contact | Use a movement skill to pass through the ring without lingering; track placement order |
| Blood Maiden – HP Breakpoints | Summons 4 Hellborne Disciples | Switch focus to Disciples briefly or use AoE to clear while maintaining Blood Maiden damage |
| Hellborne – Spawn | Fallen champion with abilities mirroring one of the 5 player classes (class abilities vary per spawn) | Identify class archetype quickly; treat as a mini-boss with class-appropriate burst windows |
| Helltide Commander – Portal Invasion | Spawns after all portal combatants are cleared; grants Aberrant Cinders, items, and Obols on defeat | Clear wave enemies efficiently before engaging Commander; standard boss fight |
| Kixxarth, Helltide Assassin – Torment only | Roaming assassin boss; grants Aberrant Cinders and high Legendary drop chance | Engage when encountered; high priority kill for Cinder and loot value |
