## Strategy by tier

1. **Entry (Standard Difficulty)** — Clear events and kill monsters for Magic Tempering Manuals and basic crafting mats; collect Helltide Chests for early rune drops.
2. **Torment Difficulty** — Prioritize Helltide Chests for Rare and Legendary Tempering Manuals; target chests that can drop Legendary runes for runeword setups.
3. **Optimized Torment runs** — Collect Boss Lair Keys from monster kills and chests alongside runes; funnel keys into Initiate Lair Boss encounters for additional loot layers.
