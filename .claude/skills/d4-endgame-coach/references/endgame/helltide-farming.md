# Helltide Farming

> Season: 13 · Patch: 2.4.1 · Last refreshed: 2026-05-25 · Source: https://maxroll.gg/d4/resources/helltide-guide

## Goal

Farm Aberrant Cinders to open Tortured Gifts, collect Forgotten Souls for Occultist enchanting, gather crafting materials (Living Steel, Angelbreath, Exquisite Blood), and obtain Boss Lair Keys from Hellborne kills.

## Unlock / access

- Available from Normal difficulty onwards on seasonal characters
- Active every hour on the hour; lasts 55 minutes, followed by a 5-minute break
- Occupies a fixed set of four connected zones within a region (two zones in Nahantu)
- Aberrant Cinders **do not carry over** between Helltide events; spend before the timer expires
- Dying causes loss of **half** of currently held Aberrant Cinders
- Helltide Threat Meter status **does** carry over between events

## Strategy by tier

1. **Any difficulty — baseline loop:** Kill enemies and elites for Cinders → prioritize Local Events for 3–6 Cinders from Event Chests (highest yield) → collect Fiend Rose / Screaming Hell Vein nodes (3–4 Cinders each) → spend Cinders on 75-Cinder Gifts (most efficient for Forgotten Souls) before the 55-minute timer expires.
2. **Threat Meter management:** Killing enemies and opening Gifts fills the Threat Meter through three tiers. Use Hellspires and Ravenous Soulspires to build Threat rapidly. At max Threat the player becomes Hell Marked; surviving depletes the meter and spawns a Hellborne. Threat Meter status carries over to the next Helltide.
3. **Torment — Hellborne farming:** Push Threat aggressively via Hellspires / Ravenous Soulspires → trigger Hell Marked → kill spawned Hellborne for Boss Lair Keys (Varshan, The Beast, Grigoire, Lord Zir, Urivar) and crafting materials.
4. **Torment — Accursed Ritual:** Collect 3 Baneful Hearts (from Gifts or Hellborne drops) → deposit into pedestals at ritual site → defeat enemy waves → kill Blood Maiden for Exquisite Blood, Forgotten Souls, and Legendaries. Watch for Hellborne Disciples summoned at health breakpoints.
5. **Torment — Portal Invasion:** Kill Occultists to trigger Invasion Portals → defeat all portal combatants → Helltide Commander Boss spawns → kill for Aberrant Cinders, items, and Murmuring Obols.
6. **Torment — Mystery Chest priority:** In Kehjistan, 3 Tortured Gift of Mysteries spawn per Helltide (vs. 2 elsewhere); route there when targeting 14–16 Living Steel or Angelbreath.
7. **Death prevention:** Dying loses half your Aberrant Cinders. Open Gifts in batches before engaging dangerous content, or portal to safety to bank progress.

|---|---|---|
