# Boss Ladder

> Season: 13 · Patch: 2.4.1 · Last refreshed: 2026-05-16 · Source: https://maxroll.gg/d4/resources/bosses-overview

## Goal

Progress through the Boss Ladder — a tiered structure of Lair Bosses — to farm boss-specific Uniques, Mythic Uniques, and currency for the next tier, culminating in Belial, the Exalted Lair Boss.

## Unlock / access

- **Initiate Lair Boss Keys** drop from War Plans, Nightmare Dungeons, Helltides, the Undercity, and other endgame activities.
- **Greater Lair Keys** drop from defeated Initiate Lair Bosses; required to open Greater Lair Boss Hoards.
- **Betrayer's Husk** drops rarely from any Lair Boss or via the Ambush Encounter; 2× required to craft Belial's Lair Key.
- **Ambush Encounter** triggers randomly when opening any Lair Boss Hoard; defeating the boss there drops a Betrayer's Husk alongside the original boss loot table.
- **Astaroth** (Nightmare Boss) requires Torment 1 or higher to fight.
- All Boss Ladder bosses can be challenged without committing resources before the fight.

## Strategy by tier

1. **Initiate Lair Bosses** — Farm Lair Keys from endgame activities, then run any of the five Initiate bosses (Grigoire, Lord Zir, Beast in the Ice, Urivar, Echo of Varshan). Each Hoard costs 1 Lair Key and drops a Greater Lair Key to fund the next tier. Watch for the Ambush Encounter when opening Hoards.
2. **Ambush Encounter** — Triggers randomly on any Hoard open. Defeat the boss to earn a Betrayer's Husk plus the normal boss loot table. Stack Betrayer's Husks toward Belial's key (2× needed).
3. **Greater Lair Bosses** — Spend Greater Lair Keys on Duriel (Gaping Crevasse), Echo of Andariel (Hanged Man's Hall), The Butcher (The Broiler, Gea Kul, added May 2, 2026), or Harbinger of Hatred. All share a loot table; no keys drop here. Continue collecting Betrayer's Husks.
4. **Exalted Lair Boss (Belial)** — Once 2× Betrayer's Husk are held, craft Belial's key. Belial (Lord of Lies) is the final Boss Ladder step. Hoard requires the Betrayer's Husk obtained from the Ambush Encounter.
5. **Pinnacle Boss (Echo of Lilith)** — Located in the Church Ruin area of Nevesk. First kill rewards a Resplendent Spark and cosmetics. Defeating her completes the Seasonal Journey.
6. **World Bosses (parallel track)** — Ashava, Avarice, Azmodan (added Season 11), and The Wandering Death spawn at fixed zones across all regions. Drop Spoils and Scattered Prism. Manage the Resilience mechanic by coordinating with other players to break it.
7. **Nightmare/Infernal Bosses (parallel track)** — Astaroth (Escalating Nightmares, T1+ required) accompanied by The Amalgam of Rage. Bartuc, Lord of Chaos appears at the end of an Infernal Hordes run.
