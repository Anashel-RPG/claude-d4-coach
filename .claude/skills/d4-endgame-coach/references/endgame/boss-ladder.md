# Boss Ladder

> Season: 13 · Patch: 2.4.1 · Last refreshed: 2026-05-22 · Source: https://maxroll.gg/d4/resources/bosses-overview

## Goal

Progress through a three-tier ladder of increasingly difficult boss lairs, culminating in Belial (Exalted Lair Boss) for guaranteed Ancestral Uniques and Mythic Unique chances. Keys gate each tier — you must farm down to climb up.

## Unlock / access

- **Boss Lair Keys (Initiate tier)** — drop from: War Plans, Nightmare Dungeons, Helltides, Undercity
- **Greater Lair Keys** — awarded by opening any Initiate Lair Boss Hoard (requires 1× Boss Lair Key)
- **Betrayer's Husk (×2)** — required to open Belial's Exalted Hoard; sources:
  - Rare drop from any Initiate or Greater Lair Boss Hoard
  - Belial Ambush Encounter (triggers randomly after any Boss Lair encounter; a timer starts after the boss dies — Belial may spawn when it completes)
- **Torment 1** minimum required for Astaroth (Escalating Nightmares) and Bartuc (Infernal Hordes) — these are separate from the Boss Ladder proper

| Tier | Drops | Notes |

| **Infernal Hordes Boss — Bartuc** | Obducite, Neathiron (resets Masterworking rolls) | Requires Torment 1+ and 666 Burning Aether |
| **World Bosses** (Ashava, Avarice, Azmodan, Wandering Death) | Exquisite Blood, Scattered Prism, boss-specific Spoils | Players auto-merged into shared instance at spawn location |

## Strategy by tier

1. **Farm Boss Lair Keys** via Helltides, Nightmare Dungeons, War Plans, or Undercity until you have a stockpile.
2. **Run Initiate Lair Bosses** (Urivar, Grigoire, Beast in the Ice, Echo of Varshan, Lord Zir) — open every Hoard to collect Greater Lair Keys and watch for Betrayer's Husk drops.
3. **Watch for the Belial Ambush** after each boss kill — defeat him to earn Betrayer's Husk reliably.
4. **Run Greater Lair Bosses** (Duriel, Echo of Andariel, Harbinger of Hatred, The Butcher) with Greater Lair Keys — continue accumulating Betrayer's Husks.
5. **Stack 2× Betrayer's Husk**, then challenge **Belial** at the Palace of the Deceiver (Kehjistan) — select your target Boss Ladder boss before looting to direct the loot table.
6. **Echo of Lilith** (Church Ruin, Nevesk) — Pinnacle Boss; complete for Resplendent Spark and Seasonal Journey credit.
7. **Astaroth** — reach the final dungeon of Escalating Nightmares at Torment 1+; he is accompanied by The Amalgam of Rage.
8. **Bartuc** — spend 666 Burning Aether at Torment 1+ after an Infernal Hordes run for Obducite and Neathiron.

## Boss mechanics (if applicable)

| Phase | Mechanic | Counter |
|---|---|---|
| World Bosses (all) | **Resilience** — drastically reduces damage taken | Coordinate with other players in the shared instance to burst through the damage threshold |
| Belial Ambush | Spawns randomly on a timer after a Boss Lair encounter concludes | Stay in the area after the boss dies; don't leave before the timer resolves |
| Belial Exalted Hoard | No fixed loot table — you select a Boss Ladder boss to determine drops | Pre-decide your target before spending 2× Betrayer's Husk |
| Astaroth | Accompanied by The Amalgam of Rage (infernal hound) | Requires Torment 1+; clear via Escalating Nightmares final dungeon |
| Bartuc | Triggered only with 666 Burning Aether at Torment 1+ | Farm Burning Aether during the Infernal Hordes run before the encounter |
