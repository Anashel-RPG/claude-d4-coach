# Boss Ladder

> Season: 13 · Patch: 2.4.1 · Last refreshed: 2026-05-17 · Source: https://maxroll.gg/d4/resources/bosses-overview

## Goal

Progress through a tiered boss structure (Boss Ladder) to farm boss-specific Uniques, Mythic Uniques, and upgrade materials. The ladder culminates in Belial, Lord of Lies — the Exalted Lair Boss.

## Unlock / access

- **Lair Keys (Initiate)** — dropped from War Plans, Nightmare Dungeons, Helltides, the Undercity, and other endgame activities
- **Greater Lair Keys** — obtained by opening Initiate Lair Boss Hoards
- **Betrayer's Husk (×2)** — required to open Belial's Hoard; drops rarely from all Boss Ladder bosses, and from the Belial Ambush Encounter
- All bosses can be fought without spending resources before the fight; keys are only consumed when opening the Hoard after the kill
- Bartuc (Infernal Hordes) and Astaroth (Escalating Nightmares) both require **Torment 1** or higher

### Key acquisition chain

```
Endgame activities → Lair Key
→ Kill Initiate Boss → open Hoard → Greater Lair Key (+ rare: Betrayer's Husk)
→ Kill Greater Boss → open Hoard → Betrayer's Husk (rare)
→ 2× Betrayer's Husk → open Belial's Exalted Hoard
```

## Strategy by tier

1. **Farm Lair Keys** from Helltides, Nightmare Dungeons, War Plans, or the Undercity until you have a stockpile before starting a boss session.
2. **Clear all five Initiate Lair Bosses** (Urivar — Fields of Judgement, west of Kichuk; Grigoire — Hall of the Penitent, south of Ked Bardu; Beast in the Ice; Echo of Varshan; Lord Zir — Ancient's Seat, Darkened Way). Open every Hoard to collect Greater Lair Keys and watch for Betrayer's Husk drops.
3. **Spend Greater Lair Keys on Greater Lair Bosses** (Duriel — Gaping Crevasse, east of Gea Kul; Echo of Andariel — Hanged Man's Hall, east of Tarsarak; Harbinger of Hatred; The Butcher). Each Hoard has a rare chance to yield a Betrayer's Husk.
4. **Watch for the Belial Ambush Encounter** — on rare occasions after any Boss Lair kill, a timer starts and Belial may spawn. Defeating him here awards Betrayer's Husk.
5. **Accumulate 2× Betrayer's Husk**, then challenge Belial at Palace of the Deceiver (Kehjistan). Before opening his Hoard, select the Boss Ladder boss whose loot table you want — Belial's Hoard mirrors that table. Rewards include a guaranteed Ancestral Unique and a high chance at Infernal Hordes Compasses.
6. **Echo of Lilith** (Church Ruin, Nevesk) is the Pinnacle Boss — prioritise the first kill for the Resplendent Spark and Seasonal Journey credit.
7. **Astaroth** (end of Escalating Nightmares, Torment 1+) for crafting materials and Legendary gear.
8. **Bartuc, Lord of Chaos** (end of Infernal Hordes run, Torment 1+, 666 Burning Aether) for Neathiron and Obducite.
9. **World Bosses** (Ashava, Avarice, Azmodan, The Wandering Death) spawn at fixed open-world locations; join the auto-shared instance for Exquisite Blood and Scattered Prism. Note: World Bosses use a **Resilience** mechanic that drastically reduces damage taken — spread out high-burst windows accordingly.
