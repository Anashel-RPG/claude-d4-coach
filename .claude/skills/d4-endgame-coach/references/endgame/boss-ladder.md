# Boss Ladder

> Season: 13 · Patch: 2.4.1 · Last refreshed: 2026-05-18 · Source: https://maxroll.gg/d4/resources/bosses-overview

## Goal

Progress through a tiered system of increasingly powerful boss encounters to farm boss-specific Uniques, Mythic Uniques, crafting materials, and key currency for the next tier.

## Unlock / access

- **Requires:** World Tier 4 (Torment difficulty); Torment 1+ required for Bartuc and Astaroth
- **Lair Keys** (used for Initiate Lair Bosses) drop from: War Plans, Nightmare Dungeons, Helltides, Undercity
- **Greater Lair Keys** drop from Initiate Lair Boss Hoards → used for Greater Lair Bosses
- **Betrayer's Husk** (×2) required to open Belial's Exalted Hoard — rare drop from any Boss Ladder boss; guaranteed source is the Belial Ambush Encounter
- **Belial Ambush Encounter:** triggers randomly after defeating any Boss Lair boss; a timer starts post-kill and Belial may spawn when it completes

## Strategy by tier

1. **Farm Initiate Lair Keys** via Helltides, Nightmare Dungeons, War Plans, or Undercity.
2. **Kill all five Initiate Lair Bosses** (Urivar, Grigoire, Beast in the Ice, Echo of Varshan, Lord Zir) — open each Hoard with 1× Lair Key to collect Greater Lair Keys and fish for Betrayer's Husks.
3. **Watch for Belial Ambush** after each boss kill — defeating him yields Betrayer's Husks needed for the Exalted Hoard.
4. **Kill Greater Lair Bosses** (Duriel, Harbinger of Hatred, Echo of Andariel, The Butcher) with Greater Lair Keys — each has a rare Betrayer's Husk drop and a Mythic Unique chance.
5. **Accumulate 2× Betrayer's Husk**, then challenge **Belial** at the Palace of the Deceiver (Kehjistan) — select your preferred Boss Ladder boss before opening the Hoard to set the loot table.
6. **Echo of Lilith** (Church Ruin, Nevesk) — Pinnacle Boss; target after build is fully functional; first kill grants a Resplendent Spark.
7. **Astaroth** — complete Escalating Nightmares at Torment 1+ and clear the final dungeon to access him.
8. **Bartuc, Lord of Chaos** — run Infernal Hordes at Torment 1+, accumulate 666 Burning Aether, defeat him at run's end for Neathiron and Obducite.
9. **World Bosses** — join shared instances at spawn locations (Crucible, Saraan Caldera, The Seared Basin, The Fields of Desecration, The Cauldron); target for Exquisite Blood and Scattered Prisms.
