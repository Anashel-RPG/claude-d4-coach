# Boss Ladder

> Season: 13 · Patch: 2.4.1 · Last refreshed: 2026-05-23 · Source: https://maxroll.gg/d4/resources/bosses-overview

## Goal

Progress through a tiered boss hierarchy — Initiate → Greater → Exalted Lair — to target specific Uniques, Mythic Uniques, and crafting materials. Final objective is defeating Belial, Lord of Lies (Exalted Lair) for a guaranteed Ancestral Unique.

---

## Unlock / access

**Key sources (Boss Lair Keys):**
- Lair Keys (for Initiate bosses) drop from: War Plans, Nightmare Dungeons, Helltides, Undercity
- Greater Lair Keys come from opening Initiate Lair Boss Hoards
- Betrayer's Husk (×2, for Belial) comes from the Belial Ambush Encounter (rare random trigger after any Boss Lair kill) or as a rare drop from any Lair boss

**Difficulty requirements:**
- All Boss Ladder bosses: accessible at any difficulty (no stated minimum)
- Astaroth (Nightmare Boss): requires Torment 1+; accessed via last dungeon of Escalating Nightmares
- Bartuc, Lord of Chaos (Infernal Hordes): requires Torment 1+ and 666 Burning Aether

## Strategy by tier

1. **Farm Lair Keys** — run Helltides, Nightmare Dungeons, War Plans, or Undercity to stockpile Lair Keys before engaging Initiate bosses.
2. **Clear Initiate Lair Bosses** — defeat any of the five (Urivar, Grigoire, Beast in the Ice, Echo of Varshan, Lord Zir); spend 1× Lair Key per Hoard to collect Greater Lair Keys and watch for Betrayer's Husk drops.
3. **Watch for Belial Ambush** — after each Boss Lair kill, a timer runs; if Belial spawns, defeat him to earn Betrayer's Husk. Farm until you hold 2×.
4. **Clear Greater Lair Bosses** — spend Greater Lair Keys on Duriel, Harbinger of Hatred, Echo of Andariel, or The Butcher. All four share a loot table; pick whichever Unique you are targeting.
5. **Challenge Belial (Exalted)** — spend 2× Betrayer's Husk; select a Boss Ladder boss at the Hoard to direct his loot table; collect guaranteed Ancestral Unique.
6. **Escalating Nightmares → Astaroth** — requires Torment 1+; complete the activity to reach the final dungeon where Astaroth (and his companion The Amalgam of Rage) await.
7. **Infernal Hordes → Bartuc** — run Infernal Hordes on Torment 1+ and accumulate 666 Burning Aether to unlock Bartuc; rewards Neathiron and Obducite.
8. **Echo of Lilith (Pinnacle)** — defeat once per season for the Resplendent Spark first-kill reward; counts as a Seasonal Journey objective.
