# Boss Ladder

> Season: 13 · Patch: 2.4.1 · Last refreshed: 2026-05-19 · Source: https://maxroll.gg/d4/resources/bosses-overview

## Goal

Progress through a tiered chain of Boss Lairs — Initiate → Greater → Exalted — to farm Uniques, Mythic Uniques, and crafting materials. Belial (Exalted) is the ladder's final step and drops a guaranteed Ancestral Unique.

---

## Unlock / access

- **Tier 1 — Initiate Lair Bosses:** Defeat the boss, then spend 1× Lair Key to open their Hoard.
  - Lair Keys drop from: War Plans, Nightmare Dungeons, Helltides, Undercity.
- **Tier 2 — Greater Lair Bosses:** Spend 1× Greater Lair Key to open their Hoard.
  - Greater Lair Keys drop from: Initiate Lair Boss Hoards.
- **Tier 3 — Exalted Lair Boss (Belial):** Spend 2× Betrayer's Husk to open his Hoard.
  - Betrayer's Husk: rare drop from any Boss Ladder boss; also obtained via the Belial Ambush Encounter.
- **Belial Ambush Encounter:** Triggers randomly after defeating a Boss Lair boss; a timer starts post-kill and Belial may spawn when it completes.
- All bosses require **Torment 1 or higher** (implied by ladder access; Astaroth and Bartuc explicitly require Torment 1+).

## Strategy by tier

1. **Farm Lair Keys** from Helltides, Nightmare Dungeons, War Plans, or Undercity.
2. **Clear all 5 Initiate Lair Bosses** (Urivar, Grigoire, Beast in the Ice, Echo of Varshan, Lord Zir) — open each Hoard for Greater Lair Keys and a rare shot at Betrayer's Husk.
3. **Clear all 4 Greater Lair Bosses** (Duriel, Harbinger of Hatred, Echo of Andariel, The Butcher) using Greater Lair Keys — continue accumulating Betrayer's Husks.
4. **Watch for the Belial Ambush Encounter** after any Lair kill — defeating him here yields additional Betrayer's Husks; his Hoard requires 2×.
5. **Open Belial's Exalted Hoard** with 2× Betrayer's Husk — select a Boss Ladder boss from the prompt to determine the loot table; rewards a guaranteed Ancestral Unique plus Infernal Hordes Compasses.
6. **Run Escalating Nightmares** (Torment 1+) to reach Astaroth at the final dungeon for crafting material income.
7. **Run Infernal Hordes** (Torment 1+, 666 Burning Aether) to fight Bartuc for Obducite and Neathiron.
8. **Attend World Boss spawns** (Crucible, Saraan Caldera, Seared Basin, Fields of Desecration, The Cauldron) for Exquisite Blood and Scattered Prism — players are auto-merged into a shared instance.
9. **Echo of Lilith** (Church Ruin, Nevesk) is the Pinnacle Boss — required for the Seasonal Journey; drops a Resplendent Spark on first kill.
