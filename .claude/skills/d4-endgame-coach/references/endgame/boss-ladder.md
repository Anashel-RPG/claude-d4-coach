# Boss Ladder

> Season: 13 · Patch: 2.4.1 · Last refreshed: 2026-05-25 · Source: https://maxroll.gg/d4/resources/bosses-overview

## Goal

Progress through a tiered chain of Boss Lairs — Initiate → Greater → Exalted — to farm Uniques, Mythic Uniques, and crafting materials. The ladder's key-gating forces systematic boss routing rather than free-choice farming.

---

## Unlock / access

- **Initiate Lair Keys** (open Initiate Hoards) — drop from: War Plans, Nightmare Dungeons, Helltides, Undercity
- **Greater Lair Keys** (open Greater Hoards) — drop from: defeating any Initiate Lair Boss and opening their Hoard
- **Betrayer's Husk × 2** (opens Belial's Exalted Hoard) — rare drop from any Initiate or Greater Lair Boss; also obtainable via Belial Ambush Encounter
- **Belial Ambush Encounter** — triggers randomly after any Boss Lair encounter; a timer starts after the boss dies and Belial may spawn when it completes
- **Bartuc, Lord of Chaos** — requires Torment 1+ and 666 Burning Aether (earned inside Infernal Hordes)
- **Astaroth** — requires Torment 1+ and completion of Escalating Nightmares (resides in the final dungeon)
- **Echo of Lilith** — Pinnacle Boss; located in Church Ruin, Nevesk; defeating Pinnacle Bosses is often a Seasonal Journey objective

## Strategy by tier

1. **Farm Lair Keys** via Nightmare Dungeons, Helltides, War Plans, or Undercity until you have a stockpile before starting runs.
2. **Run all five Initiate Lair Bosses** (Urivar — Fields of Judgement; Grigoire — Hall of the Penitent; Beast in the Ice, Echo of Varshan, Lord Zir — Ancient's Seat, Darkened Way). Open each Hoard to collect Greater Lair Keys and watch for Betrayer's Husk drops.
3. **Convert Greater Lair Keys into Greater Lair Boss runs** (Duriel — Gaping Crevasse; Harbinger of Hatred; Echo of Andariel — Hanged Man's Hall; The Butcher). All four share a loot table — pick whichever is most efficient to reach. Collect Betrayer's Husks.
4. **Watch for the Belial Ambush Encounter** after any Boss Lair kill. A timer starts post-kill; if Belial spawns, defeating him can yield Betrayer's Husk.
5. **Accumulate 2× Betrayer's Husk**, then challenge Belial (Palace of the Deceiver, Kehjistan). At his Hoard, choose which Boss Ladder boss determines the loot pool — pick whoever holds your target Unique. Guaranteed Ancestral Unique drop.
6. **Run Infernal Hordes** to build 666 Burning Aether, then fight Bartuc, Lord of Chaos (Torment 1+) for Obducite and Neathiron (Masterworking materials).
7. **Run Escalating Nightmares** (Torment 1+) through to the final dungeon to fight Astaroth for crafting materials and Legendary gear.
8. **Kill Echo of Lilith** (Church Ruin, Nevesk) for the first-kill Resplendent Spark and Seasonal Journey credit.
9. **Join World Boss spawns** (Crucible, Saraan Caldera, The Seared Basin, The Fields of Desecration, The Cauldron) for Exquisite Blood and Scattered Prism. Damage is reduced by the Resilience mechanic — bring sustained group DPS.

## Boss mechanics (if applicable)

| Phase | Mechanic | Counter |
|---|---|---|
| World Bosses (all) | **Resilience** — drastically reduces damage taken throughout the fight | Sustained group DPS; do not rely on burst windows alone |
| Belial, Lord of Lies | **No fixed loot table** — player selects a Boss Ladder boss to determine Hoard drops | Choose based on target Unique before opening Hoard |
| Belial Ambush Encounter | **Timer-based spawn** — timer starts after any Boss Lair boss is defeated; Belial may spawn on completion | Remain in the area after killing a Lair Boss; be ready to engage |
| Astaroth | **Accompanied by The Amalgam of Rage** (infernal hound) | (not covered in source) |
| Bartuc, Lord of Chaos | **Gated by Burning Aether** — requires 666 Burning Aether plus Torment 1+ | Stockpile Aether across multiple Infernal Hordes runs before engaging |
