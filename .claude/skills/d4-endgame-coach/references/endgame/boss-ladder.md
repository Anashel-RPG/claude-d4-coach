# Boss Ladder

> Season: 10 · Patch: 2.4.x · Last refreshed: 2026-05-16 · Source: https://maxroll.gg/d4/resources/bosses-overview https://maxroll.gg/d4/resources/boss-loot-table-cheat-sheet

## Goal
Defeat tiered Boss Lair encounters to collect Unique and Mythic Unique items by spending Boss Lair Keys to open Hoards.

## Unlock / access
1. Reach Torment 1 difficulty.
2. Complete endgame activities (War Plans, Nightmare Dungeons, Helltides, Undercity, etc.) to obtain Lair Keys.
3. Challenge bosses freely; spend keys only to open the Hoard after defeating them.
4. Defeat Initiate Lair Bosses to receive Greater Lair Keys for Greater Lair Bosses.
5. Collect Betrayer's Husk (drops from Ambush Encounter Belial) to access Belial, the Exalted Lair Boss.

**Hoard drop rules:**
- Guaranteed 1 boss-specific Unique per Hoard.
- In party: 33% chance of 1 additional class-specific item per party member.
- ~50% of non-guaranteed drops from General Unique pool; ~50% boss-specific.
- Belial (Exalted) has higher Mythic Unique drop chance than other Lair Bosses.

## Strategy by tier

### Initiate Lair Bosses
1. Farm endgame activities to stockpile Lair Keys.
2. Challenge boss (free), then spend key to open Hoard.
3. Watch for Belial Ambush Encounter after opening a Hoard — defeating him drops Betrayer's Husk.
4. Use Greater Lair Keys obtained here to access Greater Lair Bosses.

### Greater Lair Bosses
1. Spend Greater Lair Keys obtained from Initiate bosses.
2. Same Ambush mechanic applies — Belial can spawn after Hoard opening.
3. All four Greater Lair Bosses share the same loot table structure but have distinct boss-specific drops.

### Exalted Lair Boss (Belial)
1. Accumulate 2× Betrayer's Husk from Ambush Encounters.
2. Travel to Palace of the Deceiver, Kehjistan.
3. Defeat Belial, then select a boss from the Boss Ladder to determine the loot table when opening the Hoard.

### World Bosses
1. Navigate to any of the five spawn locations.
2. Bring a group — Resilience mechanic greatly reduces damage taken by the boss.
3. Good source of Exquisite Blood.

### Pinnacle Boss (Echo of Lilith)
1. Located at Church Ruin, Nevesk.
2. Considered aspirational — not expected to be defeated by all players.
3. First kill rewards Resplendent Spark (used in Mythic Unique crafting).

### Nightmare Boss (Astaroth)
1. Complete Escalating Nightmare Dungeons (Torment 1+).
2. Spend Escalation Sigil to access; repeatable.

### Infernal Hordes Boss (Bartuc)
1. Enter Infernal Hordes (Torment 1+), near Yshari Sanctum Waypoint, Kehjistan.
2. Accumulate 666 Burning Aether during the run.
3. Spend Infernal Hordes Compass; defeat Bartuc at end of run.
