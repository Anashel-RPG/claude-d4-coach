# Boss Ladder

> Season: 13 · Patch: 2.4.1 · Last refreshed: 2026-05-24 · Source: https://maxroll.gg/d4/resources/bosses-overview

## Goal

Progress through a tiered system of Boss Lairs — Initiate → Greater → Exalted — to farm boss-specific Unique loot, Mythic Uniques, and crafting materials. Final step is defeating Belial, Lord of Lies (Exalted Lair Boss).

## Unlock / access

- **Lair Keys** (for Initiate bosses) drop from: War Plans, Nightmare Dungeons, Helltides, Undercity
- **Greater Lair Keys** are rewarded by opening Initiate Lair Boss Hoards (1× Lair Key → 1× Greater Lair Key)
- **Betrayer's Husk ×2** required to open Belial's Exalted Hoard — rare drop from any Boss Ladder boss; also obtainable via Belial Ambush Encounter
- **Belial Ambush Encounter:** rare random event that triggers after any Boss Lair encounter; a timer starts after the boss dies and Belial may spawn on completion
- All Boss Ladder bosses require defeating the boss first, then spending the key to open their **Hoard**
- Torment 1+ required for Astaroth (Escalating Nightmares) and Bartuc, Lord of Chaos (Infernal Hordes)
- Bartuc additionally requires **666 Burning Aether** (earned inside Infernal Hordes runs)

## Strategy by tier

1. **Farm Lair Keys** via Nightmare Dungeons, Helltides, War Plans, or Undercity.
2. **Run Initiate Lair Bosses** (Urivar, Grigoire, Beast in the Ice, Echo of Varshan, Lord Zir) — spend 1× Lair Key per Hoard to collect Greater Lair Keys and watch for Betrayer's Husk drops.
3. **Run Greater Lair Bosses** (Duriel, Harbinger of Hatred, Echo of Andariel, The Butcher) — spend 1× Greater Lair Key per Hoard; all four share a loot table, so pick whichever is convenient. Farm Betrayer's Husk here as well.
4. **Accumulate 2× Betrayer's Husk** — drops from any Boss Ladder boss at rare chance; also watch for the Belial Ambush Encounter to trigger after any Lair fight.
5. **Challenge Belial (Exalted)** — located in Palace of the Deceiver, Kehjistan. Spend 2× Betrayer's Husk to open Hoard; choose a Boss Ladder boss to set the loot table before opening.
6. **Echo of Lilith (Pinnacle)** — Church Ruin, Nevesk. Priority if completing Seasonal Journey; drops Resplendent Spark on first kill.
7. **Astaroth** — complete Escalating Nightmares (Torment 1+) to reach the final dungeon.
8. **Bartuc, Lord of Chaos** — run Infernal Hordes (Torment 1+) and spend 666 Burning Aether at end of run; rewards Obducite and Neathiron for Masterworking.

## Boss mechanics (if applicable)

| Phase | Mechanic | Counter |
|---|---|---|
| World Bosses (all) | Resilience — drastically reduces damage taken | Coordinate with other players in shared instance to hit damage thresholds |
| Belial Ambush Encounter | Spawns randomly after a Boss Lair defeat; timer begins on boss death | Stay in the lair area after killing any Boss Ladder boss until timer resolves |
| Belial, Lord of Lies — Hoard | Loot table is not fixed; player selects a Boss Ladder boss to determine drops | Choose based on which Unique items you need before opening |
| Astaroth | Accompanied by The Amalgam of Rage (infernal hound companion) | Manage both targets simultaneously; Torment 1+ required |
