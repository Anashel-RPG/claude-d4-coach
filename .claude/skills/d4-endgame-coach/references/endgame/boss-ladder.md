# Boss Ladder

> Season: 13 · Patch: 2.4.1 · Last refreshed: 2026-05-20 · Source: https://maxroll.gg/d4/resources/bosses-overview

## Goal

Progress through a tiered boss structure — Initiate → Greater → Exalted Lair Bosses — to farm boss-specific Uniques, Mythic Uniques, and key crafting materials. Defeating Pinnacle Bosses is often required for Seasonal Journey completion.

---

## Unlock / access

**Key sources** (Lair Keys drop from):
- War Plans
- Nightmare Dungeons
- Helltides
- Undercity

**Tier requirements:**

| Boss Tier | Entry Cost | Difficulty Req |
|---|---|---|
| Initiate Lair Boss | 1× Lair Key | — |
| Greater Lair Boss | 1× Greater Lair Key | — |
| Exalted Lair Boss (Belial) | 2× Betrayer's Husk | — |
| Astaroth (Nightmare Boss) | Complete Escalating Nightmares | Torment 1+ |
| Bartuc, Lord of Chaos (Infernal Hordes) | 666 Burning Aether | Torment 1+ |

**Betrayer's Husk** is obtained from the Belial Ambush Encounter — a random event that triggers after defeating any Boss Lair boss. A timer starts after the boss dies; Belial may spawn when it completes.

## Strategy by tier

1. **Farm Lair Keys** — run Helltides, Nightmare Dungeons, War Plans, or Undercity until you have enough Lair Keys to chain Initiate bosses.
2. **Initiate Lair Bosses** — kill Urivar, Grigoire, Beast in the Ice, Echo of Varshan, or Lord Zir; open Hoard with 1× Lair Key to receive a Greater Lair Key and a rare chance at Betrayer's Husk.
3. **Greater Lair Bosses** — use Greater Lair Keys on Duriel, Harbinger of Hatred, Echo of Andariel, or The Butcher. All four share the same loot table; pick based on convenience.
4. **Belial Ambush farming** — after any Boss Lair kill, wait for the timer; if Belial spawns, defeat him to collect Betrayer's Husks. Accumulate 2× to unlock his Exalted Hoard.
5. **Exalted Lair Boss (Belial)** — spend 2× Betrayer's Husk, then choose a Boss Ladder boss to apply their loot table to Belial's Hoard. Guaranteed Ancestral Unique drop.
6. **Side activities** — run Infernal Hordes (Torment 1+, 666 Burning Aether) for Bartuc and Obducite/Neathiron; run Escalating Nightmares (Torment 1+) for Astaroth rewards.
7. **World Bosses** — join shared instance at spawn locations (Crucible, Saraan Caldera, Seared Basin, Fields of Desecration, The Cauldron) for Exquisite Blood and Scattered Prism. Damage is capped by the Resilience mechanic, so prioritize showing up over solo optimization.
8. **Pinnacle Boss** — Echo of Lilith (Church Ruin, Nevesk) for Resplendent Spark on first kill; typically a Seasonal Journey requirement.
