# Boss Ladder

> Season: 13 · Patch: 2.4.1 · Last refreshed: 2026-05-21 · Source: https://maxroll.gg/d4/resources/bosses-overview

## Goal

Progress through a tiered boss structure — Initiate → Greater → Exalted — to farm boss-specific Uniques, Mythic Uniques, and crafting materials. Defeating Belial (Exalted) is the final step and often a Seasonal Journey objective.

---

## Unlock / access

**Lair Keys — how to obtain**

| Key Type | Sources |
|---|---|
| Lair Key (Initiate) | War Plans, Nightmare Dungeons, Helltides, Undercity |
| Greater Lair Key | Loot Hoard of any Initiate Lair Boss |
| Betrayer's Husk (×2) | Belial Ambush Encounter (rare, triggers after any Boss Lair kill) |

**Difficulty requirements**

- Astaroth (Nightmare Boss / Escalating Nightmares) — Torment 1+
- Bartuc, Lord of Chaos (Infernal Hordes) — Torment 1+ and 666 Burning Aether
- Boss Ladder bosses — no stated minimum beyond having the correct key

## Strategy by tier

1. **Farm Lair Keys** via Helltides, Nightmare Dungeons, War Plans, or Undercity until you have a comfortable stack of Initiate keys.
2. **Run Initiate Lair Bosses** (Urivar, Grigoire, Lord Zir, Beast in the Ice, Echo of Varshan) — spend 1× Lair Key per Hoard to collect Greater Lair Keys. Watch for Betrayer's Husk drops.
3. **Watch for Belial Ambush** — after any Boss Lair kill a timer may trigger and Belial spawns; defeat him to earn Betrayer's Husks (primary source).
4. **Run Greater Lair Bosses** (Duriel, Harbinger of Hatred, Echo of Andariel, The Butcher) — spend 1× Greater Lair Key per Hoard. Farm until you hold 2× Betrayer's Husk.
5. **Challenge Belial (Exalted)** at Palace of the Deceiver, Kehjistan — spend 2× Betrayer's Husk; select a Boss Ladder boss to determine the loot table. Guaranteed Ancestral Unique + Infernal Hordes Compasses.
6. **Side content — Escalating Nightmares** (Torment 1+): reach the final dungeon to fight Astaroth for crafting materials and Legendaries.
7. **Side content — Infernal Hordes** (Torment 1+, 666 Burning Aether): defeat Bartuc for Neathiron and Obducite.
8. **World Bosses**: check spawn locations (Crucible, Saraan Caldera, The Seared Basin, Fields of Desecration, The Cauldron) for Exquisite Blood and Scattered Prism; expect Resilience to cut your damage output significantly.
9. **Echo of Lilith** (Pinnacle, Church Ruin, Nevesk): target after gearing up — first kill drops a Resplendent Spark and counts toward the Seasonal Journey.

## Boss mechanics (if applicable)

| Phase | Mechanic | Counter |
|---|---|---|
| World Boss (all) | Resilience — drastically reduces damage taken | Coordinate with other players in the shared instance to hit damage checks; prioritize debuffs/vulnerability |
| Belial Ambush | Spawns on a timer after a Boss Lair kill (rare proc); Hoard costs 2× Betrayer's Husk | Don't leave the lair immediately after defeating a boss; wait out the timer |
| Belial Exalted Hoard | No fixed loot table — player selects a Boss Ladder boss to set drops | Choose the boss whose Unique you want before opening |
| Astaroth | Accompanied by The Amalgam of Rage (infernal hound) | Requires Torment 1+; clear Escalating Nightmares to reach final dungeon |
| Bartuc, Lord of Chaos | End-of-run Infernal Hordes boss; costs 666 Burning Aether | Requires Torment 1+; accumulate Burning Aether throughout the Infernal Hordes run |
