# Nightmare Dungeons

> Season: 13 · Patch: 2.4.1 · Last refreshed: 2026-05-18 · Source: https://maxroll.gg/d4/resources/nightmare-dungeons

## Goal

Farm Glyphs, Masterworking materials (Obducite), Legendaries/Uniques, Distilled Fear, and Escalation Sigils. The primary draw is the **Horadric Strongroom** found inside dungeons, followed by acquiring Escalation Sigils to run Escalating Nightmares.

---

## Unlock / access

- **Requirement:** Consume a Nightmare Sigil to open the dungeon.
- **First Sigil:** Complete 10 Whispers and turn them in at the Tree of Whispers (also tied to Seasonal Journey progression).
- **Ongoing Sigil sources:** Whispers of the Dead rewards, World Bosses (Torment difficulty), drops inside Nightmare Dungeon runs, and Escalating Nightmares rewards.
- **Crafting Sigils:** Occultist — costs 60 Sigil Powder + Gold. Salvage unwanted Sigils to recoup Sigil Powder.
- **Sigils are final:** No re-rolling. Dungeon, tier, and affixes are locked on creation.
- **Escalating Nightmares:** Requires an Escalation Sigil (obtained from Horadric Strongrooms, available as early as Torment 1).

## DPS / EHP checkpoints

- Each Torment level adds cumulative penalties to Damage Reduction from Armor and all elemental resistances.
- **Escalating Nightmares:** Each subsequent dungeon in the chain increases monster Life and Damage on top of inherited affixes.
- **Horadric Strongroom:** Monster difficulty scales with the parent dungeon's Torment level; higher Torment = harder Strongroom tests.
- **Vampiric Elites affix:** Elites always roll Vampiric + deal **+20% damage**.
- **Waller Elites affix:** Elites always roll Waller + deal **+20% damage**.
- **Berserkers affix:** Monsters deal **+50% damage**, take **+20% damage**.
- **Hunters affix:** Monsters move and attack **+33% faster**.
- **Profane Aegis affix:** All monsters gain **50% Max Life as Barrier**.
- **Lesser Aegis affix:** Some monsters gain **50% Max Life as Barrier**.

## Strategy by tier

1. **Getting started** — Prioritize Sigils with the **Horadric Strongroom** positive affix to guarantee a Strongroom. This is the most efficient Obducite and Escalation Sigil source.
2. **Avoid the "Obducite Mine" affix** — Currently bugged (Season 13, Patch 2.4.1); it does not function.
3. **Inside Horadric Strongrooms** — You have **100 seconds** to gather Horadric Attunement. Complete Pillar Rituals (~15 sec each) for **+2 Attunement/kill for 10 seconds** after. Follow purple wisps of energy to locate the Horadric Gateway.
4. **Strongroom Tests — prioritize high-value modifiers:**
   - *Ghosts* — Guide Ghost Apparitions to Pillars: **+75 Attunement** each.
   - *Lure* — Lure Mummies to Pillars: **+40 Attunement** each.
   - *Wandering* — Catch Wisps of Energy: **+25 Attunement** each.
   - *Tethering* — Kill Pillars' tethered monsters: **+4 Attunement** each.
   - *Doom* — Idol grants **+25% Attunement** while held, but **drops on potion use** — avoid using potions.
   - *Weight* — Same **+25% Attunement** Idol, but **drops on evade** — avoid dodging.
   - *Resistance* — All resistances **−10%**, grants **+5 Swiftness Motes per Shrine**.
5. **After 100 seconds** — A random dungeon boss spawns as the Strongroom's final encounter. Defeat it to spawn the Grand Horadric Cache (reward scales to Attunement gathered).
6. **Escalating Nightmares** — Chain 3 Nightmare Dungeons back-to-back. Affix count escalates: Dungeon 1 = 1 positive + 1 negative; Dungeon 2 = 2+2; Dungeon 3 = 3+3. After the third dungeon, a pillar spawns transporting you to fight Astaroth (Exalted).
7. **Synergy** — Nightmare Dungeons display active Whisper Bounties; complete them simultaneously for extra efficiency.

## Boss mechanics (if applicable)

**Astaroth (Exalted)** — Final boss of Escalating Nightmares. Three-phase fight.

| Phase | Mechanic | Counter |
|---|---|---|
| Phase 1 | Astaroth rides the Amalgam of Rage; both attack together | Focus damage on the Amalgam to deplete its health and trigger phase transition |
| Phase 2 | Amalgam slumps in the center to recover; Astaroth attacks alone | Kill Astaroth during this phase to **end the fight early** |
| Phase 3 | Both Astaroth and the Amalgam of Rage attack **independently** | Split awareness between two separate threat sources; prioritize the higher-damage target for your build |
| All phases | Three positive and three negative affixes active (revealed only after completing the third Escalating Nightmare dungeon) | Plan resistances and defensives around revealed affix set before engaging |
