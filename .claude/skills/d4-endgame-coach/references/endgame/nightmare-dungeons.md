# Nightmare Dungeons

> Season: 13 · Patch: 2.4.1 · Last refreshed: 2026-05-17 · Source: https://maxroll.gg/d4/resources/nightmare-dungeons

## Goal

Farm Nightmare Dungeons to level Glyphs, collect Masterworking materials, acquire Escalation Sigils, and run Horadric Strongrooms for efficient Obducite generation.

---

## Unlock / access

- **First Nightmare Sigil:** Complete 10 Whispers in Nightmare Difficulty → turn in at Tree of Whispers; also obtainable via Seasonal Journey completion or from World Bosses on Torment difficulty.
- **Sigil crafting:** Unlocked at the Occultist after reaching level 15 and completing a regular dungeon. Costs Gold + 60 Sigil Powder per craft.
- **Sigil Powder sources:** Salvage unwanted Sigils at the Occultist; complete Events.
- **Escalating Nightmares:** Requires an Escalation Sigil (dropped from Horadric Strongrooms, available as early as T1).

## DPS / EHP checkpoints

- Each Torment level adds extra penalties to Damage Reduction from Armor and all elemental resistances — stack Armor and resists before pushing higher tiers.
- **Escalating Nightmares:** Each consecutive dungeon in the chain increases monster Life and Damage on top of inherited affixes; budget survivability accordingly for Dungeon 3.
- **Negative affix damage additions (quick reference):**
  - Berserkers: monsters deal **+50% damage**, take **+20% damage**
  - Hunters: monsters move and attack **33% faster**
  - Lesser Aegis / Profane Aegis: some or all monsters gain **50% Max Life as Barrier** — bring Barrier-strip or burst damage
  - Chilling Wind Elites: Elites always have Chilling Wind, deal **+20% damage**
  - Strike affixes (Bleeding/Burning/Corrupting/Frostbiting/Poisoning/Sparking): **+30% Physical damage** as listed type over 3 seconds
  - Elemental Strike affixes (Cold/Fire/Lightning/Poison/Shadow Strikes): **+20% Physical damage** as listed element

## Strategy by tier

1. **Early Nightmare (low Torment):** Prioritize Sigils with the **Horadric Strongroom** positive affix to guarantee a Strongroom; follow purple wisps to locate the Horadric Gateway, or watch for its minimap icon when nearby.
2. **Horadric Strongroom runs:** Enter the Strongroom, complete Pillar Rituals (~15 sec each — stay inside circle and kill mobs) to boost Attunement generation (+2 Attunement per kill for 10 sec). Interact with all Shrines (Agility Test: +25% Attunement from kills for 10 sec per Shrine). Aim for Tier 6 before the 100-second timer expires; a random dungeon boss spawns at the end — kill it to claim the Grand Horadric Cache.
3. **Strongroom Test priorities:**
   - **Ghosts:** Guide Ghost Apparitions to Pillars → **+75 Attunement** each (highest value)
   - **Lunatics:** Kill Pillar-tethered Lunatics → **+8 Attunement** each
   - **Lure:** Lure Mummies to Pillars → **+40 Attunement** each
   - **Wandering:** Catch Wisps of Energy → **+25 Attunement** each
   - **Tethering:** Kill tethered monsters → **+4 Attunement** each (lowest, but passive)
4. **Escalating Nightmares:** Use an Escalation Sigil to start. Run three back-to-back dungeons — Dungeon 1 has 1 positive + 1 negative affix; each subsequent dungeon adds one more of each, reaching 3 positive + 3 negative in Dungeon 3. Monsters also gain increased Life and Damage each dungeon. After Dungeon 3 a pillar transports you to Astaroth.
5. **Escalating Nightmares affix selection:** Avoid stacking multiple elemental Strike affixes when your resistances are already penalized. **Obducite Mine** positive affix is currently **bugged and does not function** (Patch 2.4.1) — do not factor it into planning.
6. **Pushing higher Torment:** Ensure Armor and resistance caps are met before stepping up; Barrier-stripping tools are mandatory for Profane Aegis dungeons.

## Boss mechanics (if applicable)

**Astaroth (Escalating Nightmares final boss — Exalted version)**

Affixes are revealed only after completing the third Escalating Nightmare dungeon.

| Phase | Mechanic | Counter |
|---|---|---|
| Phase 1 | Astaroth rides the Amalgam of Rage; both act as a single combined unit | Burn down the combined form to trigger phase transition |
| Phase 2 | Astaroth fights alone while the Amalgam of Rage recovers | Focus all damage on Astaroth; avoid the recovering Amalgam |
| Phase 3 | Astaroth and the Amalgam of Rage split and attack independently and simultaneously | Kill **Astaroth** to end the encounter — killing the Amalgam instead resets back to Phase 2 |
