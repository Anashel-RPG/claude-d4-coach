# Nightmare Dungeons

> Season: 13 · Patch: 2.4.1 · Last refreshed: 2026-05-16 · Source: https://maxroll.gg/d4/resources/paragon-boards https://maxroll.gg/d4/resources/bosses-overview https://maxroll.gg/d4/resources/tempering-guide https://maxroll.gg/d4/resources/barbarian-class-overview https://maxroll.gg/d4/tierlists/push-tier-list

## Goal

Farm Glyphs (Paragon upgrade) and Obducite (Masterworking material) efficiently. Secondary benefits include Boss Lair Keys and Tempering Manuals.

## Unlock / access

- Available at Torment difficulty tiers
- **Nightmare Sigils** are required to enter a Nightmare Dungeon — one sigil consumed per run
- Sigils can be crafted or found as drops from endgame activities
- **Horadric Cube recipe:** transmute 5 Nightmare Sigils → 1 Escalation Sigil (Amalgamation recipe)
- **Escalating Nightmares** is a separate but related activity capped by Nightmare Dungeon progression; final dungeon hosts Astaroth (Season 9+ boss)

## Rewards

| Tier | Drops | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Standard Difficulty | Magic quality Tempering Manuals, Obducite, Boss Lair Keys | Lower-tier sigils only |
| Torment Difficulty | Rare & Legendary quality Tempering Manuals, Obducite, Boss Lair Keys, Glyphs | Legendary Tempering Manuals require Torment |
| Any tier (Treasure Breach / Goblin sigil) | Elevated Obducite yield | Best single-activity source of Obducite |

Additional universal drops: Unique & Mythic Unique items (Temperable as of Patch 2.4.1), Runewords/Rune components (Vessel of Hatred expansion required).

## Strategy by tier

1. **Early Torment** — Run any available Nightmare Sigils to begin Glyph leveling; collect Rare Tempering Manuals to fill build recipes.
2. **Mid Torment (Obducite focus)** — Prioritize sigils with the **Treasure Breach affix** (goblin NMD sigil); enemies, chests, events, and end-of-activity reward all drop Obducite.
3. **Obducite group farming** — Stack four goblin (Treasure Breach) Nightmare Dungeon sigils and run them in a group rotation; considered the optimal Obducite farm strategy.
4. **Glyph leveling** — Nightmare Dungeons (alongside the Pit) are the primary Glyph XP sources; prioritize completing the dungeon objective to trigger Glyph upgrade prompts.
5. **Key farming** — Boss Lair Keys for Initiate Lair Bosses drop incidentally; no special sigil affix required.
6. **Sigil conversion** — When holding excess low-tier Nightmare Sigils, use the Horadric Cube Amalgamation recipe (5 → 1 Escalation Sigil) to upgrade progression.
7. **Escalating Nightmares cap** — Progress through Escalating Nightmares to reach the final dungeon and fight Astaroth for pinnacle-tier rewards.
