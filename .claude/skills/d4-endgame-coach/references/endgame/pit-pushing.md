# Pit Pushing

> Season: 13 · Patch: 2.4.1 · Last refreshed: 2026-05-19 · Source: https://maxroll.gg/d4/resources/pit-guide

## Goal

Level Paragon Glyphs (max level 150) and push the highest Pit tier your build can clear. The Pit of Artificers is the primary — and essentially exclusive — method for upgrading glyphs.

---

## Unlock / access

| Realm | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Seasonal | Complete Capstone Dungeon **Hellish Descent** at Season Rank II |
| Eternal | Reach **level 70** |

- Access point: interact with the **obelisk in Cerrigar/Temis**
- As of Season 11 (Dec 2025), **no entry materials required**
- Introduced with patch **1.4.0 (Season 4)**

---

## Rewards
| Tier | Drops | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Any clear (boss slain before timer) | **4 glyph upgrade attempts** | Base reward for a successful clear |
| Any clear (no death) | **+1 bonus glyph upgrade attempt** | Deathless bonus |
| Any clear (via War Plans Pit Skill Tree) | **Up to +4 additional glyph upgrade attempts** | Unlocked through War Plans nodes |
| Any clear | Glyphs not yet found elsewhere | Can drop at end of run |
| Boss slain with time remaining | **Additional tier unlock(s)** | More time remaining = more tiers unlocked |

## DPS / EHP checkpoints

- Monster HP and damage increase with every tier; Pit scales to **Tier 150**
- Glyph bonus: if **Pit tier − glyph level > 20**, the upgraded glyph receives a **guaranteed extra level**
- Glyph milestones to plan around:

| Glyph Level | Effect |
|---|---|
| 25 | +1 radius increase |
| 50 | +1 radius increase |
| 51 | Legendary upgrade — unlocks a new affix |
| 150 | Maximum level |

- **Gear checklist before entering:**
  - Gear fully **Tempered** and **Masterworked**
  - Best **Gems** equipped (via Jeweler)

## Strategy by tier

1. **Early glyph leveling** — Run the lowest tier you can clear quickly. Speed > difficulty when leveling glyphs from scratch.
2. **Prioritize Elites** — Elite Monsters provide the most progression toward summoning the Pit boss; skip white trash when possible.
3. **Five floors, portal-connected** — Layout and monsters are fully randomized each run; adapt pathing on the fly.
4. **Summon the boss** — Fill the monster-progression bar within the **15-minute timer**; the boss spawns nearby once the bar is full.
5. **Don't die** — Deathless runs grant +1 glyph upgrade attempt; protect that bonus.
6. **Push for time** — Kill the boss with as much time remaining as possible to unlock more tiers per run.
7. **Higher tiers for glyph efficiency** — Once your build is stable, run the highest tier where Pit tier exceeds your glyph level by >20 to trigger the guaranteed extra-level bonus.
8. **War Plans investment** — Unlock Pit Skill Tree nodes via War Plans to reach up to +4 additional upgrade attempts per run.

## Boss mechanics (if applicable)
| Phase | Mechanic | Counter |
|---|---|---|
| Spawn | Boss spawns near player once monster-progression bar is filled | Stay mobile; boss appears close by |
| Any phase | Boss pool is randomized, drawn from the same list used in **The Tower** | Learn Tower boss patterns; same mechanics apply |
| Any phase | Different bosses have varying HP totals and unique mechanics | Adjust burst windows and survivability per boss |
| Enrage / soft enrage | 15-minute hard timer — boss must die before it expires | Prioritize boss kill over full map clear once bar is filled |
