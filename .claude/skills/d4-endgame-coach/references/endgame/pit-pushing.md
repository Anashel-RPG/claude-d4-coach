# Pit Pushing

> Season: 13 · Patch: 2.4.1 · Last refreshed: 2026-05-16 · Source: https://maxroll.gg/d4/resources/pit-guide

## Goal

Push the highest Pit tier possible to upgrade Glyphs, unlock higher tiers, and level Glyphs toward their Legendary threshold.

## Unlock / access

- **Introduced:** Update 1.4.0 (Season 4)
- **Access point:** Interact with the obelisk in Cerrigar/Temis
- **Entry cost:** None — materials no longer required as of Season 11 (December 2025)

| Realm | Unlock condition |
|---|---|
| Seasonal | Complete Capstone Dungeon "Hellish Descent" at Season Rank II |
| Eternal | Reach level 70 |

## Rewards

| Tier | Drops | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Any (cleared) | 4 Glyph upgrade attempts | Base reward for slaying the boss |
| Any (cleared) | Glyph drops (including ones not yet found) | Awarded at end of run |
| Any (cleared) | Next tier(s) unlocked | Extra tiers awarded based on time remaining after boss kill |
| Pit Skill Tree nodes | Up to 4 additional Glyph upgrade attempts | Requires spending War Plans |

## DPS / EHP checkpoints

- Monsters scale with player level and increase in HP and damage with every tier increase.
- **Tier-difference rule:** If `(Pit tier) − (Glyph level) > 20`, the Glyph is guaranteed an extra level on upgrade.
- **Glyph milestones to target:**

| Glyph level | Milestone |
|---|---|
| 25 | +1 effect radius increase |
| 50 | +1 effect radius increase |
| 51 | Legendary upgrade — unlocks a new affix |

- Run lower tiers to level Glyphs quickly from scratch; push higher tiers once Glyphs need the tier-gap bonus.

## Strategy by tier

1. **Know the structure:** 5 finite floors connected by portals; layout and monsters are randomized each run.
2. **Respect the timer:** You have **15 minutes** to slay enough monsters and summon the final boss.
3. **Prioritize Elites:** Elite Monsters contribute the most progression toward summoning the boss — always detour for them.
4. **Skip trash when ahead:** If your kill rate is comfortably ahead of the timer, skip low-density corridors to save time.
5. **Bank time for tier jumps:** Additional tiers unlock based on time remaining when the boss dies — kill the boss fast for multi-tier skips.
6. **Glyph leveling runs:** Drop down to a tier ~20+ below your Glyph's current level for guaranteed double-upgrades per clear.
7. **Pushing runs:** Stay at the highest tier you can clear with time remaining to maximize tier unlocks.

## Boss mechanics (if applicable)

| Phase | Mechanic | Counter |
|---|---|---|
| All | Boss is randomized from the same pool used in The Tower | Learn boss patterns from Tower encounters — identical behavior |
| All | Boss HP and damage scale with tier | Ensure EHP and burst DPS meet the tier's threshold before attempting |
| Kill | Time remaining on death determines bonus tier unlocks | Maximize clear speed; avoid unnecessary damage downtime before boss pull |
