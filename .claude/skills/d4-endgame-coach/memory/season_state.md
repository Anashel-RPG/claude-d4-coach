Season: 13
Patch: 2.4
Last confirmed: 2026-05-29

## Known meta shifts this season
- Two new classes added: **Paladin** and **Warlock** — playable and present in all data sources.
- Seasonal power system: **Charms** (6 Charm slots) + **Seal** (1 slot). Replaced Season 11's Incense and Season 10's Chaos Perk systems. Charms grant category skill-rank bonuses + slot-typed unique affixes; a full optimized set is a major multiplier layer. See `references/systems/seasonal-charms.md`.
- **Lord of Hatred** content: items flagged "Lord of Hatred Item" drop from LoH activities.
- Endgame loop unchanged in structure: Pit pushing (glyph upgrades, glyph cap 150), Nightmare Dungeons (Obducite / masterworking mats), Helltide (Aberrant Cinders / Living Steel), boss ladder (Tormented uber bosses).
